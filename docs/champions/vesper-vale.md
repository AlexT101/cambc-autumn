# Vesper Vale

*A ranged champion with strong basic attacks, bonus gold and a short dash.*

<img src="../images/champion-vesper-vale.webp" alt="Vesper Vale" width="320">

## Kit

**Vesper Vale**

**Passive:** Vesper Vale gains 33% more gold. A Treasure emblem adds another 20 percentage points, for a combined 53% bonus.

### Slot 0: Attack reset

Resets your basic attack cooldown.

- **Target / range:** Self
- **Walking:** Compatible
- **Cooldown:** 8 owner turn ends

### Slot 1: Dash

Move up to 2 steps, including diagonals.

- **Target / range:** Ground · 2 tiles
- **Walking:** Compatible
- **Cooldown:** 8 owner turn ends

## Starting stats

| Stat | Base | Growth per matching shard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Health | 900 | 100 |
| Strength | 50 | 5 |
| Focus | 100 | 10 |

Attack type: Ranged. Basic attack range: 3 tiles.
