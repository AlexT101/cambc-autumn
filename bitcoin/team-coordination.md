# team coordination

*from the #general-cbc channel*

one of us says "i really dont like this"
and quietly removes t
we never learned what t was
it's gone now, and so is the plan

one of us wants the radar spun clockwise
win at the top, then fight, then kda,
caps at twenty-five, never zero,
then asks again, word for word, in case the first one got lost

one of us asks for bitcoin,
gets told no, then no again,
settles for a poem in a folder
and makes the bot push it to main

and the bots we build do the same:
cassi dies, and issac goes down with her,
stellar holds sixty gems and recalls empty,
four champions wait at spawn for a teamfight that never comes

but the replays keep getting read,
the charts point a little closer to fifty,
and somehow, in the end,
everything still gets merged
