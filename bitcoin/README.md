## ode to the block we never mined

a hash went out at midnight, hex and lean,
in search of zeros nobody had seen.
it knocked on nonces, one through four billion,
and came back empty, every last vermillion.

the difficulty laughed, a mountain of bits,
our macbook fan prepared to throw its fits,
but we stood down; the owner said "go light",
so no sha-256 was harmed tonight.

instead we keep a link, a pdf of dreams,
nine pages long, with merkle trees and schemes.
no coins, no wallet, not a single sat,
just proof of work: we typed this. that is that.
