# Frozen504 source rescan and selection

Scope: all 60 `src` files from `oc-emb5-504` (commit08f4be1ce0e8ae1485f389abddc0508af971cd42), with declaration/consumer searches across root, economy, combat, model and draft. `inventory.json` records every source SHA and2,508 lexical declaration lines, including learned tables and runtime derived values. It is a reproducible index, not a claim that every numeric literal is an independent knob. The decisions below also consider important literals embedded in expressions (notably `cycle_horizon`). `PARAMETERS.md` gives exact sites, ranges and rationale for the 27 selected floats.

## Selected interaction: farming, health capital, capture allocation and shopping

The existing19-knob tune mixed combat value, safety calibration, escape spacing and economy. This run holds threat prediction and combat action valuation fixed and tunes the price of farming time, gold and healing. Recall/finish thresholds still affect combat exit decisions: this is a coupled behavioral policy search, not a pure economy or fuel-only change.

- **Shop/gold:** mutable `gold_price_scale`, `mid_gold`, `fight_weight`, `fight_camp_prior`, `shop_gain_scale`; previously missed shop-local `income_prior_gems`, `prior_rounds_per_gem`, `prior_kills_per_gem`, `prior_tribute_cut`. Prism now uses reactive emblem choice at every ordinal, so these priors and observed fight-share shrinkage are relevant to current504. Measured income still progressively outweighs the fixed prior. All denominators remain positive. The kill/tribute prior rates are modest ranges around existing values; no direct reward-table edits.
- **Capture/time:** `holder_gold_price` in capture.cc also affects farm takeover; `efficiency_power` and `pair_hold_share` allocate captures by use of gold; `rounds_per_gem` prices remaining shop benefit and camp urgency. The embedded100 in `cycle_horizon` is an actual discount timescale, not an engine constant: expose it as `cycle_horizon_scale` without changing the max-dimension/50 map scaling.
- **Farm/crew:** `home_power`, `camp_risk`, `crew_tank_line`, `crew_trip_risk`, `crew_stickiness`, `crew_rival_factor`, `mono_gather_share`, `mono_stickiness`, plus farm-local `stickiness`. These govern solo versus crew target values, grouping, commitment and health trips. Fixed crew membership, proximity, waiting and visibility rules prevent a joint change in the belief model. `crew_rival_factor<=1` remains a discount; trip risk remains positive.
- **Health policy:** mutable `recall_health_fraction`, `safe_recall_health_fraction`, `finish_camp_health_fraction`, `heal_trip_fraction`. Preserve existing map conditioning of the first and gold price; neither is a dead or completely overridden default. `condition_on_map` adds feature shifts then clamps, rather than replacing with a fixed value. Safe recall upper.14 stays below the conditioned threatened-recall lower clamp.1489. Other conditions, wakecold's refusal predicate, enemy reach, mitigation and retreat logic remain unchanged. The map-conditioned recall line may cross `hurt_health_fraction` already; no new false ordering guarantee is asserted.

## Not free policy parameters

- **Engine facts:** champions/emblems stats, attack/dash/ability ranges and cooldowns; capture/tribute/recall times; resource rewards; shard and emblem rules in champions.h/emblems.h/model/world.h/shop.cc. In particular kill_base_gold100 and hunter_kill_gold50 are not shop-price guesses. Cambridge rules remain authoritative.
- **Representation and exactness:** comms shifts/masks/max_group255; draft plan widths/checksums; turn-order rank/modulo constants and fixed `turn_*` values; bitboard dimensions; sentinel IDs/infinity values; array caps; rounded-up damage padding. Tuning these can invalidate correctness instead of expressing strategy. Derived dimensions are not extra independent parameters.
- **Compute/fuel:** main recovery/refill floors, util end/search/act/last reserves, pathing cheap guards, reveal budgets, draft work caps, open-plan iterations, combat search depth/nodes/query limits. Explicitly excluded under root's decision; training still happens at 100k so production work/value tradeoffs are measured.
- **Learned artifacts:** F92 draft_model.h coefficients, emblem_table.h decisions, map feature normalization and conditioning fit coefficients. These belong to their own fitted pipelines. 504 learned overrides sometimes bypass reactive shop decisions for other champions; that attenuates, but does not eliminate, the selected shop knobs (all Prism rows are reactive).
- **Diagnostics/build switches:** test_draft remains false; map_model_draft/comms/open_plan booleans remain unchanged; debug/fact/parity settings are not strategic floats.

No selected knob is an unused declaration: declaration and consumer checks were made for all 27. The source inventory includes derived expressions/runtime initializations; they are not blindly sent through `tunify.py`. The custom source map must require each selected exact site once and fail on drift rather than silently skip it.
