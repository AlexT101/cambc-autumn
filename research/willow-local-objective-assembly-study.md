## Narrow next design: local approach, then member completion

Prefer this over unrestricted chip damage or a new team protocol. Keep one nominee and the existing bounded fresh group descriptor; preserve ordinary work, banking, capture and escape priority. This would be a new latest-base arm, not a safety relaxation to the preserved full-group arm.

1. **One local pair, one member.** Choose a visible melee actor and visible damage actor whose actual positions are each at most one legal step from complementary work positions for the same fresh damaging member. Neither may have a kept channel or conflicting productive claim; self may switch after value comparison. No remote claimant, max-health pool or hidden readiness. Stable actor/member IDs break ties. Do not require both actors to have already posted this group.
2. **Approach is separately affordable.** At most two own turns may be spent assembling. Each chosen move must have current enemy/stun legality and a full personal camp-phase-plus-real-withdrawal reserve, assuming the other actor contributes nothing. Walking does not itself wake a sleeping camp, but reserve calculations still charge its possible attack. The actor may post the existing group claim only while actually taking this bounded approach. No group damage is authorized merely by the projected pair. Missing/changed companion, useful competing work, timeout or new danger clears the local attempt; an old claim alone never renews its timer. A small local attempt timer is required; the original siege timer must not force engagement.
3. **Work requires actual arrival.** Before any attack and after movement, rebuild current facts. Require the anchor's current HP to cover completion of the selected damaging member plus withdrawal; every contributor retains its independent bailout. Use actual current stations, strict nearest-target roles, pending-stun/acted delays and owned mitigation. Do not require these stations to reach every harmless resource in the group. Rechoose and recertify after a member dies; neither a previous certificate nor the claim authorizes the next member. Initially retain basic-only output; this remains a limited arm, not a complete scaled-Prism model.
4. **Price the whole opportunity, pay only for our share.** Compare with the existing affordable farm choice, including the two approach turns, selected-member clear, remaining group cleanup, capture and altar/tribute terms. Use the existing whole-group clearing estimate as a forecast for cleanup, not zero cleanup or a safety guarantee. Apply proportional contribution credit and a conservative incomplete-group discount (initial experimental coefficient0.5); require the resulting rate to exceed the ordinary rate. Do not award the whole512-gem map outcome to either participant. The value model is falsifiable: damage that never converts and assembly turns that displace productive work are costs.

A model activation is an already nearby tank at distance2 and ranged worker at distance4 from a5000-HP,200-damage member. Tank has3500 current HP and100 basic; ranged has1600 HP and465 basic. Each can reach its respective distance1/3 station in one move. The tank can afford its approach and independent exit even if the ranged actor refuses. If the ranged actor then arrives, actual-station completion takes about9 basic rounds; a conservative six-phase anchor exit costs `(9+6)*200=3000`, fitting current HP before any intervening charged hit, and can be recertified afterward. A harmless member outside those fixed attack stations no longer makes this damaging-member certificate impossible, but its travel/clear/capture cost still reduces value. If actual HP no longer fits, do not attack. This is an activation example, not the historical Fish state or a full-health-boss guarantee.

## Willow's role and stopping condition

Willow is optional service, never mandatory tank credit. Against range5 camp fire, a camp-free distance6 station cannot heal a melee worker at distance1 with range4 Heal. Therefore assembling Willow there does not sustain that worker. Existing immediate all-ally healing and the isolated forecast-formation arm can serve real recovery opportunities after withdrawal; no future heal enters current safety or clear-time admission. No Focus buying or replacement of HoR follows.

The first useful outcome is actual tank/DPS convergence followed by damaging-member completion and eventual capture, at a measured cost in foregone ordinary work. If local potential pairs never appear, or the member-completion reserve still fails, stop this assembly experiment rather than add remote recruitment or credit indefinite chips. Camps not regenerating preserves damage, but does not preserve ownership: abandoned chip damage may subsidize the opponent. Current evidence does not justify that broader policy. No additional export is needed to specify this local experiment; existing latest-base Willow forecast and staging qualification remain the cheaper main-line work.
