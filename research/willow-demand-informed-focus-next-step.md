# Next Focus experiment: separate observed demand from invented utility

2026-10-23. Source-only review against frozen integration `4db4eecbda37e23dbce0b162d2cf7441a3d32a554d6534cf196e3ffbedcdf2fd`, principally `self_abilities.cpp:419–460`, `plan.cpp:960–995,1982–1987`, and `bot.cpp:1229–1385`. No source changes or runtime.

Prefer an explicit, limited role preference to a new scalar healing-utility model. The clean baseline remains the isolated one-BruiserFocus arm: after begun partials and Treasure, with one protective family completed, exchange only a fresh model-selected Bruiser Strength for one Bruiser Focus; an owned completed Bruiser Focus prevents repetition. Rebase that exact policy onto 4db for any new comparison. Retain actual chosen stats/cost/own-upkeep saving in the quote and affordable-prefix calculations. Do not add healing to raw_output, fabricate Strength savings for Focus, or use expected healing in safety admission. This tests a stated service-versus-farming tradeoff, not optimal item valuation. A new demand gate only narrows its already sparse activation; it cannot make a chooser that selects Power instead of Bruiser test Focus. Inspect qualifying purchases/partials before allocating a broad panel to an inert arm.

A demand-informed version is conceptually more selective, but is not yet a better identified experiment than that fixed baseline. Successful current healing does not measure the benefit of extra Focus. For a fresh completed Bruiser comparison, let H be the current heal cap and D the pre-cast deficit. Bruiser Strength adds 5 Focus and Bruiser Focus adds 25, so the frozen-cast marginal ceiling is `min(D,H+75) - min(D,H+15)`, not simply 60 HP per full current heal. A deficit above today's cap but below H+15 provides no extra benefit between those completed emblems. For a partial purchase, use the two actual partial builds, including completion bonuses only when completed. The full exchange loses 12 Strength and keeps the same 50 max HP; cast frequency and actual basic opportunities remain decisive.

Eligibility must also be evaluated for each proposed cap. The existing 60%-of-cap threshold rises with Focus; a cast accepted at the old cap can be rejected at the new one unless the low-health exception applies. Thus capped demand alone is insufficient. A useful bounded sample records selected recipient deficit, max HP/fraction, offered cap and successful cast, and computes both alternatives' acceptance and clipped gains. It must include zero/negative schedule effects rather than count only favorable saturated casts. These are fixed-snapshot counterfactuals, not realized extra healing, saved lives or guaranteed recipient work.

The cheapest honest collection boundary is already present in ordinary `heal_ally`: retain the selected record's unclipped deficit and max HP alongside best_gain before the next native import, and update a small successful-cast record only if `cast_slot_on` returns true. Failed casts contribute nothing; no extra ally scan is necessary. But call this a selected live-record sample, with freshness limitations across any fuel yield. The combat planner separately reads deficit during construction, then executes `Op::heal` through the stored mate without a fresh health query. Counting its original deficit as exact pre-cast demand would overstate freshness after earlier planned actions or yields. Either mark those samples unknown and leave them out of a conservative demand gate, or explicitly design a later freshness read; do not silently treat planned demand as actual. The step/formation helpers that finish through heal_ally naturally use its selection seam.

Do not yet convert those HP ceilings into quote savings. One can illustrate recipient recovery time as extra absorbed HP divided by usable HP, multiplied by a recall/return trip, but that assumes the recipient otherwise recalls, that this heal changes the recall boundary, and that the observed route/work continues. Our existing own-upkeep history does not establish those facts for another actor. The same cast already sacrifices a possible reset, while a larger heal at an unchanged cast has no additional cast cost; a new heal enabled or removed by the threshold has a different opportunity cost. Walking also competes with Willow's basic. HP and basic damage cannot be compared as interchangeable units, and healing HoR can reduce his missing-health damage. A coefficient-free scalar ranking is therefore not available from current counters.

If a demand-aware preference is subsequently authorized, keep it a separate finite experiment using copied successful-cast samples and an explicit bounded observation policy. Do not introduce an arbitrary conversion from HP to gold/DPS or promise that one saturated cast proves a persistent role. Its discrete eligibility must be included in the prices cache key; otherwise fresh purchasing and retained farm/shop quotes can disagree. Partial items must remain committed even after demand expires. Both best_emblem and affordable_prefix must use the same selected item and actual partial/completed build. A general service-utility term would require shared full/prefix evaluation and cache semantics, which is larger than this next experiment.

Recommendation: retain the fixed one-BruiserFocus policy as the first clean Focus comparator, with activation checked before broad testing; use marginal-demand observations to explain that comparison rather than immediately bolt on a new scalar model. Continue the separate Health arm as a provider-survival comparison. The corrected Marcella Treasure result remains 2.4 gold per tributed gem with Treasure versus 2 without: nothing here supports generic removal of Treasure from non-Vesper roles.
