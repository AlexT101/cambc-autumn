# Willow: replace an unproductive formation step with forecast service access

2026-10-23. Review/design only; no source or runtime changes. Recommended next isolated Willow experiment, retaining the current draft. This is a narrow amendment to existing formation, not a new partner protocol or a relaxation of safety.

## Why this arm

The fixed P/V/M/W replacement of HoR lost136–194/330, including58–106 on the second generated suite. All16 exact-source published metadata files in `replays/willow-four-pick-157f5-vs-a00fff/batch-run-01-release-published4p/analysis/` were inspected. They show several mechanisms, not a single absence of healing: Rows has zero PvP/deaths and late conversion; Interlock has candidate357/352 collected gems versus1002 both sides; Shortcut has9/10 candidate deaths versus3/4. Even Swirl's losing side receives12570 healing, overlapping winning-game healing totals. Healing volume cannot select the draft or establish saved productive turns.

HoR supplies a second melee worker, walk-plus-basic, current self-shield and dash/stun area damage. Replacing it with a ranged healer taxes movement and independent capture throughput. Five-pick Willow preserves that complementary role. Swapping Vesper instead sacrifices +33% gold and mobility; swapping Marcella loses melee/stun immunity/tribute gold; swapping Prism loses bag scaling and the current pairing. None is justified by these16 games. Static service-area overlap, independent work lanes and altar distance are reasonable future features, but no selector or second blind replacement is recommended now.

The source gap is narrower than “Willow only heals Prism.” In `bots/variants/willow-audit107-ready-heal-work/src/bot.cpp`, `local_support`4817 enumerates all allies' worthwhile current heals4880–4892. What remains Prism-specific is the **zero-gain formation direction**, work claim and request protocol4916–4983. HooksB's own r32 view proves useful current Marcella healing already works:480/1200HP at distance3, heal360 plus a basic, reset still on cooldown3. The independent ready-heal helper covers immediate access when Prism is ineligible. Neither needs replacement.

A subtle activation constraint: Willow's basic range is3; Wolf/Elk/Golem/Monolith reaches are3/4/4/5 (`cambc-rules.yaml`). Thus a strictly camp-free support station cannot also basic a live damaging member. Requiring that as the economic certificate would make a proposed frontline work-station policy nearly inert. Harmless members or dead-striker cleanup are exceptions, not a general frontline plan.

## Exact change boundary

Let existing `local_support` finish selecting its original best station. Run this optional reranker **only** for Willow when that original result is a move (`best != here`), `best_gain == 0`, heal is ready now, `leave == false`, protected work is false, formation has not expired, and the existing active-clear veto is false. The ordinary loop must have found a valid strict-safe move. If any current worthwhile heal exists, do not run it. Also veto if Willow has useful current stationary output (a legal enemy basic/cast, or an eligible camp basic/member finish); use existing read-only target enumeration, not speculative DPS or an action performed just to test availability.

Preserve capture, urgent bank, shop, forced escape, immediate healing, current productive output and the original movement allowance. No reranking a stay into a move, no added turn of waiting, no cooldown-only follow, no broad change to `support_endpoint`, and no alternative recipient written into the Prism mailbox. This boundary prices the action against a walk the parent already chose; both alternatives lose the same ranged basic that turn. A cast-ready heal next turn is an opportunity, not guaranteed income.

## One-phase forecast, with bounded present information

Use the existing visible-allies list, at most its8 slots. A recipient must have a successful current visible record, be alive, already have acted this round, be within5 tiles, and have a valid current communicated group claim consistent with its actual attack/ability working range. Reject kept recall, tribute, teleport, spawn recovery and explicit conflicting work. Already-acted is intentional for this first arm: it avoids assuming the recipient will choose to stay later in the current round. It does **not** promise it will stay for Willow's next turn.

Only examine that claimed group's at most16 members. Larger groups are outside this experiment. Require group alive/current observation, at least one positively observed live member with `health_round == world.round`, current visibility, awake belief (`!maybe_asleep(i)`), and observed group damage this round. For each qualifying damaging member in range of the recipient, count it only if the recipient is strictly nearer than every other currently visible live champion in that member's range; ties contribute zero. This is a conditional forecast from the current scene, not a certification of the next target. Unseen champions, intervening kills/stuns and ally movement on the next round can invalidate it. Unknown or nonpositive member health contributes zero and never proves death.

Let raw eligible damage be D. For value, use **0.63×D**: assume both Jungle and Durability even when ownership is unavailable, avoiding a fresh item-map query or optimistic raw-health loss. Subtract a conservative active shield amount when an existing current record can establish it; if shield status/amount is uncertain, reject that recipient for the forecast rather than querying or assuming zero. Do not use forecasts for a recipient with current HP≤D. Do not forecast opponent damage, unobserved missing health, future damage beyond the next camp phase, or any future shield/heal.

Projected missing health = current missing health + the resulting nonnegative forecast damage. Projected gain is capped by Willow's current heal amount. Require it to pass the existing `worth_healing` rule at projected recipient health. This can value upcoming injury even when today's small deficit is not yet worth a cast. It is not a change to the actual heal rule: after movement and on later turns, `heal_ally` still uses real current native health and readiness.

No new eager world scan, persistent injury ledger, global route search or fuel gate. Consume/copy per-member scalars immediately if using a scratch-backed index. At most8×16×the existing visible-champion count of cheap distance comparisons. Select one recipient finalist before building its range/LOS viewer mask; if its exact mask fails, retain the parent instead of an open-ended retry search.

## Station ranking and expiry

Reuse the original nine legal stations, current strict `SupportExposure.safe()` and ordinary cleared-camp-entry exclusions. Safety continues to assume Willow can take the full current risk herself; forecast injury/healing never enters an HP reserve, escape, enemy or camp gate.

The alternate station must bring the finalist into real range4/LOS when neither the current tile nor the parent move provides that access. Compare projected useful gain against the parent's best potential access to **all** qualified forecast recipients; a cheap conservative parent upper bound may count every recipient within Chebyshev4 even without an LOS mask. Only a strictly larger alternate value changes the parent. Equal value keeps the parent. Among equally valuable alternate stations, use existing home-distance order then fixed direction order; recipients use projected gain, projected health fraction, then stable actor identity. This avoids replacing a good forecast to Prism with an equal one to Marcella merely by list order.

Reuse the original move/observe/safety recheck and native heal execution. Neither success of the forecast nor changed anchor counts as productivity. It must not reset `formation_start/turns`, write a new claim, or extend the four-turn formation budget. Actual delivered healing, actual output and the original route-progress rules retain their existing effects; do not invent alternate route progress toward this recipient. After a failed move, original refreshed fallback behavior applies and no copied record survives observation.

The arm spends the same one walk, keeps no extra waiting budget, and enables a real next-turn heal if the scene persists. No part of admission assumes that heal will occur or that Marcella will protect Willow.

Required checks: preserve parent when current heal/output exists; never move when parent stayed; unknown health/shield, sleeping member, tied target, unacted recipient, expired formation and invalid claim reject; current enemy/camp safety still vetoes; fresh failure/re-observe does not use old pointers; claim change or recipient departure stops association. Positive two-round check uses an already-acted recipient on the first round, then tests both next-round actor orders: delivered heal if still accessible, normal fallback if it moves, no waiting demand. A camp killed/stunned before its phase must yield no invented heal or expiry reset. The example is a logically activating test, not a claim about an observed historical missed action.

Measure changed zero-gain moves, their one/two-round **actual delivered** useful heals, recipient subsequent productive turns/capture completion, unused moves, lost stationary actions, formation expiry and fuel. A heal-count increase without better work/conversion is insufficient. If ordinary formation reaches the same service or the arm only chases recipients who depart, reject it. Evaluate with the retained draft first, including five-pick Willow alongside HoR; do not bundle this with draft, item, Monolith or recall changes.

It cannot credit a healer's promised continuation. This is a separate arm, not needed to test the formation hypothesis.
