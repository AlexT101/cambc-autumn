## Goal: honest beliefs about unseen and contested groups in farm choice (variant `bots/variants/h-stale`, `./copybot variants/chy-ref variants/h-stale`)

Evidence (a 1,024-game trace study of chy-ref's farm switches, `research/mac13-hecate/logs/switch-study.txt`; trace copy `bots/variants/h-trace` shows where farm's values come from): 60% of turns are farm turns and 28% of those are walks towards a group the champion then never works. The single largest fixable source:
- **Stale enemy-side beliefs.** `World::update_camp` keeps an out-of-view group "alive" forever unless a respawn time is known, and nothing infers enemy captures. Walks that start towards a group believed alive, on the enemy's side, not seen for 100+ rounds are abandoned 79.5% of the time (13% for own-side groups seen within 100 rounds); they are 16.6% of all abandoned farm turns, and 72.7% of abandoned long trips are 20+ tiles. In 62% of those streaks the group was respawning or cleared on arrival, 92% of the respawning ones last captured by the enemy; 64% had never been seen by us.
- **The max_wait cliff** (economy/farm.cc ~1973 `if (idle > params::max_wait) continue;`): a respawning group is hidden while its wait beyond our arrival exceeds max_wait (2), then appears at a large value when it comes within; "new group appeared because its respawn came within max_wait" is 8.2% of abandoned turns (switches away from a walk already under way).
- **A visible enemy nearer the group**: walks started with an enemy in sight nearer the group than us are abandoned 61% of the time (24.5% with no enemy in sight).

Design: one coherent change to what farm believes, three parts, each behind its own compile-time switch in params.h (`namespace stale { inline constexpr bool unseen_cycle = true, wait_priced = true, enemy_race = true; }`) so they can be ablated. Every term must come from the rules, the map or a measured quantity the bot already has; no new hand-picked constants.
1. **unseen_cycle.** For a group believed alive and unseen, define `t_e`: the soonest the enemy could have taken it again since our last reading = the enemy's walk to it (the mirror of our spawn's distance, or whatever enemy-spawn distance field pathing has) + one clear by an enemy champion (the bot's own clear-time estimate for the group, as farm uses for us) + capture time. While rounds since our last reading exceed `t_e` and the enemy reaches the group sooner than we do (the side test farm/camps already use, e.g. home_ground or enemy-side distance), treat it as farmed by the enemy on a cycle of T = t_e + respawn (its longest member respawn): on our arrival it is respawning with probability respawn / T, with respawn / 2 rounds left on average, so farm adds the expected wait respawn^2 / (2T) to that group's time (and the gems/gold its capture would bring are unchanged; a fresh sighting replaces the prior as today). Groups we have never seen count as last read at round 0. Apply it where farm prices a group's time (the trip / wait term), not as a filter.
2. **wait_priced.** Replace the hard `idle > max_wait` skip by pricing the wait inside the rate (the time term already has trip + clear + capture + trips home; a wait beyond arrival adds its rounds), so a respawning group's value rises smoothly as its respawn nears instead of jumping in. Check how the existing code accounts the wait (max(trip, respawn left)?) and keep it consistent; keep any other purpose max_wait serves (e.g. not standing idle on a far group: the rate already penalises idle rounds, which is the point).
3. **enemy_race.** For a group with a visible enemy (seen this round) nearer to it than we are, and the group on that enemy's side or the enemy already working it (hitting its members, standing on/adjacent to its tiles), price that we arrive second: if the enemy's arrival + clear + capture ends before we can get there, the group will be respawning when we arrive (its respawn timer added to our wait, as part 1 does), unless our stand there would win the fight against it (use the existing fight/stand verdicts; don't invent new risk numbers). Keep the fuel bounded (only groups farm already evaluates fully, only enemies in sight).
Keep everything else unchanged. Fuel: these are a few arithmetic terms per evaluated group; keep host calls out of inner loops.
