## Goal: retreat to friends, not only home (variant `bots/variants/h-refuge`, `./copybot variants/chy-ref variants/h-refuge`)
Evidence: in mirror games most deaths come from retreat chase-downs: the pursuer keeps pace from behind while home is 12-40 steps away (research/macro-atlas/NOTES.md "Deaths outside a visible Prism's single cast": 552 deaths in 400 games, triggers stand-lost, pack zone, recall line). The win rate of fights rises steeply with local numbers (+1: 61%, +2: 65%, +3: 77%). research/EXPERIMENTS.md lists "keep the carrier with allies and flee towards them" as untried. The reference retreats only towards its spawn (combat/fight.cc `retreat` ~1579, `retreat_step` ~224, and model/escape.{h,cc}'s plan whose goal is the home field `Pathing::to_ally_spawn`). The pursuer (the reference) prices its chase with the allies it SEES; our allies just out of its sight are not in its fight, so a retreat into them turns its won chase into a lost fight it did not anticipate.

Design:
1. **Refuge.** When retreating (the `retreat` branch, and the escape plan's goal), consider as goals, besides the home field, the tiles within 1-2 of a REFUGE: an alive ally (visible, or believed from comms: its claim's group centre / posted position, only when recent and plausible) such that the fight at that tile, us + that ally (+ other allies there) against the pursuers we see, is not lost (use the existing fight simulation, e.g. Safety::fight / stand_trade with the ally counted present; keep the fuel bounded: test at most the 1-2 nearest candidate allies). Prefer the refuge when it is reachable in clearly fewer of our turns than the next home-field tile that ends the danger (e.g. the refuge is <= half the steps home), and when the pursuit model (escape plan) says the way there is not worse than the way home. A linked Cassi/Issac body counts its partner as a refuge.
2. **Reception.** An ally that sees a teammate retreating with a visible enemy pursuing it within the enemy's reach (the teammate hurt or the enemy approaching) treats that pursuer as prey even if it would not otherwise (preyable/prey in fight.cc), when the joint fight (it + the teammate + others in sight) is not lost; this is a defensive join on our ground, not a long hunt: only allies within ~6 steps of the pursuer.
3. Keep everything else: no new triggers for retreat, no farm changes.
