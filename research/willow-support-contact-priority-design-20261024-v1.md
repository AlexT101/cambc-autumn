# Willow recall contact priority: bounded experimental design

2026-10-24. Source-only handoff; no implementation, build, replay decoding or new runtime. Public Elkring source identities remain unknown. This is an isolated scheduling experiment justified by a verified interaction, not a proved regression fix or a reason to roll back f7. The source prototype is ready to implement with the camp safeguard below; inaccessible public private-memory proof is not an implementation gate.

## Evidence and the missing concept

See `astraeus-normal-five-pick-matched-review-20261024-v1.md` and its pinned paired Willow9 r158–167 exports. Identical r161 observations lead to the same move, but new Willow spends reset for55 damage and ends recalling at9,25. Old priority14 becomes new−41 under the current formula, because remaining outgoing damage drops110→55 against the recorded763HP enemy. That correctly removes unavailable outgoing damage; the mistake to test is whether **no substantial outgoing damage should imply idle scheduling while an exposed support is recalling**.

Ryth has already acted and stands9,22,45Strength, AoE cooldown6 and ready reset. One legal activation reaches2 with walk+melee/reset; two activations can approach one step and then walk+melee/reset at distance3. His next activation alone cannot hit Willow's endpoint. Do not call this immediate danger, reserve the old110 damage, or transplant the previously rejected ranged-Vesper melee formula. `safety.cpp:708–759` supplies readiness-aware `strike`; `837–860` explicitly widens a two-activation threat by an initial walk. Its geometric estimates ignore placement/visibility and are upper bounds (`safety.h:43–69`).

The existing lethal escape offer (`turn_order.cpp:229–266`) is not inconsistent: it answers a different question and never promises to preserve a nonlethal pursuit gap. `can_act_before` (`144–155`) only tests whether some allied ordering could precede an enemy. It does not show that a particular priority achieves that ordering. `World::rank_turns` (`world.cpp:1358–1393`) and `World::slot` (`world.h:433–442`) order within teams, then alternate teams. Old Willow gets first slot on even rounds162/164/166; new Willow is last. Ryth starts odd rounds163/165/167 regardless of Willow's priority once he is the enemy's first champion.

## Proposed first arm

Add a **contact floor13** only to Willow's fallback priority, only while she ends the turn alive, off allied spawn and recalling, and only when the conditions below hold. Preserve the actual fallback calculation and all existing kill/protect offers. Floor13 is the existing boundary above every living idle role (maximum12) and below actual combat danger14–23; dead champions also use13 and retain normal id ties. It is not a new damage bucket or a high protective band. No priority is remembered from earlier turns.

1. Reuse `live_self`, native `state` and filtered `live_enemies` already obtained in the non-teacher branch of `set_turn_order` (`turn_order.cpp:293–325`). Run the contact check only if no existing wish wins and the computed fallback is below13. Do not apply it to tribute/teleport or general farming. A current recall is observable intent to complete a cancellable return, **not proof that `want_home` selected the retreat branch**: opportunistic recalls can also qualify. Keep that admission explicit in the experiment.
2. Consider at most the existing visible live enemy vector. For the first arm require the enemy already acted this round, has current-round position/readiness/priority information and is not channeling. This avoids speculating about its unspent current activation or a teleport. Do not widen to tracked unseen enemies. Use current end-self position and the recorded enemy position; after our attack, do not resurrect a filtered dead enemy. Also require a matching `priority_start_handles` entry with `priority_start_enemies.units[i].stunned == false`, using the already-read turn-start snapshot (`world.cpp:1209–1223`), not a fresh host call or the ally-only `ChampionBelief::stun_pending`. Missing snapshot rejects. `champion_info.state` has no named stun flag, and an acted enemy's spent action flags cannot answer next-turn readiness. The saved `has_stun` can conservatively reject a duration0 buff that expires next turn; preserving that narrow coverage loss is preferable to falsely assuming a known stunned enemy can perform the approach. Future allied actions can still change its opportunities after this snapshot.
3. Compute a bounded contact nomination from actual enemy readiness: `strike(e, round+1).hits(d)` **or** `strike(e, round+2).hits(max(0,d-1))`, where `d` is Chebyshev distance from our endpoint. The second term means an approach walk on its next activation followed by a later legal strike; it is a two-activation geometric upper bound, not damage before our next turn. Reuse the existing strike memo. No Reach placement, path search, fresh visibility query, damage summation, or pretend refresh of Willow's spent reset. Walls, enemy incentives and future stun can make the nomination false; this is why it earns only the low contact floor and cannot force movement, holding or combat.
4. Require a **projected ordering benefit**. From the existing allied next priorities, count allies ahead of Willow using the unchanged fallback and again using13, with handle/id tie-breaking. Convert both ranks to next-round global slots using the existing alternating-team formula, including unequal team sizes. Keep enemy projected next rank from the current known priority snapshot. Nominate only when the proposed slot is before this enemy and the fallback slot is not. Existing `can_act_before` may be a cheap rejection but is insufficient by itself. Do not mutate `World` priorities/ranks to do this calculation, and do not raise above13 to beat an engaged ally.
5. Before accepting a nomination, require the **priority-independent camp safeguard** below. Evaluate it only after finding contact and a projected rank benefit. A zero from the existing camp damage estimate alone is insufficient.
6. Apply `max(fallback,13)` once if any enemy qualifies and the camp safeguard passes. Keep existing protect/kill priorities, native redundant-write check and current end-reserve behavior unchanged. Add no fuel gate. No callback into `want_home`, `stand` or the economic evaluator.

This is a predicted rank improvement using already-known priorities, not a guarantee: allies/enemies yet to act may still change their next priority, and a dead ally at13 can remain ahead by id. An A-first enemy at slot0 cannot qualify against any B slot. These limits are part of the policy rather than reasons to invent globally coordinated priorities.

## Camp tie safeguard and exact witness admission

Earlier priority can transfer a camp's tied nearest-target attack onto Willow **before champion turns begin**. `Safety::find_members` (`safety.cpp:1651–1730`) shrinks each member's reach using the currently predicted next-round priority tie winner. `camp_damage(p,true,true)` (`2069–2079`) sums those already-shrunken reaches. Its cached zero must not be reused as proof of zero after changing the order; even a later recomputation can retain the old ranked geometry.

Use the existing cached local index `map.groups_near(endpoint,map.max_camp_reach)` (`world.h:140–144`, `world.cpp:347–395`). Do not scan the whole map or materialize another threat board. For each returned group with positive static Strength, reject unless the existing CampBelief proves it remains empty through next round's camp phase:

- `state == respawning && respawn_round > round+1`; or
- `state == cleared && state_round > 0 && state_round + group.respawn > round+1`.

The second bound is deliberately conservative: even if capture happened immediately after the last known-cleared observation, respawn cannot precede that observation plus the group's respawn interval. Do not accept an arbitrarily old cleared state, an asleep/live state, old zero member HP alone, or a currently stunned member. Do not assume an ally remains a closer target. This group-level bound may reject when only a harmless member is nearby or the harmful member's actual range is smaller than the map maximum; that false-positive cost is acceptable for the first arm and avoids scanning all members of a large group. `note_capture` can supply a respawn belief through existing comms (`world.cpp:1284–1300`); `update_camp` advances it (`1613–1651`). No new dynamic reads are needed.

**Elkring admission remains unknown, for a specific reason.** Endpoint9,25 is distance2 from Elk13 at10,27 (range4), group7. The exported local position19,2 is in B's own frame; it must not be mistaken for raw coordinates. The r161 actor export labels this Elk `static_map_only` with no dynamic health. Observer events show the group cleared145 and paid152, with earliest respawn212. Thus a static raw-range-only veto rejects the witness; an existing Willow belief containing the recent clear or respawn212 would pass the specified exemption. The public export does not expose that belief. This uncertainty does not prevent implementing/testing the source arm: verify its admission and refusal in controlled source-known states rather than weakening the camp safeguard to force this public example.

## Why this scope, cost and interpretation

Willow already has a no-fight support policy in `bot.cpp:2857–2861`; normal damage bins make a ready reset accidentally substitute for escape urgency. A separate low contact floor preserves the spent-reset accounting and distinguishes nearby pursuit from harmless idle work. At the r161 recorded geometry, the second contact term nominates Ryth, and moving Willow above allied12/9/−29/−32 would make B's next first slot available without displacing any engaged ally. That is a source-level expected result, **not a source-known runtime witness or a claim the whole loss is fixed**.

The enemy loop is bounded by six. Two readiness-specific strike memo lookups per eligible enemy and two rank counts over at most six allies suffice; rank counts can be computed once before the enemy loop. Only then query the existing nearby-group cache once. That cache enumerates local camp tiles on a cold miss (maximum camp radius5 under current rules), may grow its existing storage, and uses scratch-bitboard host operations through `positions_within` (`util.h:367–381`); a warm hit reuses the index. It is not zero-cost or guaranteed allocation-free. No new dynamic-state host reads, full-map scan, member scan, persistent cache or board implementation is needed. Reuse the existing live-enemy vector; keep the existing fallback `priority_self` and `live_start_enemies` reads in their current order. Cache only ordinary scalar results needed after fallback. There is no compile/fuel claim until a separate authorized build/measurement.

## Falsifiable validation and rejection criteria

The first source-known check should report already-computed scalars: recalling flag, fallback/final priority, qualifying enemy id, distance, immediate/contact boolean, old/new projected slots, camp guard result/first rejecting group and its state/timing fields, and actual next-round slots. Keep these debug-only and account for trace perturbation; release actions decide the result. No new replay intake or runner is requested here. Controlled checks must include live tied camps (reject), known respawn after next phase (admit if all other conditions hold), respawn due next phase (reject), stale cleared history beyond the conservative bound (reject), and the other team's first-slot threat (no impossible ordering benefit).

Success at mechanism level is more **actual** turns ahead of the nominated pursuer while preserving legal actual cooldowns, without displacing an engaged ally. Assess recall completion, nonlethal damage/stuns before Willow's next turn, escape distance, delivered heals and productive farm/capture access; count idle allies delayed. Do not use heal volume alone or one match outcome as strength evidence. Separate immediate-strike from approach-only nominations: excessive geometric false positives or frequent unnecessary recall cancellations in the latter are a direct reason to narrow/remove that term. If actual ordering never changes, readiness mismatches appear, a selected enemy cannot legally approach, or displaced allies lose more service than Willow gains, reject or refine the arm. A source-known r161-like witness is useful but is not a prerequisite to implementing this isolated experiment.

No change is proposed to movement guards, reset attack selection, capture continuation, hidden-enemy safety, or the Pilgrimage group12 policy. Its unseen-bush death remains a separate information problem.
