Unlike the ranged branch, it does not immediately call `cast_at_camp` there. Evaluation is bounded by the existing fuel/reserve loop (`2159–2174`). If no choice is evaluated, its later fallback walks toward the remembered choice and returns (`2385–2395`); that route does not itself cast. A selected choice can still fail attack-tile preparation and take the walk fallback (`2430–2435`). The ordinary in-range selected-target branch does cast (`2443–2447`).
