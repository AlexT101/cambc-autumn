These are source cost paths, not a measured breakdown. In contrast, `heal_ally:458–469` returns for a champion without Heal before constructing a service scan, so no-Willow controls cannot be paying the recipient field-query cost.

A focused implementation can give the chooser/prefix utility a compile-time disabled specialization, selected once at the quote boundary for non-Willow or pre-Treasure. Its disabled path should retain the parent's exact upkeep arithmetic, candidate order, begun precedence, remaining-cost arithmetic and first-Treasure stat; omit service cache storage and per-candidate service calls. Keep one shared source for the original candidate enumeration rather than two independently maintained policies. Preserve matching full/prefix utilities and signed service in the enabled specialization. Reuse a caller's existing Willow test to avoid repeated empty lifecycle calls where possible. The Prices revision can be handled separately for the Willow service case while preserving its original cache key and invalidation behavior; do not silently stop invalidating a published service revision. Compiler inlining/code size may offset a source-level saving, so no fuel reduction is promised.

Do **not** equate “published profile empty” with the parent's policy: after Treasure, Willow still has the new fresh Bruiser Focus candidate even when service evaluates to zero. The disabled specialization is only for the explicitly unchanged non-Willow/pre-Treasure scope unless the existing enabled candidate set is retained.

## Lifecycle and model limits to assess in the extracted evidence

- **Coverage is selective.** Only successful ordinary casts with fully known recipient data enter the usable estimate. Planner heals are counted but not sampled. A full-health or otherwise never-selectable recipient can currently make the whole scan unknown if its required data are missing. Removing that exclusion would be a coverage/model change, not part of the exact-preserving cost arm. Check actual covered/missing/planner proportions before changing it.
- **Sparse epochs can erase the signal.** Stat/emblem purchases and observed stat changes reset pending history; gaps or unconfirmed turns also reset it. This is conservative against stale attribution, but a support champion with frequent purchases or rollover gaps may reach a shop with few usable samples. Conversely, published observations intentionally remain fixed across a contiguous dead→spawn/shop visit, including multiple buys, while the proposed Build changes. Do not “fix” that required freezing during a cost refactor.
- **Published data age independently of pending data.** After leaving the shop, later heals do not update published service until the next visit. Pending gap/stat invalidation does not immediately erase an older published profile while off spawn. The original design explicitly publishes by visit; it is a delayed economic estimate, not live support demand. Compare publication age/epoch to the actual quote before attributing a changed farm price to current service.
- **Exposure and selection have deliberate bias.** Missing ordinary casts occupy ring slots and their alive time remains in exposure. Planner casts remain only in the exposure/counters, with no service numerator. Before eviction, a long low-service epoch dilutes all samples; after eviction, exposure starts after the evicted successful ordinary cast. Death time does not count as alive exposure. None of these rules establishes the future service rate on a changed route or formation.
- **The conversion is first-order.** Historical deficits are replayed on a fixed cast schedule, including the signed60% threshold and changed target. Earlier larger heals could reduce later deficits; a suppressed heal could free a reset; allies may not actually avoid recall; their recovery rounds may have different strategic value. Those are model assumptions, not collector correctness bugs. Own Strength/Health/farming upkeep remains essential and must not be removed to make Focus activate.

The most focused implementation recommendation is the disabled-utility specialization plus redundant-read suppression, kept separate from initialized-prefix storage if review complexity grows.
