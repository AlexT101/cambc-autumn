There is a concrete pre-purchase feedback path in the Health source:

- `bot.cpp:1292–1298` returns the actual Jungle Health valuation inside the first-protection override. `prices()` at 1335 caches that chosen emblem's saving, then converts it to per-gold value. This is a truthful Health quote, not a Strength proxy.
- Farm at 1954 multiplies this value by actual gold-payment bonuses; at 2204 it weights each group's gold by that result. A changed future item can therefore change economic job selection at zero current gold. Neither the direction nor the selected group's decisive term was logged here.
- Local support at 4945–5008 uses Prism's communicated claim, Willow's current work identity, active-clear state, readiness and formation budget. Item value is not directly in the station comparator. A changed economic job or route history can nevertheless change later access to allies and eligibility for a formation move.
- Direct shopping is a different explanation and is excluded at the nominated zero-gold state: `wants_shop()` at 3360–3364 returns false below one shard's price before evaluating the shop trip. Manual recall cancellation/restart by itself is not evidence of shopping.

The most useful next question is whether r102 actually selected different economic work because of the changed gold value, or whether the same work produced a different route/formation action. That is narrower than attributing the later healing reduction to Health stats.
