## What the model actually does

`bot.cpp:113 main_stat` counts only damage casts/landings and basics. Willow has no qualifying damaging spell, so its fallback cast multiplier is always0 and main stat is always Strength. `count_use`5250 counts her actual heal/reset casts, but the heal-slot count is never used by this selector. `raw_output`833 correctly represents own damage/basic-reset output, not ally healing. `upkeep_cost`1195 prices own clear speed, received damage and recall/return travel; it has no ally-service benefit. Merely adding heal casts to `main_stat` would leave Focus with no direct saving in the actual chooser, quotes or shopping model, and can change which candidates are considered without pricing the intended benefit.

`best_emblem`1230 respects begun partials, Treasure-first101, latest Bruiser completion103, Durability Health and optional Willow Jungle Health. Bruiser Strength/Health incidentally add5 Focus per completed emblem; thus current Willow Focus need not stay120 forever. Bruiser Focus is not considered for Willow and, under the current own-output/injury objective, is dominated by Bruiser Strength: both add50 Health, while Strength gives+15S/+5F versus Focus+3S/+25F. The extra20F has zero modeled service saving. `prices`1330, `affordable_prefix`1356, shopping and bank/farm gold valuation all consume this same chooser. A future item policy must return truthful selected-stat/completion/cost/saving throughout these paths; changing the purchase stat after quoting a Strength upgrade would be inconsistent.

## Marginal quantities and their limits

One30-gold Strength shard adds3 damage per actual basic, including an actual reset-produced basic. The ideal stationary damage model adds `3*(1+1/8)=3.375` DPS, or27 per8 rounds (29.7 with Monster). Walking, missed targets and actual action schedules reduce that. One Focus shard adds5F and therefore **at most15 restored HP per actual heal**, ideal1.875HP/round if a useful heal occurs every8 rounds and absorbs it all. These are different objectives, not interchangeable units. Heal and reset use separate cooldowns and can be staggered; they still compete for the same cast when both are wanted on one turn, and a ranged support walk spends a basic.

For a recorded heal with pre-cast deficit D, current amount H and one extra Focus shard, the immediate counterfactual ceiling is `min(D,H+15)-min(D,H)`. It is0 when D≤H. Larger Focus also raises `worth_healing`'s60% threshold, so a previously accepted small heal can disappear unless the recipient is below half HP. An actual accepted cast does not prove the extra HP would avoid recall/death, enable another camp phase or remain useful. Willow cannot heal herself: Health and Jungle/Durability protect the service provider as well as her farm work. Healing HoR also reduces his missing-health damage multiplier.

At150 gold: Power Strength adds18S; Power Focus30F/+90 nominal heal. Jungle Health adds250HP plus the first30% camp mitigation; Jungle Strength15S with that same mitigation; Jungle Focus25F/+75 nominal heal. Bruiser Strength adds15S/5F/50HP; Bruiser Focus3S/25F/50HP; Bruiser Health3S/5F/250HP. A same-family Bruiser stat comparison is consequently a cleaner bounded support trade than removing Jungle protection or Treasure income.

The new independent-step price can eventually exceed available healing: gain must exceed3B. With no Monster and base F120, even a full360 heal fails at S≥120; with Monster the boundary is S≥109.09. This is only the new helper's conservative movement price, not a ban on generic in-range heals or existing formation. It is a deliberate own-output tradeoff, and not by itself evidence that the item build is wrong.

## Recommendation and smallest useful experiment

First measure **marginal absorption at real casts**, not offered/possible heals: pre-cast deficit, amount, actual gain, actual current stats and the existing attack/reset counts at a purchase boundary. This can be captured at existing heal selection seams without scanning allies every turn. Do not accumulate offered HP, count a cast as fully useful automatically, or add a service term to farm DPS. No isolated Willow Focus arm was found in the saved history; older generic Focus/kiting failures are different policies. The earlier prefix-shop303 arm tested shopping access, not this missing service objective.

If that evidence shows repeated above-cap demand during an actually active service role, the smallest controlled item arm for Astra to specify is **at most one completed Bruiser Focus in place of a newly selected Bruiser Strength**, after Treasure and all begun partials, while retaining current Jungle/Durability/Health alternatives. It exchanges12S for20F at equal price and equal50HP, keeping a concrete opportunity cost rather than declaring heal HP equal damage. Require observed marginal demand rather than map name or visible future recipient promises; decay/observation support and the minimum demand condition need explicit design before implementation. One full heal alone is not a defensible sustained-demand certificate. Always finish a begun item, and keep the chosen alternative's real stats, completion, price and own-output saving consistent in `best_emblem`, `prices`, prefix and shop consumers. The service preference is an explicit experimental policy tradeoff, not a proven extension of the current upkeep utility. Do not fabricate a recalled-trip saving to force its quote positive.

I do **not** recommend forced Focus-first or a generic heal multiplier in `raw_output`. Current Strength remains defensible where Willow actually attacks hundreds of times and added Focus would be mostly capped. If a cheap, stable demand certificate cannot distinguish those cases, retain current policy and defer this arm rather than invent a coefficient. Qualification must jointly measure purchase activation/completion and quote behavior, extra actual absorbed healing, recipient productive continuation, our camp damage/captures/capital, own deaths/return time, lost basic/reset turns, helper admission, fuel/errors and team result on broad+fresh panels. Falsify the Focus hypothesis if demand rarely absorbs the upgrade, casts vanish under the higher worth threshold, providers/recipients separate, or farm/survival losses outweigh delivered service. No draft or safety relaxation follows from these item observations.
