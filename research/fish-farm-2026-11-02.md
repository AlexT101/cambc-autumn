## Findings

- **Fish stays packed and we split.** Fish's nearest teammate is 4.0-4.9 tiles away against our 6.7-7.4. Its largest
  cluster (allies linked within 8) holds 77-83% of its bodies against our 65-68%. Its bodies are outside that cluster
  23% of their alive time, ours 36%, and both are mostly walking or banking when outside. Our team first splits into
  two clusters at a median r17, Fish's at r34. Fish's first target has a crew of 3.5 (65% of its bodies), ours 2.4.
  Local astraeus self-play shows the same spread as our ladder games, so this is our own behaviour, not the opponent's.
- **Crews come from who is already near.** At the first hit on an Elk, Fish has 3.69 allies within 10 tiles and we
  have 2.65. Its second member starts hitting a median 2 rounds after the first, ours 4. Crew sizes:

  | Groups | Fish | Us |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Lone Elk, crews of 4+ | 53% | 16% |
  | Lone Elk, crews of 3+ | 82% | 50% |
  | Wolves, crews of 2+ | 70% | 58% |
  | Wolves, crews of 3+ | 45% | 18% |
  | Clusters, crews of 2+ | ~40% | 15% |

  Fish's crews are 4+ on 61% of two-Elk groups and 70% of Golems.
- **Big crews clear faster and lose nothing.** Elk and Golem groups take a median 17.5 rounds with 4+ champions and
  25-30 with 2. We lose 9% of our Elks to the enemy and Fish loses 1%. With crews of 2 we lose 11% and Fish loses 0%.
- **Fish's crews move on together.** After a crew clear, 70% of Fish's crew hit the same next target, and 31% of
  crews go there whole. Ours: 61% and 20%. The next hit comes a median 17 rounds after the clear for Fish and 21 for us.
- **Packing costs no throughput.** Camp damage per living champion-round is level: 17.8 for Fish, 18.1 for us. The
  gain is in what that damage converts to:

  | Per game | Fish | Us |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Capture gems | 240 | 193 |
  | Capture gold | 1227 | 981 |
  | Gold earned | 1602 | 1168 |
  | Gems per 1k camp damage | 8.79 | 8.26 |
  | Gems per tribute (5.9 vs 6.0 tributes) | 32 | 20.6 |
  | Deaths | 1.79 | 2.93 |
  | Gems lost by dying | 6.7 | 16.3 |

  Fish also fields more bodies (5.42 against our 4.82, from the draft): capture gems per body are 44 against 40. Our
  champions die with a median 3.59 enemies within 8.
- **Fish's Cassi farms with the pack.** It deals 4512 camp damage, 80% of it in crews of 3+, and dies 0.51 times a
  game. Ours is a gold farmer (`gold_farmer`): 3312 camp damage and 0.91 deaths. Fish's Marcella holds captures (hits
  only 12% of rounds) and Vesper does the most damage (9644).
- **The body gap is the draft.** Fish drafts Cassi & Issac (two bodies for one pick) in 134 of 135 games, we do in
  53. That is all of the 5.42 against 4.82 bodies. Fish's pair deals 8072 camp damage by r300. Most single picks of either team deal
  4000-6700; only Fish's Vesper (9644) and our Varg (7351) come near or beat it. astraeus drafts it in 50% of local A/B games.
- **Fish's Marcella earns gold by holding.** She earns 611 gold by r300 (ours 372) and captures 96 gems (ours 61)
  while hitting only 12% of rounds: she holds the crews' captures and tributes them for her 2 gold a gem.
- **Fish sends nearly the whole team to its first strong group.** Its first strong clear has a crew of 4.3 of 5.45
  bodies (ours 2.9 of 4.83), more often an Elk (55% against 40%). Its second strong group starts at a median r41,
  after the first is done; ours at r33, in parallel.
- **The other top teams.** Mite and Ad Astra spread like us: largest cluster 70-73%, 4+ crews on 20-27% of lone
  Elks. Fish packs the same against every opponent. pbg packs like Fish (80%, 48% 4+ on lone Elks) yet loses 114 of
  141 games with 4.88 deaths a game. Packing is not enough on its own: it pays with Fish's low deaths.

## Rules to test

Earlier cohesion terms were level: buddy cohesion read 49.4% and a cohesion bonus near claims was level
(EXPERIMENTS). So the variants change who joins whom, with no attraction term:

1. **A crew moves on together** (`as-crew-follow`). An ally stays our crew-mate for `crew_mate_rounds` (12) after we
   last claimed the same strong group. A crew-mate's next strong claim gets the crew hold (`crew_stickiness` 1.5) in
   farm's claimed tier, as our own claim does, in the prune bound and in the value. It uses claims already in comms,
   so it costs no fuel. Expected: more 3+ crews after the first clear and fewer splits.
2. **Cassi farms with the pack** (`as-cassi-pack`). Cassi is no longer a gold farmer (`gold_farmer`, `farms_gold`).
   Tavi on Tavi maps still is. Expected: bigger Elk crews and fewer lone Cassi deaths, at some cost in gold-cluster
   gold. The gold farmer read "level" when it was added.
3. **Allies located by comms join crews** (`as-pos-crew`). Most of our allies are out of sight, so `crew_available`
   never expects them. Each ally now posts its coarse position in the bits left after the capture record:
   - The record is 1 + cell × team + the poster's index, in square cells of the smallest side up to 8 that fits.
   - Ally i posts in rounds ≡ i mod team, so readers date a post from the poster's index. That holds under any turn
     order, unlike fuchsia's deltas.
   - A post counts for team-size rounds. It costs one write per team-size rounds and no fuel gates.
   - It needs at most 10 bits (side 8, 6 bodies, 100x100). Bits left after the capture record, over the local A/B
     maps: 13+ for 3-4 bodies on every map, 11+ for 5 bodies on 97% and for 6 bodies on 72%. 00ambush (6 bodies) fits
     at side 1. A trace there read allies in sight within 1 tile of their post in 98% of reads; two reads were 38-45
     tiles off (moved by recall or respawn since posting, presumably).

   Consumers:
   - `crew_available`, by the posted position.
   - `plan_crew`'s order of expected allies.
   - `share_damage`'s arrival test.
   - Claimers' freshness, in `plan_crew` and both of farm's claimer counts (`crew_claimer_known`).

   Expected: more crews proposed with the right members, and larger crews on Elks.

A/B jobs, each against `variants/astraeus-9abbbde` (queued in `replays/dmm/jobs3.txt`):
- `as-crew-follow-vs-astraeus`
- `as-cassi-pack-vs-astraeus`
- `as-pos-crew-vs-astraeus`

Each job's farm metrics land in `replays/dmm/<name>-farm.txt`. Compare crew sizes and splits there with this page.

crew-follow and pos-crew barely moved the team. Measured with `mine.py` on their 1196 analysed A/B games against
astraeus:

| | crew-follow | astraeus | pos-crew | astraeus | Fish |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lone Elks with crews of 4+ | 20% | 17% | 18% | 18% | 53% |

So a 1.5x hold or comms-located allies do not make the team pack. The farm's rate counts the crew's split of the
reward and the trip. By that rate, joining a crew is never better than an equal camp alone at the same distance: the
team's throughput is the same, and only the trip differs. A crew pays through what the rate leaves out: fewer camps
lost to the enemy, fewer deaths and local strength in fights. A test of packing needs a pull strong enough to change
crews.

## Round 3: where the rounds go (r1-300)

`replays/bigarm/scripts/fishnov/timeuse.py` sorts each alive round of a champion by what it did. In order of precedence:
it hit a camp; it damaged or stunned an enemy (fight); it channelled a tribute; it was in a recall that completed; it was
walking back to an altar it then tributed at with gems (altar walk); it stood within 3 of a group with live camps or a
capture pending and was not closing on it (wait, or hold if the group was cleared); it was walking toward a group it then
hit or waited at (walk). Everything else is other: standing at spawn, standing elsewhere, or moving. Dead rounds count
separately. `tusum.py` prints the means. Our A/B builds trace each turn's branch (`D` lines), which it splits each
activity into with `--branches`.

- **Against Fish, we lose the rounds to death.** We are dead for 32 rounds per champion by r300, and Fish for 17. That
  is twice Fish's, and it costs us 6 hit rounds. 56% of our deaths come outnumbered (more enemies within 8 than allies
  + 1), against 21% of Fish's. Those are fights and packing (rounds 1-2), not farm time.
- **Fish banks less often, with bigger bags.** Its bank time is 24 rounds; ours is 29 on the ladder and 40 in
  self-play. Each Fish team tributes 5.3-5.8 times by r300 at 32 gems, and ours 5.8 times on the ladder and 9.0 in
  self-play, at 20 gems. Fish recalls 12.3 times a team (5.4 with a bag), and astraeus 20.1 (10.3 with a bag). Fish's
  pair barely banks: Cassi 14 and Issac 18 rounds, with altar walks of 3.6 and 3.7 against our 12-17. Fish's captures go
  to Marcella (5.1 shares by r300), Vesper (5.7) and Vendetta (4.4), and only 1.8 and 1.6 to Cassi and Issac. Our pair
  holds 4.3 and 5.1. Recall time is 39% `capture_recall` (Q, a recall timed to land as a hold pays, so mostly hold
  time) and 34% `recall_kept`.
- **Fish walks more and hits about as much.** Fish walks 86 rounds against our 70 and stands idle less (2 rounds
  against 5). It hits 66 rounds against our 73 in self-play. Its Vesper hits 96 rounds for 9.6k damage, against our 72
  rounds for 6.4k. Our Vesper moves without hitting 62 rounds against 34.
- **pair-pack's extra deaths are the pair's, to camps.** Over whole games, camps kill 0.58 bodies a game in pair-pack
  (0.23 of them Cassi or Issac, each taking its partner too: 0.23 linked deaths). In astraeus they kill 0.46 (0.04 of the
  pair, 0.04 linked). That is +0.3 bodies, all from the pair. A pair game loses 0.23 pairs to camps in pair-pack and
  0.08 in astraeus's. Cassi is now the body killed most (0.12 a game against 0.02): cassi-pack took her off the
  harmless gold groups into camp crews. Fish's deaths are 28% to camps against us, too.

The biggest gap outside the draft is bank time: 16 rounds per champion in self-play, mostly altar walks and
recalls by bodies with small bags. Caution, earlier recalls and bigger bag limits have all lost or come out level
(EXPERIMENTS). The variants below change who carries instead. Captures go to bodies that already carry or that bank
cheaply. That way fewer bodies make trips and each trip carries more.

Time and deaths:
- **The pair farms more.** Bank time fell from 29 to 26 rounds for Cassi and from 39 to 35 for Issac, and their hit

(Varg, Vendetta, Heart of Ruin) or never bank (Prism).
