## Both requested corrections hold

**Qualification allocations (`plan.cpp:846–898`).** The current implementation tests the existing member-position vector against `world.visible` and uses prebuilt `group_at` lookup for occupant membership. It neither calls `G.tiles()` nor constructs complement/intersection bitboards. Three fixed49-entry arrays deduplicate at most49 endpoint groups; each encountered group is qualified once, including failures. The revised claim of no new dynamic allocation in this qualification block is supported by source. It does not mean zero cost: member scans can extend beyond the49-cell search grid, and otherwise eligible groups scan allied/enemy vectors. Distinct groups have disjoint camp tiles, bounding the aggregate member scan by camp-tile count; no whole-map tile loop or new route field is added here. Existing search/goal machinery can still allocate and add work.

**Signed progress (`plan.cpp:1880–1887`).** The prior unconditional suppression is replaced by an exact predicate: suppress field progress only at an economic endpoint whose group equals `intent.group`, with `intent.to_altar == false`. A distinct farm objective retains `(here_steps - endpoint_steps) * step`, including negative movement away, positive movement toward and zero equal-distance movement. An altar objective retains its term even if stale `intent.group` happens to equal the economic group. Ordinary non-economic endpoints retain baseline behavior. Missing/unreachable fields, fewer than two candidates or disabled goal walking still produce no field term, as before.

For illustrative `step=10`, current distance5, a different objective at distance3 receives+20 and one at7 receives−20. A same-objective economic endpoint receives0 field credit; its occupancy quote remains separate. This is an algebraic source check, not an engine result. Ordinary strike leaves on an economic tile obey the same objective predicate, so their unrelated route penalty is preserved too. The separate removal of an unused later farm-walk credit at economic endpoints (`1514–1518`) remains appropriate because successful economic commitment returns before farm/chase.

## Visibility and state: supported at this call site, not a global guarantee

Qualification uses World beliefs, not a fresh native camp query. `b.state_round == world.round` is necessary but alone does not prove current visibility; `World::observe()` refreshes vision/champions without refreshing camps. The deleted upstream `camps_current` helpers are not restored.

The actual nonzero-economic caller is the first strike search in `decide` (`bot.cpp:4936–4961`). Start-of-turn World update has read vision, champions and camps. A start-of-turn ward relocation triggers `see_again` before this search (`4883–4885`), refreshing the visibility mask and visible occupants. Earlier successful local-support movement, capture yielding or Vesper handoff returns from decide, rather than proceeding into this economic search. Failed hidden-blocker attempts may add native visibility that World has not copied; using the older visibility subset then abstains on newly revealed members rather than proving them visible. No earlier successful camp-damage action normally precedes this entry. The later `strike_after` call explicitly sets `anchored=true` and leaves both economic quotes zero (`plan.cpp:2227–2229`), so it cannot reuse pre-move visibility for a new economic option.

Thus I found no reachable stale-position admission specific to the present economic caller. This is a call-site argument, not a reusable guarantee for `read_economics`. A future port after movement/damage must re-establish it. World and native group state can still diverge after caught observation errors; exact occupant/holder agreement and current-round state reduce admission, but do not replace recording error cleanliness. Ward expansion can reveal a whole group while its holder estimate remains older; occupants must agree, and the estimate must be unexpired. `held_since/payout_round` remains an estimate rather than engine-readable capture progress, and the code does not multiply it into a claimed guaranteed reward.

Normal-fuel controls should explicitly include a group revealed by a relocated own ward, a lost-visibility group, a newly cleared member, a failed hidden-blocker attempt and a same-turn after-goal call. Expect conservative abstention where beliefs are not current; do not add a full observation or speculate about a hidden holder merely to force activation.

Current fully visible cleared positive-gold/zero-gem non-Monolith groups qualify only with consistent occupant/holder beliefs, no other allied body and an unexpired enemy-hold estimate. Native self position is used. An enemy occupant receives denial value while alive; modeled death changes it to prospective own-capture value, while stun does not remove occupancy. Unknown/unrepresented enemy occupants remain conservative. None is an immediate gold payment or six-turn survival guarantee.
