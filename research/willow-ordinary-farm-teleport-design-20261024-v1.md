## Channel, arrival and action-order constraints

Teleport cannot be cancelled; damage does not interrupt it, but stun or death does (`cambc-rules.yaml`, teleport/channel/stun sections). A one-turn `Safety::threatened` result is not a three-turn commitment certificate. For the initial quiet-route arm, conservatively exclude a source or landing exposed to a **credible** enemy over the channel horizon using public kit maximum reach plus possible approach during that interval, not only currently-ready cooldowns. From a start in roundR, arrival is own turn-start R+3; up to four enemy activations can precede arrival depending on order. Reuse the existing tracked-position/possible-set credibility policy for unseen enemies with that expanded bound. Do not label unobserved enemies impossible, but do not require global enemy visibility. This is a conservative local admission test, not an exact future fight prediction or a claim no hidden opponent can intervene.

Camp exposure also spans future phases: count the three source camp phases before arrival, including a known respawn before R+3; do not extrapolate a current stun or sleeping flag through all three. The nearby static-member index bounds the work. Price a conservative source-damage allowance and its existing healing/upkeep cost, and keep the ordinary health affordability/reserve; do not credit future allied tanking/heals. A landing must have a finite ordinary onward route and pass the existing local movement/camp checks with known respawns included. Unknown camp lifecycle may use the normal pessimistic belief; this is no license to query every camp globally. Static camp tiles are never landings, so respawn cannot put a monster on the destination itself, though nearby monster attacks still matter.

Use fresh current records after any earlier heal/attack/cast/ward that changes local information. Earlier attacks or casts are legal before starting a channel; do not unnecessarily erase useful work or assume exclusive casting forbids crystal use. The hook belongs where the selected farm action would otherwise travel, after higher-priority support/combat/holding/banking decisions. A held capture must not be abandoned merely to take the travel shortcut. Avoid adding a speculative pre-farm attack whose damage was not already selected. The price still charges the full channel, with no speculative value from actions during its skipped turns.

SDK `consumable::use(destination)` (`sdk/cpp/cambc.h:2710–2720`) returns false on a newly revealed hidden obstruction; known-invalid targets throw. Make one chosen attempt, then observe/revalidate and return to ordinary fallback if it fails, without cycling through blind destinations. A successful start is terminal for gameplay actions that turn: skip strike/heal/ward/recall tails that are illegal while teleporting. Keep ordinary comms/read-only bookkeeping. At actual arrival rebuild observation/pathing/Safety normally and revalidate objective/claim/camp state; do not resume a stale attack sequence. A visible reservation prevents informed units entering, but does not justify assuming future occupancy or enemy behavior is fixed.

Willow receives exactly the same route option only after her existing support/hold/bank priorities have yielded to ordinary farm travel. Her0-gold witness will not suddenly activate it. That is a factual funding limit, not a reason to ban non-Vesper crystals or divert all capture income. If a later funded Willow travel case skips reachable valuable healing, measure that lost service instead of calling reduced walking an automatic benefit.
