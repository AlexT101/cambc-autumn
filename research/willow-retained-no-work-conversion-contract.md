# Retained Prism: a terminal no-work conversion handoff

2026-10-23. Bounded source proposal against frozen canonical integration `4db4eecbda37e23dbce0b162d2cf7441a3d32a554d6534cf196e3ffbedcdf2fd`. Read `bot.cpp` and `research/willow-fd99-rows-recall-stationarity-source-review.md`. No source/runtime changes. This is independently testable policy consistency, not attribution of the Rows replay.

There is a real source-level gap worth a small experiment. Retention means a nonwinning Prism bag is not yet closing (`685`); closing prices altar travel, tribute and the existing margin (`674`). Farm's work deadline adds approach, wait, clear and capture (`2259`). The latter can reject all proposed work while the former still permits retention. At the terminal no-choice return (`2378`), ordinary decide ignores farm's boolean; there need be neither productive work nor conversion. A selected respawn wait, current capture, early attack, retained route after partial evaluation, or higher-priority combat/retreat is not this state.

## Proposed narrow contract

Add an ephemeral terminal result such as `no_work_before_conversion_deadline`, distinct from farm's existing boolean meaning. Do not change global closing, retention or recall thresholds. Produce this result only at ordinary decision F's final no-choice branch, after all normal own/risky/shared/help selection:

1. This is retained Prism with positive **bankable** bag, not siege, capture holding/contesting or a turn already used for combat/work. Early successful attack/capture/pickup/movement returns remain unchanged. Keep the no-move/no-basic/no-cast-spent condition explicit before acting on the result; do not turn a partially used turn into a new conversion plan.
2. The normal loop actually completed: `k == evaluated == n_groups`, with a fresh world. No new fuel threshold or expanded scan. An iteration count is not proof that each candidate was forecast; preserve original pruning and short circuits.
3. No selected job in any tier exists. Every selected profitable wait keeps its original route/wait behavior. Do not use a failed route to a selected job as this trigger.
4. At least one positive-worth, finite known candidate reached the existing deadline computation and was rejected there. Set this witness only inside the actual evaluated `too_late` result; never repeat its route/model calls to manufacture a witness.
5. A positive-work possibility skipped solely because its respawn wait exceeds max_wait, or because required evaluation was unavailable, prevents the certificate. This prevents an intentional wait-until-eligible state from being mislabeled as a time-impossible clear. Track only scalar uncertainty at existing branches; no additional group scan or forecast. Structural exclusions and known unreachable approaches remain exclusions under the existing policy. Competitive pruning must not be labeled deadline failure; with final no selection it supplies no positive time proof.

Consume this result once at the ordinary farm caller, after the earlier priority actions have declined. Perform existing conversion routing/altar choice, not another carry_bag call that can chase or recursively farm. Scope any bypass of retention to this explicit invocation. Clear the abandoned farm claim/crew identity before announcing conversion. Do not fabricate closing or winning status, remove camp/enemy guards, or grant any_risk permission.

**Submission needs an explicit safety check.** Current `bank_gems` (`776`) returns immediately for retained Prism. Merely setting memory.banking therefore fails; ordinary decide also clears that flag while retaining (`5085`). Moreover, `submit_here` (`709`) normally trusts caller safety at the starting tile. A dedicated no-work invocation must require `tribute_ok` for every attempted submission, including a failed movement followed by submission from the original tile. Preserve locks, avoidance, pack routing and ordinary movement guards. The implementation can use an explicit conversion reason/option, but ordinary banking callers must retain their existing behavior. Do not globally remove the retention guard.

Start with no permanent conversion latch. Next turn reevaluates normal work under current information; a newly feasible job may legitimately resume. A successfully started tribute follows existing continuation rules. This limits the cost of a stale no-work assessment and preserves the retained bag's combat benefit until actual tribute. It does not guarantee monotone movement or successful banking: route blockage remains possible, and moving toward an altar can make another job feasible. Measure that reversal behavior before adding persistence. Existing end-turn recall stubs remain unchanged; a one-turn result must not silently commit future recalls.

## Activating model case and limits

For example, suppose the existing race horizon is 100 turns, altar walking takes 10, and the current bag needs 30 tribute turns. The ordinary 20-turn margin leaves closing false. Every otherwise viable evaluated job needs at least 65 turns of approach/wait/clear/capture, followed by 10 walking and at least 30 tribute, so each existing deadline fails. There is no selected wait, uncertain skipped work, immediate useful action or higher-priority objective. The proposed result permits a safe conversion attempt now instead of a no-choice return. These numbers illustrate the existing inequality; they are not new thresholds or a claim about Rows.

The certificate means no useful job survived the current policy's complete evaluation, with a genuine deadline rejection and no unresolved wait. It does not prove no hidden camp, later teammate work, changed race rate, or future respawn can improve the alternatives. The race estimate already includes known/posted team capital and teammate tribute rate; do not replace it with this carrier's own capture rate, which would assume it continues farming after leaving. The experiment should report actual activation, movement/tribute success, next-turn reversals, capital converted, forgone work and native fuel errors. Do not claim that avoiding eleven Rows standing turns would alone erase its completion deficit.

Recommendation: implement this terminal result and one-invocation safe conversion handoff as a separate 4db child if approved. It is a more precise experiment than unconditional early banking or changing every idle return. If conservative unresolved-wait gating prevents activation, report that boundary before broadening it; do not silently reinterpret incomplete knowledge as a deadline proof.
