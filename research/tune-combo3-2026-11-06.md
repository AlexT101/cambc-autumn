# SPSA retune of astraeus with the three camp changes (combo3), 2026-11-06

Question: do three rule-correct changes that each read level or worse (the attack counter fix, area hits and stuns
in the camp-clear estimate, camp damage by the nearest-champion rule) pay off once astraeus's SPSA-tuned constants
are retuned for them? The decisive comparison is tuned combo3 against astraeus tuned the same way (the control),
head to head and against a common panel, not tuned combo3 against untuned astraeus.

Tools: `research/tune-combo3-2026-11-06/` (`params.txt`, the tuned set; `tune_fresh.py`, tune.py with fresh maps
every iteration).

## Bots

- `bots/variants/astraeus-8c0343f`: astraeus at 8c0343f5e (CHANGES 440), the control's base.
- `bots/variants/astraeus-combo3`: 8c0343f plus the three changes (merge of `astraeus-campdmg2`,
  `astraeus-areaclear`, and feintfix's bot.cc/util.h).

## Phase 1: integration check

*Audit* (one read-only reviewer over the combo3 diff and both parents' diffs). Every file only one parent touched is
that parent's, plus main's hunks; bot.cc and util.h are feintfix's; the shared files (bot_parts.h, crew.cc, siege.cc,
farm.cc) are areaclear plus exactly campdmg2's hunks rewritten to the kit-aware calls. No hunk dropped, no merge bug.
The two models never act on the same quantity: areaclear sets a clear's total damage and rounds, campdmg2 how that
total is split (and who is hit each phase). Per call site:
- Both models: `share_damage`/`plan_crew` (kit-aware totals, split over stands), `crew_part` (all three callers pass
  kit-aware joint totals), farm's `engaged_part` and the engaged `affordable`, the kited join check, the kited
  unclaimed partner, the crewable and claimed paths, `siege_ready` (no enemy tank, fire through `stunned_fire`).
- areaclear only, by design: solo `affordable`, `struggling`, `recovered_for_work`, `finishing_camp`, `warm_clears`,
  `kite_plan`, the prune bounds (scalar and SIMD agree; campdmg2 changed only filters there), siege staging and burst.
- campdmg2 only, by design: per-phase readings (`find_members`/`camp_damage`/`tie_hit`, `ally_camp_fire`, the shield
  trigger, `find_wakers`/`wake`, `camp_cast`'s stun value, `mono_trapped`, home `sleeping_fire`, siege cover).
- Neither: shop's emblem pricing, capture's shards, the draft (as areaclear intended).
- No double counts: stun skips are never taken from fire that rivals already reduced; no site sums allies' outputs
  but prices with our kit only; every cache campdmg2's readings could stale is keyed by kit/covers or recomputed per
  call (`camp_rivals()` resets on `invalidate()`/`refresh()`).
- find_wakers' rivals model covers what main's `held_this_round` fixed (an unacted enemy's feint is not held; a
  recall or teleport completing at its turn start is excluded by `camp_present`), and is stricter in one rules-correct
  way (an unacted enemy's kept recall or tribute is not held). `held_this_round` is now unused.
- feintfix's count_use: no interaction (`turn_acts` reset before `act`; the early returns are turns with no attack).

*Fixed* (both areaclear-internal, made more visible by summed crew kits):
- `stun_saved` gave every unit stun (unit and bare kinds) the strongest striker; stuns don't stack, so a second one
  takes another striker. Now the first takes the strongest and each further one the other strikers' mean fire
  (`stun_skips`' fifth entry). Area stuns are left as they were.
- `siege_ready` took `home_free` (spawn-stand fire that costs nothing) off already-stunned fire, so stun skips on
  those members came off twice; now the stuns are applied to the fire left after `home_free`.
- Left: dead code (`held_this_round`, `CrewWork::kit`, `Waker::ally`); `engaged_part` prices at our own
  `home_survivable`, not the crew's least; the crew total priced at our stand; partners picked by raw output.
- The two fixes change none of the 52 wide-bench games at 100M (rare paths).

## Phase 2: what ocean's tuning history says

- *Injection.* `research/diana-2026-11-03/tunify.py` turns each chosen `inline constexpr` in params.h into a
  `const&` into one global block behind a 16-byte magic (`tune.h`, `tune.json`); `tools/tune/patch.py` rewrites that
  block in the built wasm and in the fast runner's cached native library and wasmtime module, so a candidate costs no
  compile and plays byte-identically to a fresh build. Constants used in constant expressions need a hand edit
  (here: farm.cc `exposure_most` becomes a `const float`; safety.h's `hit_given_reach`/`recall_risk` and their
  static_assert are left alone by not tuning them).
- *Runs that worked.* d1 (diana, 51 constants, six-bot panel, first tune of hand-set values): +4.6 against pre-tune.
  oc2 (61 constants after many logic changes; astraeus, proteus and pre-tune ocean, ladder maps): +3.2 head to head.
  oc4 (23 constants chosen from oc2/oc3's gradients plus 413/414's new knobs; half the games against its own base):
  +1.0 head to head, panel +1.5, OOF -5%.
- *Runs that did not.* oc3 (67 constants, a second round on oc2's values; only 1 opponent in 5 its own base): +0.9
  on its panel, level head to head (50.3% of 40,960). Its gradients: 55 of 67 constants had |t| < 2, several of the
  "biggest moves" flipped sign between runs (drift around an optimum), and the ±1 ints at 2 (`pursuit_steps`,
  `ostrich_rounds`, `max_wait`) measured curvature, never slope. oc5 (26 constants on oc4's plateau): +0.6 in-run,
  level held out (50.25% of 40,960); constants already at a plateau. ocean_tune3 against astraeus read 49.0% with
  +41% out-of-fuel turns (a tune copy's patched block is not free of fuel effects when a run drifts into costlier
  behaviour). The exploiter search (x1) found no broad weakness of d1's constants.
- *Opponents and drafts.* Every ocean run used own drafts against a fixed panel (own base, astraeus, proteus,
  sometimes demeter), with the ladder maps at weight 8 plus 20 fresh suites reused across iterations. d1's overfitting
  check was a ladder test drafter outside the panel. AGENTS.md: own drafts on both sides, except deliberately against
  a test-draft opponent.
- *Lessons taken:* the head-to-head share of the objective matters most (oc3 vs oc4); retunes of a plateau find
  nothing (oc3, oc5), so a gain here has to come from the changes moving the optimum; leave out ±1 ints and
  constants with fixed-point roles; judge only on held-out suites, head to head and on a panel.

## Phase 2: design

*Parameters*: 42 of params.h's 72 (`research/tune-combo3-2026-11-06/params.txt`, 29 floats and 13 ints). The user
allowed any set. I kept a broad set rather than the three changes' neighbourhood alone, because the changes move the
farm/fight balance and constants far from the camp code (retreat ratios, recall lines, values) were what moved in
oc2/oc4; but I left out the 30 that ocean's history says only add drift or cannot be tuned this way:
- ±1-curvature ints at 2 (`pursuit_steps`, `ostrich_rounds`, `max_wait`), and small structural ints (`crew_cap`,
  `big_holders`);
- turn_order.cc's fixed copies (`turn_*`, modulo-15 rank codes) and the static_assert-bound `hit_given_reach`,
  `recall_risk` (|t| < 1 in oc4);
- constants flat or sign-flipping in both oc2 and oc3 that the changes don't touch: the mid-band shares and edge,
  crew radii and waits (`crew_available_radius`, `crew_near_radius`, `crew_wait_rounds`, `crew_seen_rounds`,
  `crew_enemy_radius`), `pack_ally_radius`, `siege_respawn_wait`, `siege_max_wait`, `siege_stickiness`, `big_gems`,
  `tribute_value`, `recall_value`, Tavi's `gold_farmer_weight`;
- untuned new knobs unrelated to camps (`reveal_mass`, `reveal_prior`, `stun_tribute_horizon`).
Kept: every camp, crew, siege, Monolith and spare-health constant (`camp_risk`, `crew_trip_risk`, `crew_tank_line`
(campdmg2's tank line), `finish_camp_health_fraction`, `crew_stickiness`, `crew_rival_factor`, `crew_ban_rounds`,
`partner_radius`, `help_radius`, `siege_*` four floats plus `siege_rounds`, `burst_rounds`, `kite_early`,
`mono_*`), shard/emblem choice (`fight_weight`, `fight_camp_prior`, `shop_gain_scale`), farm pricing (`home_power`,
`gold_price_scale`, `mid_exposure`, `max_exposure`, `pick_up_radius`, `chain_bag_limit`, `pair_hold_share`), and the
retreat/recall/value constants with real signal in oc2-oc4. Ranges: tune.py's half to double (fractions capped at
0.95), except `camp_risk` [0.23, 1.0] and `crew_trip_risk` [0.4, 1.4], which the cap would have pinned near their
values; ints move at least 1 each way.

*Opponents* (both runs, 1 game each per map): `astraeus-8c0343f`, `astraeus-combo3`, `ocean-tc3`, and
`astraeus-8c0343f-td` (test drafts, the deliberate exception: ladder compositions against our own draft). Each run
therefore plays its own base and the other line's base (half the games near-mirror), ocean, and ladder drafts.

*Maps*: `tune_fresh.py` gives iteration k the maps [64k, 64k + 64) of `merged/tc3-pool-806` (200 fresh ladder-mix
suites, 51,200 maps, shuffled once), so no map is played in two iterations; no ladder maps. Both runs play the same
maps and sign vectors at each k (same seed): common random numbers between them.

*SPSA*: oc4's settings: 64 maps x 4 opponents x 4 sign pairs = 2,048 games an iteration, weights win 0.75 / gems
0.25 / gold 0 (gold-pricing constants are in the set), a 0.25, A 100, c 0.12, 2 batches in flight, real fuel, release
builds, fast runner. 700 iterations each (1.43M games), both runs concurrently at full resolution.

*Held out* (suites no tuning touched): `tc3-h2h-8071` tuned against tuned (20,480); `tc3-self-8072` each tuned bot
against its untuned base (20,480 each); `tc3-ocean-8073` against ocean (20,480 each); `tc3-panel-8074` against
proteus, demeter, v67, ocean-td and astraeus-now-1106 (5,120 each; none in the tuning mix); `tc3-varied-8076`
(`--mix varied`, 5,120) tuned against tuned. Tuned bots are `tune.py apply` copies (both carry the same +0.1% fuel
of the patchable block).

*Tune copies*: `bots/variants/astraeus-combo3-tune`, `bots/variants/astraeus-ctl-tune`; at their defaults both
replay identically to their sources at 100M (52/52 wide with test drafts, +0.1% fuel). Runs: `replays/tune/tc3`,
`replays/tune/tc3ctl`.

## Conclusions

- **The retune does not rescue the three changes.** Tuned combo3 loses to tuned astraeus 48.0% head to head, by 2.0
  points on the panel mean (every member at or below), and by 2.5 against ocean, with about a third more out-of-fuel
  turns. The gap is the untuned one (47.6%, panel -1.9): retuning closed none of it.
- **Neither retune transfers.** Each run's +0.9 in-run gain reads level held out against its own base (49.9%, 50.3%),
  and the panel is flat for both (astraeus 69.2 -> 69.1, combo3 67.3 -> 67.1). This is oc3/oc5's result again: from
  oc4's plateau, a 1M-game SPSA on 42 constants finds only noise-sized moves. So the hypothesis fails in its strong
  form: the loss is not a matter of constants tuned under the old logic, at least not of these 42 at this resolution.
  The one consistent signal (combo3 wanting more caution on camp damage, `crew_trip_risk` t +2.6) is real but its
  held-out worth is nil.
- **What is left of the changes**: the extra out-of-fuel turns (+32-35%, areaclear's big-pack farming) are untouched by
  the tune and are the likeliest cost; per EXPERIMENTS "areaclear", a more accurate estimate makes the farm take clears
  the old pessimism avoided, and those clears cost fuel and fights. A follow-up would have to change what the estimate
  is used for (or cap the fuel of big-pack farming), not the constants around it.
- **Recommendation: drop** both tuned combo3 and the retuned constants alone (the control is level, 50.3%, OOF +4.6%).
