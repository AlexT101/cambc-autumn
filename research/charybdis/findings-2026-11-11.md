# charybdis: open findings (2026-11-11)

These come from 8 area reviewers plus one verification pass. Everything here came from reading the code; nothing was built or run, apart from the two probes below. Paths are relative to `bots/charybdis/src`. Line numbers are as of commit 2166f5c20.

Phases 1 and 2 of the plan (the pure fixes and the model corrections) are done and no longer listed: `bots/variants/charybdis-fix1` and `charybdis-fix2`, with results in [EXPERIMENTS.md](../EXPERIMENTS.md) ("Phase 1 review fixes", "Phase 2 review fixes"). What follows is what is left.

Probes (both done, stock cambc 5.0.1):
- **Own locks** (`tools/probes/probe-lock-own`): our own lock does not block our tributes. The YAML is correct. Placing it cancels our ally's running channel, which keeps its gems; our champions can then start tributes there and they score. The enemy's attempts are refused without an error, and each uses its basic attack and takes one of the lock's five hits.
- **Ability damage** (`tools/probes/probe-ability-damage-query`): the target-free `damage_amount()` includes Collectors but not Skirmish. `damage_amount(target)` adds Skirmish.

## 2. Open items (most valuable first)

### 1. Recall cancel not valued for basic attacks or landings (D4)
- **Sites:** plan.cc:1263; hit_unit 1370-1416 including splash; land_at 1479-1501; best_hit plan.cc:118; attack_target self_abilities.cc:425.
- **Fix:** read recall regardless of `casts`. Add params::recall_value on non-lethal damage off spawn, as spell_unit does.
- **Rating:** easy · fuel small · high confidence.
- **Caveat:** A/B.

### 2. Enemy tributes that win only together are not game-ending (A2)
- **Sites:** fight.cc:1107; abilities.cc:616; bank.cc:84.
- **Fix:** add the gems of the other visible enemy tributes landing no later.
- **Rating:** easy-medium · fuel none · medium-high confidence.
- **Caveat:** each new game-ending case sends the bot into stop_game_ender "whatever the risk", so A/B.

### 3. kill_turn counts basic attacks only (D5)
- **Sites:** fight.cc:613-621, feeding :643, :1124 and fixc.cc:66.
- **Fix:** on turn 0, add a ready reset hit and the best ready unit damage spell (raw × e.taken × champion_dealt_factor(), with in-range checks).
- **Rating:** easy-medium · fuel none · medium-high confidence.
- **Caveat:** changes preyable, so A/B.

### 4. Unseen enemy's next-round turn gets a strike with no walk (E2)
- **Sites:** model/safety.cc:1216-1229 (moved_strike), 1246-1249; turn_window.cc:95-121.
- **Fix:** in moved_strike, when turns < 2 and !enemy_may_act_after_us(world, c), return m.value, the full next-round strike.
- **Rating:** easy · fuel none · medium-high confidence.
- **Caveat:** A/B.

### 5. Marcella against a walking game ender casts before her stunning reset
- **Sites:** combat/fight.cc, stop_game_ender's `act`.
- **Fix:** against a tributing ender the stunning reset (or a sure kill: damage spell, then a basic attack) now comes first. A walking ender keeps the old order: damage spell, then attack and reset. A stun there costs it a turn's walk, but choosing it over the spell is a tactic.
- **Rating:** easy · fuel none.
- **Caveat:** A/B.

### 6. Dead allies keep a stale priority for 60+ rounds (H2)
- **Sites:** world.cc:2058-2065.
- **Fix:** once an ally is read dead, from the round after its first dead turn, set next_priority = packed_priority(dead_priority, index, 0) and mark ranks stale. Keep it after respawn until it is seen.
- **Rating:** easy-medium · fuel none · medium confidence.

### 7. Fully asleep groups woken by another champion are never priced (E4)
- **Sites:** world.cc:2143; safety.cc:2294-2343.
- **Fix:** in find_members, also scan alive, fully asleep groups near p that have an unacted champion within strike reach of a member.
- **Rating:** medium · fuel moderate · medium confidence.

### 8. Every visible ally blocks enemy walks and landings (E5)
- **Sites:** safety.cc:1853-1866, 1883.
- **Fix:** block only with allies that don't act before the enemy (the same timing as `fixed`).
- **Rating:** medium · fuel none · medium confidence.
- **Caveat:** makes the bot more conservative, so A/B.

### 9. finishing_camp sums solo clears (C1)
- **Sites:** camps.cc:830-842.
- **Fix:** sort groups by clear rounds; add each later group's per-round fire × the rounds elapsed before it.
- **Rating:** medium · fuel small · medium confidence.
- **Caveat:** A/B.

### 10. Medium difficulty, low value
- **C3:** per-striker reach in the reflect path.
- **G3:** walks can't cross holes in real_distance.
- **G4 (rest):** exclude believed-live camp tiles from dash landings. A landing fails on a camp tile out of sight whose monster is alive (respawned since we cleared it, or behind a wall or bush). The retry after such a failure is done; this filter would avoid the failed try.
- **G1 (rest):** a camp we kill stays in the path walls until the next vision read, because they come from camp beliefs (Pathing::live_camps). Champion kills are done.
- **G6:** knight-shaped crossings.
- **E6:** respawning enemies in safety.
- **Guard exposure for a stun-immune self** (combat/guard.cc, GuardFoes::exposure and its windows): still counts stun-only hits (Cassi's stun) for our Marcella. Filtering only the tile side would make that foe read as reaching beyond us, so the windows built from enemy reach need the same filter.
