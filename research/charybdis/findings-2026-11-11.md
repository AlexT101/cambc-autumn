# charybdis: verified findings (2026-11-11)

These come from 8 area reviewers plus one verification pass. Everything here came from reading the code; nothing was built or run, apart from the two probes below. Paths are relative to `bots/charybdis/src`. Line numbers are as of commit 2166f5c20.

Probes (both done, stock cambc 5.0.1):
- **Own locks** (`tools/probes/probe-lock-own`): our own lock does not block our tributes. The YAML is correct. Placing it cancels our ally's running channel, which keeps its gems; our champions can then start tributes there and they score. The enemy's attempts are refused without an error, and each uses its basic attack and takes one of the lock's five hits.
- **Ability damage** (`tools/probes/probe-ability-damage-query`): the target-free `damage_amount()` includes Collectors but not Skirmish. `damage_amount(target)` adds Skirmish.

## 2. Deduped confirmed list (most valuable first)

### 1. Marcella casts before her stunning reset when stopping a game-ending tribute (B1)
- **Sites:** combat/fight.cc:1145-1150.
- **Fix:** run attack_enemy, then reset_and_attack_enemy(world, true), before cast_at_enemies, as the mend branch does (bot.cc:454-457). Better: aim the stunning attack at the ender explicitly.
- **Rating:** easy · fuel none · high confidence.

### 2. Damage after ending a turn on own spawn is invisible (F1)
- **Sites:** bot.cc:743, :682, :158-159.
- **Fix:** record my_max_health() when alive on own spawn (stunned turns too); same for reveal_ward's last_health.
- **Rating:** easy · fuel none · high confidence.
- **Caveat:** memory.damage consumers change, so A/B.

### 3. Lock spares an ally's winning tribute even when the enemy's lands first (A1)
- **Sites:** economy/bank.cc:88-94.
- **Fix:** spare only if turn_start(ally, ally.channel_end_round) < turn_start(e, e.channel_end_round). turn_start is in fight.cc:1024; expose it.
- **Rating:** easy · fuel none · high confidence.
- **Probe:** confirmed that our lock doesn't block our ally, so the fix stands.

### 4. Our Durability missing from the strike search's enemy blows (D1)
- **Sites:** combat/plan.cc:2215, 2250, 2276.
- **Fix:** multiply each `b` by world.me().taken.
- **Rating:** easy · fuel none · high confidence.
- **Caveat:** A/B.

### 4b. Skirmish missing from damage to unseen enemies; Collectors double-counted on camp spells (D2, D2b)
- **Sites:** plan.cc:1073, 1076, 1104, 1319; abilities.cc:300 (missing Skirmish). abilities.cc:535-536 (Collectors counted twice).
- **Fix:** on the unseen champion paths, multiply raw by skirmish_dealt when Skirmish is owned (add a skirmish-only accessor; not champion_dealt_factor()). On camp spells, divide Collectors out of camp_dealt_factor() (Monster only), and fix the comment.
- **Rating:** easy · fuel none · high confidence (probe).

### 5. Recall cancel not valued for basic attacks or landings (D4)
- **Sites:** plan.cc:1263; hit_unit 1370-1416 including splash; land_at 1479-1501; best_hit plan.cc:118; attack_target self_abilities.cc:425.
- **Fix:** read recall regardless of `casts`. Add params::recall_value on non-lethal damage off spawn, as spell_unit does.
- **Rating:** easy · fuel small · high confidence.
- **Caveat:** A/B.

### 6. Enemy tributes that win only together are not game-ending (A2)
- **Sites:** fight.cc:1107; abilities.cc:616; bank.cc:84.
- **Fix:** add the gems of the other visible enemy tributes landing no later.
- **Rating:** easy-medium · fuel none · medium-high confidence.

### 7. kill_turn counts basic attacks only (D5)
- **Sites:** fight.cc:613-621, feeding :643, :1124 and fixc.cc:66.
- **Fix:** on turn 0, add a ready reset hit and the best ready unit damage spell (raw × e.taken × champion_dealt_factor(), with in-range checks).
- **Rating:** easy-medium · fuel none · medium-high confidence.
- **Caveat:** changes preyable, so A/B.

### 8. No fuel guard before end_turn (H1)
- **Sites:** main.cc:365-369.
- **Fix:** if aph::fuel() < ~1000, spin `while (aph::round_now() == round) {}`, set late = false, then continue. This follows archives/river/main.htb:229-235.
- **Rating:** trivial · fuel none · medium confidence that it ever triggers · no risk.

### 9. Unseen enemy's next-round turn gets a strike with no walk (E2)
- **Sites:** model/safety.cc:1216-1229 (moved_strike), 1246-1249; turn_window.cc:95-121.
- **Fix:** in moved_strike, when turns < 2 and !enemy_may_act_after_us(world, c), return m.value, the full next-round strike.
- **Rating:** easy · fuel none · medium-high confidence.
- **Caveat:** A/B.

### 10. take_hop never retries after a hidden-blocker failure (G2)
- **Sites:** bank.cc:1053-1061.
- **Fix:** when use() fails, drop that landing and pick again (a few tries).
- **Rating:** easy · a host call per try · high confidence.

### 11. Camp damage ignores ally emblems (C4, C5: separate sites)
- **Sites and fixes:**
  - farm.cc:1072: damage × a.camp_taken.
  - safety.cc:2302, 2415, 2442: camp_taken in place of taken.
- **Rating:** easy · fuel none · high confidence · low impact.

### 12. Reset hit on camps picks the lowest-health member (C2)
- **Sites:** self_abilities.cc:500-513.
- **Fix:** rank by camp_attack_targets' score through a variant without the my_can_attack() gate. Keep Vendetta's sure-kill preference.
- **Rating:** easy-medium · fuel small · high confidence.

### 13. dash_to_attack may land without sight of the target (B2)
- **Sites:** self_abilities.cc:586-591.
- **Fix:** make the first rank key "within range and sees the target", using attack_viewers(world, at, range) or own-ward sight.
- **Rating:** easy · ~1 window read · medium-high confidence.

### 14. Dead allies keep a stale priority for 60+ rounds (H2)
- **Sites:** world.cc:2058-2065.
- **Fix:** once an ally is read dead, from the round after its first dead turn, set next_priority = packed_priority(dead_priority, index, 0) and mark ranks stale. Keep it after respawn until it is seen.
- **Rating:** easy-medium · fuel none · medium confidence.

### 15. Path walls go stale after a kill (G1)
- **Sites:** pathing.cc:698-706.
- **Fix:** invalidate blocked_ when a target dies (a kill counter checked in blocked()). Dead camps also need a camp belief re-read.
- **Rating:** easy · fuel small · high confidence.

### 16. Fully asleep groups woken by another champion are never priced (E4)
- **Sites:** world.cc:2143; safety.cc:2294-2343.
- **Fix:** in find_members, also scan alive, fully asleep groups near p that have an unacted champion within strike reach of a member.
- **Rating:** medium · fuel moderate · medium confidence.

### 17. Every visible ally blocks enemy walks and landings (E5)
- **Sites:** safety.cc:1853-1866, 1883.
- **Fix:** block only with allies that don't act before the enemy (the same timing as `fixed`).
- **Rating:** medium · fuel none · medium confidence.
- **Caveat:** makes the bot more conservative, so A/B.

### 18. finishing_camp sums solo clears (C1)
- **Sites:** camps.cc:830-842.
- **Fix:** sort groups by clear rounds; add each later group's per-round fire × the rounds elapsed before it.
- **Rating:** medium · fuel small · medium confidence.
- **Caveat:** A/B.

### 19. Stale own belief, redundant observe (H5)
- **Sites:** main.cc:327; world.h:641; world.cc:1858.
- **Fix:** compare `now` to sight_pos_. Fuel-only, so bench.py replays must stay identical. Fix the world.h comment too.
- **Rating:** easy · saves ~1.2k fuel on affected turns · high confidence.

### 20. Ren-retaliation suicide checks ignore our shield (B4, D6: one defect, distinct sites)
- **Sites:** abilities.cc:353, 567; plan.cc:107; plan.cc:1223, feeding 1078, 1100, 1358, 1399, 1422, 1452, 1497.
- **Fix:** use health + my_live_shield. In Search, add a separate field, because `health` also drives Heart of Ruin's damage_scale, which must not count shields.
- **Rating:** easy · fuel none · high confidence · very low impact.

### 21. Our Marcella's safety treats enemy stuns as threats (B3)
- **Fix:** clear .stuns and stun-only hits when self is stun-immune.
- **Rating:** easy · low impact.
