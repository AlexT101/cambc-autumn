Important experimental limits, not blockers:

- Exact-ray labels cover only that ray. Off-ray dash endpoints are unknown, and a connected result suppresses the old camp dash even if the new candidate scan finds none. This can lose a useful dash while retaining walking/emergency behavior; do not describe the scan as globally optimal.
- The49 bound covers memoized candidate endpoint evaluations only. Normal walk comparison, repeated home-field tie reads, fresh occupancy validation, and post-dash re-evaluation add cost. A movement can trigger another native search. No measured fuel saving is claimed.
- The retained reserve uses current-tile DPS times a connected distance heuristic. A detour through stronger camp overlap can cost more, and allies/enemies can change future aggro. Connected geometry is improved; future survival is not certified.
- `see_again` refreshes observations/Safety and native occupancy, not the entire camp lifecycle ledger. Known kills/wakes and unseen changes retain existing belief limitations; testing should separate these from path connectivity.

Focused production-body checks should cover open-ray equivalence, a hole-separated near goal, overlap beyond maxReach+1, a guard/temporary blocker change, unknown versus exhausted labels, and dash+walk after changed occupancy/new enemy. Confirm both movement and useful subsequent farming, not only a higher survival count.
