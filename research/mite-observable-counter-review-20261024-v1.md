## Evidence contract

Read `AGENTS.md`, `cambc-rules.yaml`, replay tooling documentation, and canonical Astraeus source. No earlier Mite-specific behavioral research was found in the inspected research Markdown.

First-pass extraction request: outcomes and lengths; each Mite champion's opening capture groups/times and first shop order; final earned/spent gold, camp damage done/taken, collected/submitted/lost gems, kills/deaths; team economy and bag/score checkpoints at 50/100/200/400; large-bag deaths; tribute/recall cancels; resets/flips/steals; priorities at relevant contacts. Then nominate at most four initial own-view windows, each at most ten rounds.

Global replay state establishes outcomes and nominates windows. `rounds.sees` establishes enemies seen at actor turn start; it does not expose current ally or camp knowledge and is not enough to certify an avoidable error. Own-view export must retain observation before the relevant action, current ability/action availability, visible ally/enemy positions, health/items/cooldowns, local camp state, map terrain, and subsequent actions. Public replays require explicit unknown source/build provenance. Existing exporter limits local camps/terrain to radius five; do not silently infer knowledge about a farther alternative from their absence.

An actionable error requires a legal, actor-known alternative and a specified near-term benefit: retained capture timer, survival from already visible threats, earlier conversion, or a superior available target. An unseen ambush is an opponent outplay unless available vision/ward information gave a concrete warning. A full-game loss, gold lead, high damage total, later-dead carrier, or omniscient empty camp is not itself a decision error.

## Rule-derived constraints (not observed Mite habits)

* Vesper and Vendetta have dash plus reset, but only one spell per turn. Vendetta's kills refresh both; static two-turn cooldown assumptions are unsafe around killable camps. Vesper gains 33% gold; deny an actually contested payout rather than presume equal returns from mirror farming.
* Ryth's ground stun excludes walking; ground range four plus radius one reaches five. Tavi's unit stun excludes walking and needs a visible target. Both can be punished after a verified cast only if the current geometry and other enemy turns permit it.
* Tavi buys shards anywhere. Opponent gold is unreadable; visible item/stat changes are the legal growth signal. A recalled-other-farmers window does not establish Tavi has stopped scaling.
* Marcella is stun-immune and earns gold by tributing. Damage does not cancel a tribute. A lock or kill can stop her conversion; a stun cannot. Locks cancel all tributes at the altar, including friendly ones, and require five basics to remove.
* Captures require six uninterrupted rounds on camp tiles; standing adjacent does not contest. Priority changes apply next round and alter equal-distance camp aggro. Apparent coordination must be reconstructed in exact actor order, not inferred from end-of-round clustering.
* Enemy draft is fully public from round one; map terrain/camps are public in draft. Team identity, hidden current enemy gold, unrevealed live positions/camps, and future replay state are inadmissible runtime conditions.

## Ranked responses and existing implementation

1. **First experimental direction: profitable phased mixed-boss work.** On a public mixed-resource map with a locally observed live valuable boss, nominate self or an actually visible capable local crew, use current HP and a legal exit, separate dangerous-member damage from harmless cleanup/capture, and compare the full conversion cycle against ordinary farm. No Mite identity or fixed round/group ID is allowed. The missing behavior is admission/economic comparison for live non-sealing mixed bosses, not capture-only support. The bounded design is `mite-phased-boss-source-design-20261024-v1.md`. Start with a one-visit phased clear; explicit heal-trip attrition is a separately priced second tier. The Mite loss gives a positive opponent witness and outcome-scale reward; it does not prove our current actors would pass admission or win. Require an activated route-choice witness before claiming performance.
2. **Preserve productive ordinary routes and safe contesting.** Big World g2/g3 harmless21-gem cycles and Corridors central58-gem control are map-conditioned examples, not hardcoded goals. Existing farm already prices public groups, ownership and danger. First inspect whether normal f7 selects accessible equivalent groups; add no special aggression rule if it does. The Corridors post-dash window supports punishing visible exposure while taking useful camp control, but supplies no our-actor counterfactual. A generic combat patch is lower priority than demonstrable missed economic work.
3. **Independent, lower-priority Marcella complete-cycle valuation.** `marcella-delayed-tribute-value-source-design-20261024-v1.md` specifies a narrow empty-bag, single-holder ordinary-gem experiment. Future tribute gold must be delayed until after capture, banking and a later shop; do not put it into the pre-tribute home-trip budget. It remains unimplemented and is not the explanation for the Big World loss.
4. **Do not prioritize proactive lock travel or counterdraft changes from this sample.** Local lock placement, visible tribute stuns, winning-carrier interception, Prism capture protection and gem retention already exist. No current selected window shows a profitable missed lock approach. Locks cost movement and five enemy basics to remove, cancel friendly tributes too, and Marcella cannot be stunned. No matched draft experiment supports replacing normal Astraeus picks; broad outcome differences remain opponent/map/source confounded.

The decision is a candidate experiment, not a claim that an isolated patch has been validated. All new work must retain normal Astraeus as the baseline and exclude the user's deliberate Willow-first ladder manipulation batches.

### Existing source details

A possible incremental seam, **pending replay evidence**, is a bounded move toward a visible non-winning tribute when a carried lock can cancel it profitably. Current ordinary lock placement acts only within range two; proactive lock-route goals occur for a winning enemy. The policy must compare lost own work, friendly channels at that altar, arrival before completion, and retreat/survival.
