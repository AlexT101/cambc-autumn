## Highest-value adverse hypothesis

All source references here are `bots/Khaos_v34/src/bot.cpp`. `want_home:2854` initializes the output before every early exit and preserves the original short circuits. `outmatched:2820` records the existing pack/payout result without adding a second safety forecast. Failed contextual keep leaves normal `home=false`; it does not leak a retreat flag.

The new branch at4176–4186 requires: held cleared capture, normal homefalse, actual pack suppression by `paying_soon`, arrival strictly before payout, non-winning own capture share, not currently threatened, no immediate safe tribute, and recall outrunning the recent enemies. Because `paying_soon:2810–2817` requires payout<=R+2 and an active recall has a positive integer timer, the normal new-policy case is exactly **timer1, payoutR+2**. It does not newly start a recall or change arbitrary farm-route selection.

**Plausible loss mechanism:** completing that safe recall may surrender almost-finished capture progress, gold and local support when the actor has an empty/small bag, adequate health, no valuable shop prefix and no teammate actually maintaining the hold. No new-condition term prices this lost payout against the actor's saved bag/recovery/shop benefit. This is a genuine generalization beyond the qualified large-bag witness. It is also a deliberate restoration of existing pack avoidance, not proof that staying would be safe. Enemy approach beyond the recall horizon, contesting and ally actions remain relevant; do not fix it by forcing a two-round hold.

Specific boundaries:

- **Capture/funding:** if it is the last holder, leaving atR+1 can reset the pendingR+2 payout. If another ally remains, team progress can survive while this actor loses its share; visible holder count and actual subsequent capture distinguish those cases. No payout-value or first-upgrade exception exists. `holding_capture:1495–1514` checks lower-ID holder capacity, not an acknowledged replacement commitment.
- **Bag scaling:** the branch has no minimum bag. Pack classification itself becomes more conservative for a large bag or low health (`pack_boxes:2660–2669`), so large-carry activation is intentional. A zero-bag case is possible when the actual enemy pack outmatches allied power, not whenever enemies are merely nearby.
- **Winning/race protection:** `capture_share < gems_short` excludes an own winning top-up including visible allied active tributes (`436–442`). `game_ender:3430–3453` precedes the keep, but protects against a visible interceptable enemy winning carrier; it is not a general economic-race veto. Allies' merely carried bags are included by `race_left:672–680`, not by this top-up classifier. A capture important to a coordinated later team finish can therefore still be abandoned. This is a candidate tradeoff, not a proven missed instant win.
- **Ally availability:** keeping a recall removes a body/heal/camp worker. The new condition checks enemy reach to self, not the teammate's resulting exposure. Ordinary claims/recall stubs do not guarantee an ally will cover the hold. No saved post-release example establishes such a harmed teammate.
- **Fuel:** scalar plumbing adds no native/Safety query; the rare new branch can execute previously skipped capture-share/self reads and existing safety tests. Code-layout effects on old fuel cutoffs remain possible elsewhere. The two exact controls and Big World B's exact prefix rule them out only in those cells. ## Minimal discriminating diagnostic

When one attributable normal-draft v48 loss becomes available, start with the earliest recall ending before a nearly due capture, not whole-match heal/fuel totals. Prefer a small/empty-bag actor with a valuable group. Read only the bounded interval around that candidate: source-known branch/payout/holder evidence, bag/HP/gold/shop opportunity, actual recent enemies and ally holders, then the group's reset/capture recipient. Existing `K` alone is insufficient because it also labels ordinary home keeps. If needed, one trusted-prefix scalar trace should expose the already-computed homefalse/pack-suppression bit, timer/payout and existing keep checks; no second Safety query is required.

A harmful-policy case requires an actual new-branch activation and a concretely lost conversion/support opportunity; later defeat alone is insufficient. If a first action fork occurs before any possible activation, investigate fuel/layout or source attribution instead. If the tested own prefix has no new keep, prioritize the existing macro/information weakness rather than rewriting recall. No source-only evidence here justifies an automatic rollback or new gameplay gate; the highest-value next distinction is **large carry saved versus low-benefit near-payout withdrawal**.

### Clarification: one turn left reflects a prior commitment

The timer1 case is generally a recall already kept over earlier activations, not the routine timer4 end-turn stub. The preceding pack detection may have correctly started/kept that retreat while the payout was farther away. Neither empty bag nor the final timer/payout pair alone proves the exit has low value. In the qualified Big World trace, the preceding r737 pack-driven keep is explicit. Completing an established safe retreat after only the payout clock changes is precisely the intended behavior.

The smallest persuasive adverse witness therefore includes the preceding one or two own decisions and actual known threat evolution. Establish why the recall was kept; whether health, carry/funding, enemy readiness/contact or alternate-holder availability materially changed; and what opportunity would be lost by completing it. For the competing hold, assess known attack/stun/contest possibilities through payout and the next available action, rather than calling a current unthreatened tile safe for two rounds. A group actually resetting or going to the enemy after departure is the outcome to explain, but by itself does not prove the earlier retreat unjustified. An isolated exact-parent comparison can distinguish losing capture opportunity from a parent that instead gets killed or loses the same objective. Unknown threats remain an uncertainty, not something to assume away. The new intake metadata may select such a witness; it cannot establish this causal chain.
