## Original Sol6.1 implementation proposal — withdrawn, do not implement

The justified isolated trial is a **farm endpoint continuation check**, not a movement-history penalty. Preserve the existing current-window guard and retreat logic. Apply only when the dispatcher chose ordinary farming and is about to take its first voluntary walk; exclude leaving, banking, held captures/contests, support movement and offensive planner terminals from this first arm. Do not alter route obstacles or map selection. The guard must be applied before the native move, not after consuming it.

1. Add a cheap source-known timing prefilter for the selected legal endpoint `q`, using current visible enemy facts and known next priorities. Compare current versus following-own-activation enemy opportunity counts and cooldown-ready strike bounds. Only a newly expanded threat at `q` warrants the extra query. Record `here/q`, triggering enemy index, distance, current/projected activation counts and reach. If priorities/order are not supportable from current facts, do not claim a certified next-window result.
2. For that one endpoint, query an explicit projected timing context at our next activation with **unchanged observed positions**, projected ability availability, self at `q`, and only presently visible eligible allies. Reuse existing Strike/Reach and trade machinery, but use separate bounded scratch/caches keyed by timing: do not mutate `World.round`, acted flags, current Reach memo, or current fighter/stand caches. Build fresh fighter copies with the appropriate first activation round; reusing `made_fighters` from the current turn without re-keying is incorrect. Account for current own cast/walk commitments and don't credit an action already spent. No fabricated ally relocation or future enemy trajectory.
3. Reject only a farm endpoint that the projected policy classifies as losing while the current standing position is not losing under the same projection; use the existing `lost` and linked/lethal fall policy rather than inventing a new damage threshold. This is a conservative stationary forecast, not a guarantee about future play. Preserve an already-useful current attack; rejection blocks the walk, not the entire turn. Make the rejected endpoint unavailable to the farm's remaining descent/search/fallback attempts this turn so they cannot immediately reselect it. Existing legal alternative movement or ending the turn remains available; never force an engagement or retreat into greater danger.
4. Evaluate at most one new endpoint forecast per actor turn, after cheap filtering, within the existing guard/fight reserve budget. Use the existing insufficient-budget fallback and diagnostic shortcut, not another global gameplay fuel gate. No full-board future guard, all-neighbor simulations, history table, map-name test or blanket reversal prohibition. If the existing Pathing plumbing cannot enforce one selected-endpoint check consistently across farm fallbacks, stop the implementation for review rather than silently placing it only on one caller.

This is more than a one-line change: a separate timing context is essential to avoid poisoning current safety caches. The narrow scope and scalar prefilter make it a reasonable experimental arm, not a reason to rewrite all safety. A minimal first implementation should expose the already computed reason/results in debug only. Required witness fields are current/projected enemy opportunity counts, ready mask/reach, endpoint and current-tile result, ally/enemy roster masks, own-ground/pursued mode, budget shortcut and selected caller. Do not add the earlier exhaustive roster dump.

## Capture horizon: separate, conservative boundary

The rules put captures before camp attacks and champion turns. A hold predicted to pay at r31 only needs to remain alive and uncontested through the remaining r30 turns to receive that payout; safety until the following own activation additionally includes any r31 enemies acting before us. These are distinct horizons. `paying_soon` currently checks nearest distance>3 and payout≤round+2, then disables pursuit/pack rejection; it does not override a losing `stand`. A multi-turn team fight loss is not immediate lethality.

Existing `Safety::lethal` is a conservative damage test before the next own turn, with actual turn-order-aware Reach, but does not certify continued uncontested occupation or a post-payout exit. `turns_to_kill_idle` is a stationary simulator over enemy-side turns, ignores important map/within-side ordering details, and its zero means not killed within the requested horizon—not safe indefinitely. Neither is a sufficient one-line exception to `t.lost`. A future finite-hold exception would need an actor-known no-contest check for every group tile through payout, damage/stun exposure through the next actionable turn, and a feasible exit with then-available movement/cast budget. A ready HoR dash can both hit and contest; a stun can remove the exit turn. Surviving one incoming hit is not enough.

The current Gold v2 record has680.4HP, enemy HoR dash becoming ready, and our supporting HoR moving from distance4 to5. Those facts make retreat plausible. Until a nonperturbing r30 trace certifies the actual original branch/timing, do not implement a capture force-hold, suppress ready-dash danger, or use the diagnostic win as evidence. The base farm correction can be tested independently.

### Cheaper bounds and future-context feasibility

A corrected scalar bound is sound as an **exclusion filter** under its fixed known-state assumptions: if it cannot reach q, an exact current Reach query for that enemy cannot add a hit. Conversely, being inside its radius proves only possible exposure. An upper-bound roster that loses does not prove the exact roster loses, and a positive damage ceiling is not a reason to forbid movement. Widening all ranged dash reach or vetoing every newly reachable endpoint would invent danger and can remove productive farming. A below-health worst-damage bound can rule out immediate lethality for that specified horizon; it cannot prove a sustainable fight, lack of stun, uncontested capture or an exit. No cheaper bound now justifies a gameplay correction to this witness.

If later needed, projected scalar Strike readiness can be computed with the existing explicit `strike(c, turn_round)` entry and a small pure opportunity-count function; a dedicated scratch `Reach::build(world,e,turn_round,twice)` already accepts timing inputs without changing World. Those pieces do not require a major Safety rewrite. But a **projected `stand` result** is not available by simply changing those inputs: fighter creation at `safety.cpp:655–674` uses current World timing, memo keys omit timing, and ally participation/readiness, own action budget, camp pressure, lethal policy and unseen threats remain current-context data. `Reach` also retains World geometry/occupancy assumptions. The previous proposal's full projected safety result is therefore materially more invasive than a cheap guard predicate. It is withdrawn rather than ported speculatively.
