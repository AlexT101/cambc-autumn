## Witnesses that distinguish mechanisms

1. **Ranged-clear ownership gap.** For each turn in 10–24, need own start/end position and actual ordered actions; walk/attack/cast flags, both cooldowns, channel/stun state; camp members/cleared tiles visible at each observation; route and blockers to a free tile; visible allies, their positions/claims/acted order; and whether Vesper ever occupied group1/2. A safe, reachable pickup displaced by an optional champion hit/chase nominates moving a narrowly qualified pickup before optional combat, preserving `want_home` and established holds. If the move was already spent or access pressured, this is not that witness.
2. **Deliberate safe HoR allocation versus a missed handoff.** Need previous-round live claim plus current claim/farm/crew, consecutive-turn memory and damage; full member visibility, holder and sole incumbent identity, incumbent channel/acted state; legal dash landings and readiness; visible/remembered threats. A ready, safe handoff refused by one overly narrow condition nominates relaxing that condition only. A Vesper own-view alone cannot prove HoR's claim/decision or source execution; retain unknowns. Do not strip HoR's hold remotely or force a gap for 33% more gold.
3. **Pressure-induced abandonment.** Need visible enemy positions, health/shields, ready slots, restrictions and acted order; remembered threat age/source separately from visible facts; own health, camp exposure, safe exits, holder estimate and remaining estimated hold time. A certified surviving stay/capture sequence with better local value is required before changing retreat. Combat begins18 and incoming19 globally; that does not establish which threats Vesper knew earlier, nor that waiting through23 was affordable.
4. **Known gold contest or route undervaluation.** Need actor-visible cleared/occupied group2, legal arrival before enemy payout, enemy readiness and surviving hold/reset sequence; plus own alternative job's actual accessible reward, wait, travel and expected item activation. Only then compare a bounded gold contest or commitment term with native farm. Do not infer unseen enemy gold, exact capture progress, a future payout owner, or an opportunity from global coordinates.

Capture is **occupancy, not an action channel**: standing on a camp tile uncontested pays after six rounds; even a mid-turn empty/contested interval resets it. Walking between tiles in the same group can preserve occupancy; leaving its last allied body cannot. Recall/teleport completion can vacate the tile before payout; stun alone does not vacate it. Native bots cannot read progress. `world.cpp:1758–1822` estimates holder/start only when all camp tiles are visible and conservatively redates possible handovers; record observation freshness and continuity rather than treating its estimate as engine truth. A recall may be kept to overlap payout (`bot.cpp:3331–3371`, `4129–4174`); it refuses arrival strictly before estimated payout. Check actual arrival/payout ordering before calling recall an abandonment.
