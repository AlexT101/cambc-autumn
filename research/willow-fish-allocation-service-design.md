## Concrete source mechanisms

- `bot.cpp:1732–1752,1940–2050,2266–2377`: another ally's claim converts a job into proportional crew value; remaining HP/arrival predict whether any share remains. An affordable solo job beats an inferior shared job. A visible claimant expected to fall below its recall line can trigger help, but ordinary helping is not a general team-throughput objective. Calling an unaffordable new job needs a nearby unclaimed partner; persistent different claims do not provide that bootstrap. This can separate workers among harmless jobs without enemy pressure. It does not prove that the saved live actors had those claims.
- `bot.cpp:833–843,1195–1208,1330–1351`: gold is valued through the next emblem's estimated saved work. Historical clearing participation, damage taken and spawn distance affect that saving. A worker doing little camp work during long travel has less modeled benefit from faster output. This is a possible feedback loop, not proof that cached prices were too low in this match; obtain the actual cached price/key before changing it. Willow's healing is absent from this item-output model. With only seven heals, assigning her a full cooldown's hypothetical service would substantially overstate observed demand.
- `bot.cpp:2168–2199`: empty-handed nonwinning captures omit eventual altar/tribute travel, assuming subsequent jobs share that conversion trip. `rate` additionally discounts long cycles and incumbent weight is1.2. These can favor repeated harmless jobs over slow damaging jobs. Group gold remains in the numerator: gold-only groups are not categorically excluded from the ranking loop, despite the older nearby comment. Ranged passing hits on unchosen gold-only groups are narrower (`passing`,1756–1761).
- `bot.cpp:4837–5072`: useful actual healing, safe stationary output and bounded formation can consume Willow's turn before ordinary farm evaluation. Support ownership clears Willow's public claim; Prism therefore must not count a service promise as farm output. Formation expiry prevents indefinite unproductive waiting, but does not assign the healer a productive economic job on a remote map. Strict station safety can also exclude basic-attack stations for range4 monsters; healing is not permission to enter camp fire.
- `cambc-rules.yaml:88`: actual gems/gold go to holders at payout. A crew's proportional reward in farm value is an allocation proxy, not a personal-gold guarantee. Reassigning a damage worker to a cleared-camp holder job or a helper to a damage job must preserve both ownership and actual conversion; damage totals alone do not measure capital gained.

## Precisely when banking is obligatory

`banks_bag = bankable_bag > 0 && !retaining_gems` changes farm valuation; it does **not** itself force all non-Prism carriers to bank. Only Prism retains a nonwinning, nonclosing bag (`685–688`). Altar-unusable bags are treated as unbankable (`625–628`).

For an ordinary carrier off an altar, `carry_bag:4224–4270` can chain when it is not closing, has not spent a ranged basic, has less than30 gems at stake, has no winning bag or credible threat, and chained farm beats the best cycle starting from an altar. Farm explicitly subtracts `bank_now` and adds the candidate's bank/tribute cost in that comparison. Willow's own economy block (`4618–4620`) deliberately leaves this small safe off-altar case to that same decision. At a safe current tribute position it banks immediately; unsafe current altar waiting has separate behavior. No claim that every14-gem capture must immediately bank is justified.

There is a separate timing shortcut: `capture_recall:3306–3346`, while holding a capture, compares recall-to-bank with walk-to-bank, and sets banking when recall wins. It does not compare chaining another job. `continue_recall:4121–4132` keeps the resulting ride. This is a bank-route optimization conditional on choosing conversion, used before the post-payout chained comparison. After landing, banking is still reevaluated; `memory.banking` alone is not an irrevocable tribute order. The observed Willow345 spawn arrival is compatible with this shortcut, but the live branch and alternative chained jobs remain unproved.

Therefore a correction must not add a bank trip to every empty-bag job. A conservative first pricing experiment could charge conversion only for a predicted bankable nonretained payout already forced by the known policy (for example the resulting at-stake bag reaches30, or wins; winning captures already include conversion). This will not explain the14-gem BigWorld repetition by itself. For the smaller-bag capture-recall shortcut, first establish whether a viable chain was bypassed; then choose between a conversion-plan quote and a local defer-recall experiment. Do not silently treat recall cheaper than walking as proof that banking beats chaining.

Reuse the existing `known.to_altar`, `HomeTrip::rounds(walk, capture_overlap, gold_in)`, `chained_tribute`, `bank_now` and `from_altar_value` terms. The target is one capture-to-conversion cost, with capture overlap applied once and chained bank baseline subtracted once. Do not add another raw recall, health recovery or emblem credit on top of `HomeTrip`. Unknown future altar safety, injured recipients or ally DPS cannot certify the trip. Actual recall/tribute guards remain authoritative. Existing forecast-formation changes only the value of an already selected formation move and cannot repair this capture pricing/ownership issue by itself.

## Bounded combined direction, contingent on the controls

First compare latest teacher-draft control against unchanged Willow with the same frozen roster emulator. If restoring the damage fourth markedly improves damaging gold-camp access and early team capital while useful healing remains sparse, roster opportunity cost is the leading explanation; do not compensate by loosening Willow safety.

If Willow is retained, the coherent subsequent direction is **job-linked service plus consistent conversion accounting**. Consider one already visible, actually working claimant and Willow's existing safe9 stations. Price only current real heal gain, and any safely available current basic that shortens the observed clear; compare that finite service with Willow's own currently admissible solo job. Preserve claimant ownership and each worker's independent safety. No remote rendezvous, future-heal HP reserve, speculative tanking, or blanket shared-claim unlock. Helping for a proportional personal reward alone is insufficient when the benefit is avoiding the claimant's actual recovery trip. Conversely a zero-demand service job should lose to productive solo work rather than receive a permanent support subsidy. If no safe service station or real injury exists, this arm must decline. This is a design boundary, not a claim that the existing own-view contains such an opportunity.

The economic half should quote only a conversion the policy actually intends; it should not force every small bag to bank. Evaluate the pricing and local-service pieces separately before combining them, so improved roster output cannot be mistaken for better healing or vice versa. Do not change gold thresholds, manufacture a specific map exception or rewrite generic claims without the witness below.
