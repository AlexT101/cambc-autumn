# After one Focus emblem: price service separately

Source-only design,2026-10-24 UTC. Read Cambridge Battlecode rules, canonical4db shopping/support source, item-eight-cell diagnostics in EXPERIMENTS and the fresh incoming-loss review. No code, variant, script, build or runtime. The9b compound full test is still pending in this task's evidence; no result is assumed. New upstream Astraeus requires separate reconciliation before implementation.

**Recommendation:** do not turn healing into camp DPS or make a second Focus emblem automatic. First measure marginal usable service at existing heal decisions. If that exposes a real shopping ranking change, add a separate estimate of ally recovery rounds saved to the existing own-upkeep score. Keep Health/mitigation's value in Willow's own recovery model; do not grant them fictitious healing output or future allied work.

## What the current state can and cannot price

`bot.cpp:113` excludes healing from `main_stat`; Willow remains Strength. `raw_output:833` models basic/reset damage only and must stay that way. `Upkeep:1109` records alive turns, camp-work turns, net own injury attributed to camps/champions and average spawn distance. `upkeep_cost:1195` prices own clearing and fractional recall/return trips. It captures some provider exposure and independent-work duty, but not effective ally heals, service detours/waits, lost resets, recipient work resumed or completed ally trips avoided. Its net-health-difference input is not a complete damage-event ledger, and its mean injury model does not certify burst survival.

`local_support:4931` already prices **Prism's** recovery as gain/usableHP × (recall + outward distance), then compares detour/wait. Willow cannot read that private instance's calculation. Comms carries dated Prism-request/Willow-offer bits, claims and coarse bags; neither a request nor a claim proves deficit, completed service, future work or avoided recall. Existing visible records supply current HP/maxHP/items/channels, but no unseen ally gold, route intent or full recovery history. A committed recall is deliberately excluded. `heal_ally`, local support, bank-heal, independent-heal and combat search all have healing decision paths: sampling only one would bias demand downward.

The independent-heal step currently compares gain with three basic attacks (`bot.cpp:4806`); that is a local heuristic, not a sound unit conversion for shopping. Reusing it would price HP as damage. The new utility must not alter that movement policy in the first shopping experiment.

## Minimal conditional estimate

For the same observed eligible recipient with deficit D and candidate Focus F, effective heal is `min(D,3F)` **only if the existing worth_healing predicate accepts it**. Preserve live visibility/range, native cast readiness, channel exclusion and target ordering. A Focus alternative may change the selected target; replay that small existing candidate comparison, not an invented best possible recipient.

A completed Bruiser Focus versus Bruiser Strength adds60 nominal heal capacity, loses12 Strength, and has equal50HP/150gold. For an accepted same-target cast the extra absorbed amount is at most `min(60,max(0,D-oldCap))`. But the ordinary60%-of-cap acceptance floor also rises36HP: with recipient fraction≥50%, a formerly accepted deficit can become rejected. Count that as a negative service difference. All deficit-limited heals unchanged by the cap contribute zero, not60. Health adds no direct service credit; Willow cannot self-heal. Effects and prices must be recomputed for actual affordable shards and cross-stat completion, not granted from the first shard.

Convert incremental absorbed HP into **estimated recipient recovery rounds**, using the already-employed local-support convention: recipient recall duration plus existing cached spawn distance, divided by recipient usableHP. Missing/stale route or visible stat data means no trip credit. This is an explicit fractional-trip model, not proof a recall was prevented; shops, banking, combat escape, death and changed destinations can make its forecast wrong. Do not price retained gems as guaranteed saved bags or assume a healed Heart of Ruin retains its former missing-health damage bonus.

At a fixed observed service schedule, the candidate's per-alive-turn score is existing own `base-upkeep_cost(candidate)` plus incremental estimated ally recovery rounds divided by the **same exposure interval**. No raw healing enters camp output, crew admission or safety. Equal-valued actor rounds is a simplifying economic assumption; actual reward conversion must validate it. The own-upkeep term already charges the Strength loss and values Health/mitigation. Do not charge all existing support travel twice: only a changed schedule has marginal travel/wait/reset cost. The first estimate assumes the schedule is unchanged and makes no positive claim about service newly created by safer movement or longer life. Extra casts replacing a reset need explicit opportunity-cost accounting or exclusion; a successful existing heal cannot retroactively be counted as an additional cast.

Minimal added evidence is a local bounded service profile from already-read ready-heal candidate records: deficits/fractions, accepted target and actual success, current cap, native readiness, available cached trip weight, plus observed provider movement/wait/reset consumption. Count a turn once despite repeated helper calls. A successful cast alone misses cap-induced rejection, so preserve negative/no-cast opportunities too. Use a natural inventory/stat epoch and invalidate across unknown turn gaps; do not transplant old demand as exact current demand. No new shared-memory protocol, all-map search, native read loop or fuel gate is warranted.

Shopping consistency is mandatory: `best_emblem`, `affordable_prefix` and `prices` must consume the same frozen profile and actual Build. `prices:1330` currently keys share/stats/main/home/monster, **not service state**. Introduce an explicit profile revision at a bounded refresh boundary, rather than silently stale quotes or rebuilding farm values after every heal. Freeze the profile across one shop loop while applying `bought` and exact partial-item effects. A design that cannot make these dependencies explicit is not ready to implement.

## Two bounded experiments

1. **Quote-only service diagnostic after the fixed first Focus.** On source-known activation cells, compare the actual next emblem with a hypothetical second Bruiser Focus using the same native-ready opportunities; log own-cost difference, positive cap use, suppressed heals, target changes, estimated trip saving and missing data separately. Preserve decisions. Include the eight-cell cases with healing decreases and increased recalls, and a productive independent-Willow control. Three deficit-limited Archipelago heals and Starvation's large healing total are useful zero-benefit counterexamples; the public losses are nomination data, not source-known activation witnesses. If no positive ranking change is observed, stop rather than test a nonactivating variant broadly.
2. **Only after such a witness, one isolated service-aware shopping arm.** Keep first-Focus behavior, existing partial completion, Treasure/protection ordering and support/movement decisions. Add fresh Bruiser Focus as a candidate with the separate service term; preserve ordinary tie order and all existing Health/mitigation alternatives. No map/team names, blanket Focus/Health, permanent following or tuned new cutoff. Compare against the frozen same compound source, not merely v33, both sides with fixed drafts/opponent. Verify actual purchases and exact prefix pricing first; then assess team collected/submitted gems, recipient continued work/completed recalls, provider deaths/own output, and fuel/native status. Only a paired outcome can establish value beyond the frozen-schedule estimate.

Treasure is outside these experiments: after the first fixed Focus it is already owned. Its additive extra0.2 base gold is not Vesper-exclusive. Low-income Starvation Willow and productive Expanse Willow argue for measuring recipient income, not removing Treasure by role; current evidence does not justify another simultaneous economy-policy change.

**Falsification:** reject the extra-Focus utility if cap-binding accepted demand is absent, higher caps suppress as much useful service as they add, predicted trip savings do not produce continued recipient work/fewer completed trips, or lost Willow captures/survival outweigh conversion. Also reject a model that changes quotes without buying the intended item, cannot maintain cache/profile consistency, or only wins the selected activation cells. Health reducing deaths without better team conversion does not validate a service-value model. The initial diagnostic may show that access/routing, not shopping, remains the binding problem.
