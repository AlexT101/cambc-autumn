## Corrections

1. **Own boss damage rejects the intended work and exit.** `phased_boss.inc:112–114` calls `!safety.threatened(p)` inside `boss_enemy_safe`. Canonical `safety.cpp:2098` immediately returns threatened when `camp_damage(p,true,true)>0`. Consequently ordinary awake boss contact rejects certification (`:307–309`), certified exit (`:408`), and exposed return (`:467–468`). Use an enemy-only threat predicate here and retain the separate nominated-boss health reserve and other-camp exclusions. Do not change global Safety semantics. Sent to Sol and accepted for v2.

2. **Return cooldowns can be ready one activation too early in the forecast.** Remote return ages current cooldowns by `walk + entry_steps` (`:491`), and corridor execution by `local` (`:524`), then forecasts a moved first work activation which can basic/reset. Marcella legally moves and basics/resets on the final entry activation: N moves starting now reach and work at current round + N − 1. The native remaining-cooldown convention (`world.cpp:fire_round`, rules turn end/cooldown) therefore permits only N − 1 elapsed ticks before that joint entry/work activation. Price and execute the same schedule; either use that offset or explicitly execute and price a separate waiting activation. Unit Damage remains movement-exclusive. Sol accepted the N − 1 correction. The stationary `boss_sortie` eight-round recurrence and one-cast choice are otherwise consistent with the kit.

3. **An active job can spend its basic before its forecast/executor.** At `bot.cpp:1895`, ordinary `attack_in_range()` runs whenever the generic low-health restriction is false. Marcella's melee branch often returns false after successfully attacking, so final phased comparison still happens with the basic already spent; its reset policy then declines the reset because it requires its own basic first. A passing hit can also take an early capture return. Suppress the generic passing path for an active eligible phased work job until it loses selection, without suppressing ordinary farm for unrelated champions or new nonselected jobs. Parent independently identified this seam; verified here.

4. **The cache key does not cover its certificate inputs.** `boss_token_current` (`:43–47`) checks round, observation count, camp-hit count and position. Recall cancellation changes action availability; same-position guard installation and action/stat/inventory changes need not increment those fields. Executor native action reads prevent an illegal cast but do not repair an earlier affordability/waiver forecast. Invalidate or fingerprint the relevant inputs before reuse, especially after cancellation and before final selection. A pre-cancellation forecast may merely be pessimistic, but it is still not the claimed same-state certificate. Parent owns the concrete v2 fingerprint request.

5. **Reconcile the persistent cycle on both closed and local-return paths.** V1 releases its tombstone only when local `read_boss` sees group state 3 (`:368–372`). Missing that interval and later seeing a new full-health live group leaves every other boss suppressed indefinitely (`:353`), despite camps never regenerating. Preserve the one-trip prohibition for the unfinished cycle, but release on positive fresh evidence of a different cycle, such as an exact HP rise over retained post-work HP. Also apply cycle reconciliation at the local return/work handoff: the remote path checks `expires`, whereas `boss_candidate` can take over near the boss without that check. Fresh HP proves current health; by itself it does not prove that this remains the previous cycle. Do not treat elapsed earliest-reset time alone as proof of respawn or authorization for another trip. Sent to Sol.

## What is already sound, and what remains qualification

The local descriptor requires native lifecycle/live records and resolves not-found with a visible dead-state proof; unknown visibility declines. Only one static damaging member and at most sixteen total members qualify. The implementation excludes credited allied output, future items/heals, shields and sleeping/reset-stun savings. At a stationary work activation, requiring HP above `hit × corridor + 1` before a surviving boss's next phase reserves its hit plus the exposed exit endpoints; the final killing action needs no subsequent hit from that dead member. Second-sortie HP deducts entry exposure and requires a finish. This is conservative local arithmetic, not evidence that an actual map/stats state wins the farm comparison.

The remote HP cutoff correctly uses **last live observation round + `constants::capture_time` + respawn**, with strict-before validity. It does not use `capture_rounds()`'s extra one. Keep that distinction in v2. Per-turn refresh after own unit-target damage is compatible with Marcella's absence of within-turn allied actions; newly observed movement still needs full local recertification.

The below-35% path is structurally confined to an active certificate and restricted farm, with canonical retreat restored when the job loses. The unrelated low-health farm fallback occurs after that restoration; it does not inherit the waiver. Enemy/game-ending/bank priorities still matter. Correcting the enemy-only test must retain those priorities and exact exit survival.

The two targeted fields are explicitly cold work over public static layout; they are not remote native camp scans or a guarantee of future dynamic occupancy. Cached fields plus actual blocked/guard descent and fresh local arrival checks are appropriate within the agreed travel model. The raw other-camp range exclusion is deliberately conservative. Altar eligibility, holder/Prism/claim checks, capture and tribute are present; future normal banking is estimated rather than forced by the job.

No new fuel gate was introduced. Nonetheless per-job member imports/viewer work, bounded local tile checks, two cold native fields and up to two 128-activation forecasts are real work. `prepare_boss_routes` still constructs/combines an altar-goal bitboard on every warm call before discovering that its key is unchanged. Unconditional `BossPlan` locals in generic decide/farm paths also deserve cost measurement or cheap type-scoped construction; no fuel improvement or regression is asserted without compiled evidence. Prefer exact-result-preserving warm-cache work reduction over a new gate.

One preliminary declaration concern was checked and **retracted**: immutable v1 already forward-declares `capture_taken`, `see_again`, and `retreat` at `phased_boss.inc:4–6`. No declaration change is needed on that account.

Next review should target v2's frozen delta for the above corrections, followed by the authorized owner's focused build/mechanism checks. A win-rate panel of a nonactivating or internally inconsistent arm would not establish this mechanism.
