## Limits to retain in the experiment

The model assigns equal value to provider and recipient recovery rounds and extrapolates a fixed observed cast/movement schedule. A copied deficit is not a proven avoided recall, survival event or future capture. Planner heals remain uncovered; whole-snapshot missing-data exclusion can leave a sparse profile. Those are disclosed experimental assumptions, not source blockers or a reason to demand a favorable runtime witness before testing.

There is real new cost: an initialized16-recipient local scan, staged/ring/profile copies, bookkeeping and at most8×16 scalar comparisons per distinct candidate Focus.
