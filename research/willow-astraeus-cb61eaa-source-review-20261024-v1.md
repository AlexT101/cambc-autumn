## Cost and cache changes

`stations()` now publishes its observation/position key only after all nine tiles and the final stale check complete (`bot.cpp:4358–4377`); a thrown partial build cannot be reused as complete. `exposure_here` reuses a completed station or computes one tile after movement/end-of-turn (`4382–4395`). Willow's deferred guard returns an **empty board**, not null, when no credible enemy can hit any adjacent tile (`3080–3092`), retaining the refreshable guard contract without building camp-only station rows. The broad phase includes tracked unseen enemies, rather than equating “no visible enemy” with safety (`safety.cpp:1991–2005`, unchanged).

The earlier unconditional camp-damage computation is avoided when the full Safety bound will replace it (`4338–4352`); the separate conservative camp-exposure boolean remains. Cheap readiness/target/work filters move before station/exposure calls, and safety still filters candidates before selection. These are cost reductions, not new fuel gates or reduced safety thresholds.

`comms.cpp:79–101` caches roster endpoint indices and limits support-mailbox decoding to Prism/Willow. World initializes the complete fixed ally vector before receiving comms (`world.cpp:947–1039`); pointers are reconstructed from indices rather than retained across allocations. Removed memory fields were write-only in this source. Removing `observed_`, `camps_current` and `group_in_view` removes unused interfaces; it does **not** change camp observation semantics: `World::observe()` still updates vision/champions without `update_camps()`.
