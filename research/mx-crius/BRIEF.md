## The task
Improve charybdis's *macro*: how it approaches the game strategically. That includes:
- target selection for farms, camps and monsters;
- explicit or implicit coordination with ally champions;
- adversarial planning around enemy champions, seen or predicted behaviour and playstyles;
- movement, zone control, cohesion versus dispersion with allies;
- economy: gold and gems, when champions tribute, shop or spend, how they prioritise each over the game and by map;
- risk versus reward, protecting allies, enemy threats, how the enemy thinks;
- positioning relative to map structures, chokepoints, champions, camps and monsters;
- the balance between farming and combat, and how the bot prioritises or combines them;
- which kinds of camps and locations the bot prefers;
- how it thinks about combat at the large scale: team fights, retreat and chase, cornering.

Our bot is already strong but still loses many games, largely through poor coordination and map control. Earlier attempts included champions "summoning" allies to a camp, and moving as a pack. They failed because champions were busy, wasted turns walking to each other, or chose not to listen: the concepts were too rigid. Build something versatile instead. Treat this as a refactor of the macro, not a feature bolted on. Nothing in the current implementation is assumed optimal. The comms (8 shared bytes), the draft-to-play bytes, the planners, the safety model and the crew logic can all be extended, repurposed or replaced.

**Off limits:** draft choices (`draft/`, which champions are picked) and emblem choices (which emblem a champion builds toward). Everything else is yours, including when champions go shopping, tribute, recall or spend.

**Goal:** a bot that wins 60%+ against `chy-ref` on 10k+ fresh games at 2x fuel. This is achievable: a teacher bot that we don't yet have access to beats current charybdis 65%+ through better macro alone (farming, gold and gems, champion movement and coordination). An initial failure is not proof that an idea won't work.
