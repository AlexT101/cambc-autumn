THE PROBES

Prism engagement. Measured in mirror games: when one Prism has 1.25-2x the other's Focus and they are within 6 tiles,
the stronger kills the weaker within 60 rounds 12.7% of the time and dies 4.8% of the time. At 0.8-1.25x it is 7%
each way.
1. pb2-hunt: while our Prism's Focus is >= 1.25x every living enemy Prism we have seen (same Focus reading as
   prism_dominant in economy/capture.cc, but threshold 1.25), and the enemy Prism was seen within the last ~10
   rounds, our Prism walks towards its last seen tile instead of farming, and engages it through the usual fight
   code. Don't do this while we are channelling, holding a winning bag, or hurt under hurt_health_fraction.
2. pb2-avoid: our Prism treats every visible or recently seen enemy Prism with Focus >= 0.8x ours as off limits
   within (its ready stun reach + 2) for farm walks and targets. Keep out of that zone unless we are already
   fighting. Use a Safety/threat avoidance hook if one exists, otherwise exclude groups and tiles in that zone from
   farm choice.

Prism funnel (the carry macro, economy/capture.cc: leave_for_prism, prism_dominant, carry_reach):
3. pb2-funnel-max: prism_dominant always returns false, so allies keep feeding even a dominant Prism, and
   carry_reach() is doubled.
4. pb2-funnel-off: leave_for_prism always returns Leave::no, and the Prism never takes over an ally's hold.

Game-ender denial (bot.cc: game_ender -> holds_hard_stop -> stop_game_ender in combat/fight.cc):
5. pb2-deny-off: never walk to stop a game ender. Act only when the stop is possible from where we already stand
   this turn. Otherwise play on normally, as if there were no ender.
6. pb2-deny-wide: enemy tributes that would not win count as enders too, once their bag is >= 1/3 of what the enemy
   still needs to reach 1000. Same stop path.

Cooked comebacks (economy/comeback.cc, params::comeback):
7. pb2-comeback-off: cooked() always returns false.
8. pb2-comeback-wide: cooked once the enemy leads by >= 50 with >= 200 tributed (off below a 25 lead).

Prism hold under threat (economy/capture.cc: prism_hold_threatened):
9. pb2-hold-bold: prism_hold_threatened always false.
10. pb2-hold-shy: prism_hold_threatened true whenever any enemy champion is visible.
