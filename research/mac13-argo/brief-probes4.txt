Build 4 conversion probes of the charybdis bot. These are lever probes, so a crude but correct change is fine.

Setup:

- For each probe, run `./copybot variants/chy-ref variants/pb4-NAME` and edit only its src/.

WHY. These are mirror games of chy-ref against itself.
- Once the eventual winner first reaches 1000 wealth (tributed + carried), the finishing phase takes a median 29
  rounds but a mean of 124, and the bag holder is channelling 71% of that time.
- Completion by starting bag:
  | starting bag | completed |
  |---|---|
  | 100-199 | 65% |
  | 300-499 | 32% |
  | 500-799 | 21% |
  | 800+ | 5% |
- The winner's Prism starts 4.7 bags of 500+ a game, and 11% of them complete. Stops: stun 45%, self 23%, lock 20%.
- Under a per-turn stop hazard h, a channel of L turns with restarts takes ((1-h)^-L - 1)/h rounds, which grows
  exponentially in L. So smaller final bags convert much faster.
- A variant that waited for safe windows before long channels lost 4.4 points. It slowed conversion by 77 rounds
  and completed no more channels, so conversion SPEED is a lever.
- Today the Prism keeps its whole bag for Focus (economy/bank.cc keeps_gems) until winning_bag() or closing(). So
  its last channel is ~550-900 gems, 55-90 turns.

PROBES
1. pb4-chunk300: once our team wealth (score.ally + our bodies' bags, as bank.cc reads them: race_left_now and
   gems_short have the pieces) is >= 700, the Prism stops keeping gems whenever its bag is >= 300. It banks through
   the normal bank path (banks_bag / bankable_bag / tribute_ok), as any non-Prism body would.
2. pb4-chunk150: the same with a bag >= 150, from team wealth >= 600.
3. pb4-eager: a winning-bag channel (winning_bag() true) of any length is started and kept at any risk, as any_risk()
   already allows for short ones and for the round limit. The only exception: drop it if we would be killed before
   it ends, keeping tribute_ok's kill test for the turns left as in CHANGES 525. It is also started at the nearest
   reachable altar as soon as we reach one.
4. pb4-eager-rich: pb4-eager, plus: once the team's wealth is >= 1000, every non-Prism body with >= 30 gems banks
   at once (bag floor ignored), so its gems land in short parallel channels.
