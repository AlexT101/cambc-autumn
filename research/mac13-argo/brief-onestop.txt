TASK: build "onestop", a defence-economy design for the charybdis bot. Against an enemy's game-ending channel the
team sends ONE stopper, not every body holding a stop, and the rest keep farming.

EVIDENCE (mirror games of chy-ref against itself)
- While the eventual winner holds a winning bag and converts, the loser's capture gems fall from 136 to 65 per 100
  rounds, and its deaths rise from 1.0 to 3.2 per 100 rounds.
- Today bot.cc runs game_ender -> holds_hard_stop -> stop_game_ender (combat/fight.cc) for EVERY body that holds a
  hard stop (a ready stun or a carried lock). Each one walks in "whatever the risk", and 66% of stunners die within
  15 rounds.
- A probe that never walked to stop at all (only stops from where a body already stands) lost just 1.4 points.
- In one long game the loser made 2 captures in 1,200 rounds, its whole team cycling into the winner's Prism.
- So both extremes are close; the middle should beat them.

DESIGN
1. Designated stopper.
   - For each enemy game-ending channel we track (game_ender's choice), every body computes the same ranking of
     our bodies by the moment its stop could land: walk steps to its stun reach (or to the altar's lock range with
     a lock), plus the wait for its stun cooldown, converted with turn_start/Moment as ally_stops does.
   - Use what every body can know: visible allies, and posted positions from comms for unseen ones. Break ties by
     index.
   - The first is the designated stopper; it runs today's stop_game_ender.
2. Backup.
   - A second body also goes only if the designated stopper cannot land before the channel's end, with a spare turn
     (dead, stunned, cooldown too long, path too long), or if the designated one is the only stop and the channel
     ends within (its walk + a spare turn).
   - Otherwise no backup goes.
3. Everyone else plays as if no ender were there: farm, fight as priced, bank. This also applies to the bodies with
   no hard stop, which today mostly fight as priced; keep that.
4. A body already in reach with a ready stun, at no extra walk, still stops: the stun is free. Keep that path.
   Locks follow the same designation.
5. Leave untouched: our own tribute logic, stun_worth casts in normal fights, the walking-ender (not yet channelling)
   path, and Marcella.
