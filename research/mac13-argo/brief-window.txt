EVIDENCE (mirror games of chy-ref against itself, 2,560 maps)
- Tribute completion by starting bag:
  | starting bag | completed |
  |---|---|
  | 1-29 | 96% |
  | 100-199 | 65% |
  | 200-299 | 39% |
  | 300-499 | 32% |
  | 500-799 | 21% |
  | 800+ | 5% |
- There are 3.5 starts of 800+ a game, and only 17% of them finish even with no enemy within 8 at the start.
- When a team first reaches 1000 wealth (tributed + carried), its Prism carries a median 562 and the team has
  tributed 345.
- The winner then takes a median 56 rounds, and a mean of ~170, to convert. 11.6% of games are lost by the team
  that reached 1000 wealth first.
- Winning-bag channels are cancelled after a median 6 rounds: stun 45%, self 23%, lock 20%. The stunners are
  mostly Prism, Ryth and Cassi.
- Defenders who come to stun die 66% of the time within 15 rounds. Respawn takes 60 rounds, so a stopper's death
  opens a long window.

WHY THE CURRENT CODE STARTS HOPELESS CHANNELS
- economy/bank.cc tribute_safe/tribute_ok only ask whether we would be killed within params::tribute_window turns.
  They never ask whether a stopper reaches us before a channel of ceil(bag/10) turns ends.
- keeps_gems makes the Prism keep its whole bag (for Focus) until winning_bag() or closing(), so its last bag is
  huge and its channel very long.

DESIGN
1. Stop window, W(altar). The rounds until any enemy could stop a channel at that altar, as a lower bound from what
   we know:
   - a living enemy with a stun ability: its steps to within its stun reach of our tribute tile;
   - any living enemy: its steps to within lock range (2) of the altar, since locks can't be seen in a bag;
   - an unseen enemy: count from its last known tile, less the rounds since we saw it (the existing unseen-enemy
     beliefs may already do this);
   - a dead enemy: its respawn round plus its steps from its spawn.
   Use the existing pathing and enemy beliefs, and cache per round. Watch fuel: an estimate as the crow flies
   (Chebyshev) is acceptable where fields are too dear.
2. Start rule for a long channel (L = tribute_turns(bag) > params::tribute_window):
   - Start only at an altar where L <= W, keeping one turn of margin.
   - Prefer the altar with the most W - L among those reachable now, counting the walk to it in both L's start time
     and W.
   - If no altar fits, don't start. Keep farming or fighting as chy-ref would, and re-check every turn: W grows when
     stoppers die or leave.
   - Fallbacks, so we never lose by waiting:
     - Endgame by rounds: rounds_left <= L + slack keeps today's any-risk rule.
     - The enemy will win first unless we start now (enemy_tributes_win / their wealth race).
     - We have waited more than L rounds since we first held the winning bag. Then today's rule applies.
3. Rich-team chunking. Once our team wealth (score + carried) >= 1000, but before any single bag wins:
   - the Prism stops keeping gems (keeps_gems false) whenever a fitting window exists at a reachable altar for its
     current bag;
   - so it banks chunks while the window allows, and the final channel is short.
   Reason: once rich, Focus buys less than conversion, and every completed chunk shortens the last channel.
4. A fitting channel already running is not self-cancelled for threats that can't stop it before it ends (W > turns
   left). Kill threats are still respected: keep tribute_safe's kill test for the turns that remain.
5. Leave untouched: game_ender/stop_game_ender (defence), farm valuation, crews, fights, funnel/carry logic before
   the team is rich, and everything for non-Prism bodies except that the start rule (2) applies to any long channel.
