DESIGN "defer": price a shopping trip by the time it buys, not by the rest of the game.

Setup (same rules as research/mac13-argo/brief-probes5.txt: read AGENTS.md; `./copybot variants/chy-ref
variants/defer`; edit only its src/; write the patch with `diff -ru bots/variants/chy-ref/src bots/variants/defer/src
> research/mac13-argo/patches/defer.diff`; never edit chy-ref or onestop-a; draft, picks and emblem choices
untouched; commit nothing). Comment only counterintuitive reasoning, concisely, in the code's own style.

WHY. On a fresh 5,120-game suite, scaling the shopping-trip price showed a slope:
- params::shop_gain_scale x0.5 scored 51.6% [50.2, 52.9] against chy-ref;
- x2 scored 49.5%.
A healthy Prism shop trip costs a median 19 rounds, and it makes ~3 of them in r1-400. Fewer trips win.

The mechanism we believe is mispriced is wants_shop (economy/shop.cc). Its value is
shop_gain_scale x affordable_prefix(world).gain (+ the heal), and the gain is the purchase's per-round saving times
`rounds` = gems_share x rounds_per_gem, all our remaining farm. But we will be home anyway before long:
- heal recalls, bank trips by way of spawn, deaths and respawns;
- the shop() call in bot.cc buys on spawn whenever we are there.
So a trip made NOW only buys the saving for the rounds until our next home visit we would make anyway. After that
visit, we would own the items regardless.

DESIGN
- Keep, per champion, a running measure of how often it is on its own spawn for reasons other than a healthy shop
  trip: the mean interval between arrivals on spawn so far this game (respawns and recall arrivals both count),
  starting from a prior.
  - Prior: the measured mirror figures are, per 400 rounds, about 5 completed recalls for the Prism and 7 for Vesper,
    plus deaths. Derive one prior interval from this, or from the rules / existing bot quantities if that is cleaner,
    and say how you derived it.
  - Blend the prior with the observed intervals as counts accrue, the way shop.cc's income prior does
    (income_prior_gems).
  - Exclude the arrivals that a wants_shop trip caused, so the measure does not feed on itself.
- In wants_shop's gain, for both the Prism and non-Prism branches, replace the purchase's value over `rounds` with
  its value over min(rounds, expected rounds until the next natural home visit). That is: gain x min(1, H / rounds),
  where H is the measured interval minus the rounds since our last arrival, floored at a sensible minimum you state.
  Keep the heal term as it is.
- Keep shop_gain_scale as it is: only the horizon changes.
- Other users of affordable_prefix / prices (the farm recall pricing, home_trip) stay unchanged, unless the same
  over-count applies there in the same way. If you think it does, do NOT change them; describe them in the report.
