## One bounded implementation contract for Sol

Experiment: **sustainable grouped help**, isolated from a67. No remote following, draft change, mailbox expansion or new safety credit.

1. Preserve harmless-only group hits and the existing partial-harmless preference. Preserve immediate actual healing, urgent conversion, capture, retreat and spell-kiting priority. Do not apply a blanket solo-clear requirement to crew work.
2. On an ordinary ranged farm turn, a damaging incumbent with `memory.crew && memory.damage > 0` must reach ranked evaluation before spending its movement-excluding basic on that group. Other existing harmless alternatives may still execute. The existing damage field is observed net HP loss, not guaranteed camp attribution; this conservative trigger requires no new query or ledger.
3. For a nonincumbent damaging passing target on that ordinary ranged path, a failed solo-cost estimate cannot be waived by the Boolean `ally_engaged`; defer to ranked grouped admission. Keep affordable passing work and melee's existing free-hit/continue-walk behavior unchanged.
4. Retain the existing crew output/arrival/remaining-work loss calculation. Remove the nonclaimed Boolean engagement promotion; genuinely grouped work can still pass the existing joint-output/share calculation. Where a local same-group partner is needed, use already-known visible work/claim and arrival facts, not arbitrary footprint occupancy, explicit different work claims or duplicated partner output. The existing owner/same-group partner path may already cover the nonclaimed case; do not widen it merely to manufacture activation. Forecast damage contribution is economic modeling, not a promise of continued actions.
5. A help override needs the same own-loss and existing deadline admission as grouped work. Reuse the computed certificate where available; if ordinary value pruning avoided it, lazily price only the nearest nominated helper candidate, at most once. A struggling claimant is a reason to consider helping, not sufficient evidence that the helper can afford it. No future heal/shield, max-health restoration or unseen tank credit.
6. A failed own-loss test may enter the parent's explicit risky fallback; it must not re-enter ordinary best or unconditional help. Retain least-bad work when no better option exists, rather than silently changing that policy into forced idling/recall. Consequently this arm need not prevent every recovery trip. After native action/observation changes, do not reuse an earlier transient certificate.

The existing proportional crew-damage model remains an estimate and can misprice actual nearest-target geometry. This arm aligns admission paths; it does not claim to solve that separate modeling issue. Keep scope to this consistency experiment rather than adding a new global crew solver.

Activation examples: a hurt ranged crew incumbent must now reach evaluation; a nearby struggling claimant with predicted helper loss above spare cannot win via help alone; the same pair with joint loss below spare remains eligible even when the helper cannot solo-clear. Zero-strength camps retain normal attacks; a safe spell clear remains eligible. Exact BigWorld activation depends on the existing calculated costs, which this witness did not log.

Diagnostic counters may report already-computed deferred-early-hit, certified/rejected-help, solo/joint loss, spare, fallback tier and final group.
