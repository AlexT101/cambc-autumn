## What the current policy already does

An eligible Prism does not monopolize healing. `bot.cpp:4817` local_support enumerates every visible injured ally once the mate gate passes, ranks useful gain first, and uses Prism/request only as a final equal-value tie. Fixed Prism identity instead controls formation direction, work claim, and which recipient can actively seek recovery. Replacing the mate with a dynamic target would primarily broaden those persistent behaviors, not fix current all-ally heal ranking.

The independent ready-heal helper (`4745`, called at `5109` onward) covers quiet one-step Willow movement when no eligible Prism exists: current ready heal, strict safe nine stations, gain>3B, member-finish veto and protected-work exclusion. Current-range generic healing, bank-directed nonregressing heal steps, and combat search also already consider other allies. The helper makes no request/offer or recipient commitment. Its failure to maintain a non-Prism formation is intentional, not evidence it forgot those allies.

## Smallest falsifiable contract, held for stronger evidence

Test a **recipient-side local recovery step**, initially for visible non-Prism allies, before considering a new formation/mailbox. It must distinguish proactive detours from already-required disengagement:

- Observe an actual ready Willow with a live current record, exact heal cooldown/cast state, no kept channel, and an upcoming turn. An already-acted healer is not a same-round service opportunity. Require a legal one-step recipient endpoint within current range/LOS of her current station; no assumed future Willow step or shared vision.
- Preserve capture/tribute, urgent banking, lethal escape, current valuable damage and immediate camp finish. Price the next return/work turn using the recipient's real kit: melee attack-before-step can be free now, but an exclusive spell can block the return next turn. Do not count both walk and exclusive cast, or both reset and another cast.
- Require the recipient to survive its actual endpoint and exit without receiving the heal; no ally tank credit. Prefer a step already justified by ordinary withdrawal, with no worse enemy/camp exposure or home progress. This adds a healing opportunity to a valid escape rather than funding exposure with unreceived HP.
- For a proactive detour, require an explicit useful-work gain after charging the actual next-turn loss and any capture delay. Restored HP is real; prevented recall or a saved future clear is not established unless current known camp load/health justifies it. Fish415 is marginal224 versus at least180 deferred/lost damage plus possible finish delay, not an automatic accept.
- Reobserve after moving and expire the attempt after the imminent Willow turn; do not idle through cooldown. Resume normal work only under the ordinary health/risk test. Multiple possible heal recipients or a higher-priority Willow action can defeat service, so a proactive commitment requires stronger evidence than a visible ready cast alone.

Existing communications cannot simply be relabeled for every ally: `comms.cpp:15–24` uses one payload bit plus two epoch bits, with fixed even-Prism/odd-Willow writers. It contains no recipient ID or destination. Arbitrary writers would make requests ambiguous, and different actors' visible target rankings need not match. **Do not broaden this mailbox yet.** If recipient windows show otherwise worthwhile approaches failing specifically because Willow redirects service, the next communication experiment should be an explicitly addressed, one-service offer/ack with expiry and protected-work cancellation—not persistent formation. Its encoding must be designed separately; no existing claim/bag/sighting bits may be silently repurposed.
