# Five-pick Willow: map-visible hypotheses

2026-10-23. Short read-only design note after the presence child. Read latest8be8eaa CHANGES/TODO and Cambridge Battlecode rules. No draft implementation, new analysis scripts, extraction, build or engine. Current experiment stays fixed P/V/M/Willow/HoR; these are hypotheses for interpreting results, not a selector or claimed wins.

Willow replaces **Ryth or Vendetta**, retaining HoR. Base Willow is700HP/55Strength/120Focus, versus Ryth1200/45/100 and Vendetta1000/70/100. Willow's ranged walk consumes the basic; both alternatives can walk and attack independently. Ryth supplies area stun and Health-to-Strength scaling, while Vendetta's kills refresh dash/reset. Willow instead offers at most360 actual healing every8 rounds to another visible ally in range4, sharing that turn's cast with reset; she cannot self-heal. Her ideal45HP/round is a ceiling, not draft-time health or permission to enter a harder camp.

| Public feature family | Why it might favor Willow | Opportunity cost / falsifier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Useful joint-work geometry:** groups with safe receiving/attack stations, connected approaches and space for a healer outside all damaging members' reaches | A frontline can do sustained work while an actually safe Willow remains within heal4/LOS, rather than both repeatedly travelling home. Walls/bushes and camp reach matter, not just centroid gap. | Narrow corridors may create contact while preventing a safe receiving station; scattered work may never meet. Strict support cannot borrow a tank's protection. Falsify if nominal receiving geometry yields few actual worthy heals or displaces more clear work. |
| **Damage burden and duration of ordinary farm cycles**, separated from harmless resources | Repeated moderate injury with useful work remaining can absorb heals and preserve capital-producing time. Two unmitigated wolves deal40/phase, below the ideal45 ceiling; an Elk60 or Golem120 already exceeds it. | This is not a sustain proof: discrete phases, other attackers, cast timing and actual HP decide. Jungle/Durability could change burden only after purchased; never assume those items at draft. Harmless/quick clears have little injury to repay support, and area stun may save more damage than healing. |
| **Health-return distance together with route overlap:** spawn↔work and work↔altar geometry | Long recovery travel increases the potential value of a real heal when recipients and Willow already share productive routes. | Width alone is ambiguous: long independent walks lower service access and cost Willow basics. Short safe returns make healing less valuable. Count actual avoided return/continued work later, not hypothetical gold from a heal. |
| **Local opportunities for melee/reset work:** multi-member groups, clustered resources, clear-to-capture travel | Low repeated-kill/reset opportunity can reduce the cost of giving up Vendetta; retaining HoR still provides one durable mobile body. | Dense accessible resources can strongly favor Vendetta's refreshed attacks/dash, and larger clustered attackers reward Ryth's area damage/stun. Member/group density alone cannot distinguish these. Falsify Willow if extra service accompanies slower clears, fewer payouts or delayed Treasure/Bruiser development. |

These features must be considered together. A high-gem map can give Prism safe independent scaling with little worthy injury, rather than valuable healing demand. Saved local Monolith had13 Willow casts/3821.8 outgoingHP and no scan-nominated worthy Prism service window; Fish had28/9113.8, mostly to Marcella, and all six nominee windows were later excluded by actual eligibility. Those observations motivate measuring service access to **any** ally; they do not establish a map classifier. Healing HoR also lowers his missing-health damage bonus, and healing Ryth's current HP does not raise his max-health passive.

Latest101–104 make early capital a stronger opportunity cost: Treasure-first and modeled Bruiser/completion alter both output and survival, with positive broad/fresh results in the upstream log. Old Willow item/camp comparisons therefore cannot be transferred unchanged. CHANGES102's four-pick HoR evidence supports keeping that baseline; it does not prove Willow beats Ryth/Vendetta at five picks. The older dense-arm split and a279808–808 are insufficient grounds for a density threshold or named-map exception. Main's failed fixed-Health build arms also caution against substituting a stat rule for actual service/output value.

Draft knowledge is static terrain, spawns, altars, camp layout/types/rewards and **opponent picks revealed in previous draft rounds only**. It excludes future picks, live enemy positions/items, later injury, capture ownership and a promised heal. Current `draft_order` caches before expensive analysis/first pick; an eventual opponent-aware remaining pick would need an explicit decision time and available-feature contract. Do not quietly feed final opponent rosters or later draft fields into that cached first-round choice. No counterdraft implementation is proposed here.

First evaluate the fixed five-pick experiment against unchanged latest main on native drafts. Before any selector, use a few predeclared feature families above to report roster activation, actual heal gain/recipient continuation, lost melee/reset output, camp cycles/captures and capital, Willow safety/deaths, travel/return time, native fuel/errors and score. Correlation in the same test panel only nominates a mechanism; require an unused holdout and sufficient five-pick cells before choosing a rule. No weights, optimized cutoffs or claim of advantage are selected by this note.
