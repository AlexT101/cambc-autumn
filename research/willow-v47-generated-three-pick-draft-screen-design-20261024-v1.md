## Mechanism readout and the first possible adaptive prototype

Compare paired map/side outcomes separately for each opponent, plus native errors/rollovers and completion. For the same predeclared first100 rounds, obtain actual first capture recipient/time, carried gems, capture gold, camp work, deaths/linked losses and tribute timing from existing compact summaries where supported. Later evidence should distinguish a carry dying with income from never receiving it. Avoid interpreting a total damage or win as an accessible-ramp measurement. Inspect at most one representative changed cell per candidate if it would discriminate between geometry, allocation and combat; actor-known windows follow a concrete decision, not a search for a favorable anecdote.

Prism still supplies a ready unit stun and ground damage at zero gems; its +1.5 Focus per **all carried gems** includes monster captures and kills, not only gem clusters. Stellar trades that control/scaling for different immediate damage. Cassi/Issac supplies another worker and turn, but splits positions/bags and either death kills both and transfers both bags; independent jobs and exposure matter more than body count alone. Marcella offers walk/basic melee work, reset stun and immunity before any item, plus tribute income only when she actually banks. These mechanics motivate the feature comparisons without assuming a specific winner.

Vesper remains controlled because +33% applies to eligible monster/capture and kill income, not just gold clusters; ranged basic, reset and dash also have value without a gold-cluster supply. A later Vesper substitution must price that kit and displaced recipient independently. This screen cannot establish such a substitution or a team-name counter.

The actionable first prototype after this screen is at most a **two-choice, first-slot map rule**, selected from P/S/C/M using an observed repeatable interaction with early accessible ramp or parallel-work/geometry, while keeping V/H fixed. Compare it with both its constant recipes; use the predeclared feature bands rather than searching map-specific cut points. If one choice is broadly preferable instead, test that fixed recipe; if interactions differ by opponent, retain that uncertainty rather than inventing a prefix rule. No arbitrary one-loss veto is required, but the six published maps and these12 development maps are not independent held-out confirmation.

First-pick feasibility matters: f475 `draft.cpp:283` caches `draft_order()` before `analyse()` builds its fields, so an eventual first-slot selector cannot simply read later draft-plan distances. A small deliberate reordering/reuse or bounded map-feature calculation would need its own source/cost review; do not add repeated per-group fields or a new low-fuel policy gate. That is a concrete implementation seam after this finite screen, not a reason to postpone the fixed-roster experiment. Freeze any resulting rule before new-seed three-pick holdout validation; no thresholds are fitted on that holdout.
