**Recommend a separately priced economic terminal in the existing strike comparison as the substantive hypothesis.** A smaller late-entry arm is useful only if a source-known fixture demonstrates activation. Neither is qualified as a superior Gold Rush18 play. Do not globally replace the two `G.gems==0` predicates: their effects and their opportunity costs differ.

## What the frozen source already does

- `bot.cpp:1326–1358,1955–1957,2170–2380`: ordinary farm scoring already includes gold. `gold_paid` adds Vesper's33% and Treasure's20%; `prices.per_gold` converts gold into gem-equivalent future upgrade savings. `known.gold` includes that conversion, home-ground weight and existing-choice stickiness; `worth=gems+gold` enters `rate(world,worth,cycle)` with travel/wait, clearing, capture duration and appropriate banking terms. `rate` discounts long cycles using the existing horizon. Ally-held/claimed cleared groups are filtered; crew work prices a damage share and leaves the capture to its claimer. This is not a missing universal gold multiplier.
- `contest_capture` (`4074–4095`) has no economic comparison: it selects the nearest qualifying cleared gem contest reachable before estimated payout. Removing its zero-gem filter would make gold eligible here, but would not import farm scoring or compare forgone hits. It runs **after** retreat, strike search, existing hold, banking, a ranged-hit return, shopping and chase (`4867–4918`). It cannot repair a ranged attack that already consumed the walk.
- `contesting` (`2587–2594`) also rejects zero-gem groups. Broadening it changes `want_home`'s Willow threat exemption (`2854`), protected support work (`4573`), and strike anchoring (`4867`). Anchoring sets `moves=false` and disables dash (`plan.cpp:1072–1080`), potentially losing a dash kill or escape. That is much more than recognizing a gold capture.
- Existing `holding_capture` already accepts uncontested gold, retains the last holder, considers lower-id allies/deadlines and works with capture recall. Its Vesper/HoR handoff is separate and narrow. No new remote yielding or universal Vesper ownership is required.

Gold Rush18 starts at `(15,13)`. Actual dash to `(17,15)` plus basic does not occupy group2. `(17,16)` is three king steps away: reaching it requires walk+dash or dash+walk, sacrificing Vesper's basic and spending the cast. It is not an equally priced landing tie. Search currently requires a strike after movement (`strikes_left:678`, `grow:1548–1575`, `settle:1446`), so an endpoint bonus alone cannot recover the missing no-strike terminal sequence. Full known-clear/free-tile and survival certification is still absent from the public export.

## Arm A — isolated late entry, activation prerequisite

Keep the existing gem contest and its priority unchanged. Only when it finds no executable gem contest, consider **an immediately occupiable adjacent tile** of a currently fully visible, freshly known cleared gold-only group with a visible enemy occupant, positive `prices.per_gold`, no allied body already contesting and a positive existing `enemy_hold_left` estimate. One native walk is the scope boundary; no dash, remote route commitment, new global search or new timing threshold. Scan only the existing eight neighbors, deduplicate their groups, use the current blocked/step-guard checks and `try_move`. Rank eligible equal-cost gold groups by the existing base-gold economic conversion; this is a proxy for useful resource value, not a promise we receive that gold. Do not grant our Vesper/Treasure bonus to the enemy-denial term.

This preserves all earlier combat, sure-kill, retreat, bank, shop and chase decisions. It still trades a possible later camp basic for immediate contest; that opportunity cost remains a measured weakness. Do not add hard gold persistence to `contesting`. Once the enemy leaves, existing uncontested gold holding applies; while the enemy remains, ordinary combat and retreat can leave the tile.

**Plausible activation to demonstrate first:** a visible enemy occupies a farther tile of a cleared multi-tile gold group; a free adjacent tile is reachable but the enemy is outside a feasible strike sequence and is not selected as prey. A second possible class is a melee actor with walk left after a basic and no selected chase. Neither is claimed to exist in the public window. Require native proof of reaching this branch with movement available and of resetting an otherwise continuing hold. If normal strike/chase consumes every nominated case, stop this arm as inert; do not present a predicate diff as an improvement. This arm does not fix18, does not price early optional combat, and cannot promise persistent denial.

## Arm B — priced gold-occupancy terminal and soft continuation

Add one optional economic terminal classification to the existing strike comparison after ordinary `want_home` and protected support decisions. Scope is ordinary, gold-only, actor-known cleared groups intersecting the existing49-cell search grid. Group qualification requires current full tile visibility and consistent occupancy; skip an existing allied holder/contestant where our extra body adds no denial, and preserve current hold/bank/deadline exclusions. Cache qualification once per distinct local group. No map, opponent or champion selector; no new fuel gate or outside path search.

Permit a movement branch with no remaining strike **only when its legal endpoint set intersects a qualified economic group**. Offer only those endpoint tiles through existing free/dash/guard legality and existing native sequence execution/failure handling. Keep `owed` and `strikes_left` pruning for unrelated movement. A walk spends the ranged basic; dash spends the cast and keeps its existing opportunity cost. Add the terminal occupancy credit once, after the sequence, not per move. Reuse current threat/camp prices, removed-enemy sets, unseen-threat handling and lethal checks. No credit for killing/stunning a threat that the economic sequence did not actually affect.

Current-tile occupation can receive the same **soft** economic credit in the comparison with leaving. This offers continued gold contest without changing `contesting`, Willow's threat exemption, support protection or a hard `Intent.anchored`. Safety/escape and a sufficiently valuable kill can still win. When an enemy leaves and the existing uncontested-hold predicate applies, that existing policy owns the next turn. This isolates economic choice from a blanket commitment rule.

### Units and a testable credit without a new gold multiplier

Search utility is in enemy-health-point equivalents (`plan.h`). A normal nonlethal55 basic contributes useful damage; a kill adds remaining target health plus `life_value=300` and carried gems at `gem_value=15` (`hit_worth:111`). Ability/cooldown costs, enemy/camp exposure and goal progress also enter this utility. `farm_step_value` already converts a round of farming output via gem density and `gem_value`; it is not a gold contest valuation.

Pass a small economic quote from bot to Search, because `prices`, `rate` and `gold_paid` are private bot helpers. A deliberately bounded initial quote is **one occupancy-turn's throughput value**, using the existing `rate(world, worth, capture_rounds()) * params::gem_value` conversion:

- For denying a currently visible enemy hold, `worth = prices.per_gold * G.gold`. This uses our marginal upgrade value as a declared proxy for base-gold opportunity; it does not know enemy savings, grant our bonus to the enemy, or credit the whole group as stolen income.
- For starting our uncontested hold, use only our prospective reward share with `gold_paid` and the existing conversion. Do not credit bonuses merely because Vesper helped clear. A second allied body does not get a fresh whole-group reward or reset credit.

This is a hypothesis using the existing rate function, **not a calibrated identity between denial and earned gold**. It credits at most one turn of continued occupancy in the current decision, with no multiplier tuned to Gold Rush. It deliberately does not multiply by estimated elapsed enemy progress: `held_since` is an observation-derived estimate, sometimes dated early for conservative interception, and is not a certified lower bound on lost progress. A future progress-aware arm would need trustworthy continuous observation evidence.

Compare this credit with the complete ordinary strike candidate at the same selection point. The missing basic is paid by its absence from the economic sequence; do not subtract it again. Dash retains `dash_steps * step` cost, and spending the cast loses any competing reset/heal/shield value. Do not also award the economic leaf a fictitious unused goal walk or duplicate generic goal-progress reward for the same occupancy advance. Normal strike leaves and kills retain their existing utilities; no forced pre-strike contest outranks them.

Six-round occupancy remains a continuation requirement. Contest resets enemy progress immediately but earns us nothing while contested. Departing, dying, being displaced or completing recall can surrender the denial; once uncontested, a fresh full hold is needed. The one-turn quote is deliberately modest: it may fail to beat55 damage plus sequence costs in a Gold Rush-style state. That would reject this valuation's preference there, not justify raising a universal weight until the replay changes. It still makes the legal economic action expressible and its price auditable. Do not promise that one-phase Safety predicts survival for six rounds.

## Activation, outcomes and rejection

First require source-known, fixed-opponent/draft fixtures and normal-fuel evidence. Record the admitted group and observations, incumbent bodies, legal action sequence, walk/attack/cast flags, ordinary strike utility, economic credit, dash/opportunity costs, endpoint exposure, chosen action and native outcome. For B distinguish **not represented**, **illegal/uncertain**, **safety rejected**, **represented but outpriced**, and **chosen**; these require different conclusions. An extra search node or decision label is not successful denial.

Positive mechanism witnesses: B legally chooses walk+dash occupancy over an optional nonlethal attack when its complete utility wins; next-phase evidence shows the predicted enemy hold disruption; later actions preserve an affordable contest or leave under danger. Include a current-tile contest where leaving wins, a sure-kill whose existing value still wins, and a valid existing allied hold that remains undisturbed. A needs an actual residual-walk/no-chase activation before any broad run. Do not count an enemy payout that was already impossible as newly denied.

Measure capture continuity and payout timing/owner, earned and spent gold, upgrade activation, damage/deaths/bag losses, allied production and final conversion. Prevent repeated-reset churn from looking good through a one-turn metric. Use both physical sides, preserve productive independent-work controls, and judge competitive value only on the required paired suite and unused-suite confirmation after the mechanism survives.

Reject or revise if legality/visibility is misclassified; the one-turn quote repeatedly buys exposure or lost kills worth more than the resource delay; it displaces an allied holder without team benefit; it induces endless contest/recall churn; new terminals lose required actions to fuel; or verified activation fails to improve team reward/conversion. A nonactivating late branch is not a repair of the early combat choice. No implementation is authorized by this note.
