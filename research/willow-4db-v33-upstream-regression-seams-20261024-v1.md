## Ranked attribution and improvement work

1. **Split deferred guards from the table queue, retaining all other 4db policy.** This is the cleanest new scheduling question. At `bot.cpp:2871–3057`, the generic guard still has several existing fuel checks, may return no/partial guard, and updates its measured cost. `guard_steps` now stores a callback; `Pathing::guard()` executes it at first route/moving-search use. Delaying it changes remaining fuel and potentially warmed Safety state. It can spare a stationary actor but push first useful movement behind farm/search work, or cause the old shortcut to omit protection. This is neither an established bug nor a replay-equivalence guarantee. An eager-generic/deferred-generic comparison with the **same queue** isolates that seam; a queue-on/off comparison with the **same guard mode** isolates the other. Keep eager Willow in every arm. A 2×2 on a small fixed source-matched diagnostic panel can reveal interaction before spending on a full comparison. Read first own action/first rollover and decision/route inputs, rather than selecting the winner-only cells. Do not treat a favorable arm as qualification by itself.

   The queue runs after actions, turn-end accounting, comms, approach preparation and existing warm-up (`main.cpp:139–174`). Therefore it cannot delay an earlier action in the same unsuspended turn. It **can** cost the following turn if cold allocation/ring bookkeeping/one fill exhausts the budget; or help it by removing misses. Its cold payload can reach 13200 bytes after a 6000 per-tile allowance, already flagged in the c71 review. The missing evidence is an actual Release phase witness that useful next-round work was delayed by queue continuation. No total-fuel or round-one outcome alone supplies that. Retain the static-data queue if this witness does not occur; investigate reuse/hit yield instead of assuming its +19.18% total fuel is harmful.

2. **Make the ward-visible search exception demand-driven, preserving legality.** `Search::viewers` currently calls `ward_sees(p)` before constructing ordinary terrain viewers (`plan.cpp:440–489`). Each Search's first such query enumerates native wards; the mask is reused only when its position is unchanged. Most targets/stations need no ward exception when ordinary visibility already admits every relevant station. An exact experiment can construct the ordinary viewer result first and consult the own ward only if a relevant in-range candidate is excluded, or carry authoritative own-ward placement state into the search after successful placement. The benefit is avoiding an import/visibility dependency that cannot change that answer, not weakening ward attacks. Preserve personal-owner semantics, bush-cluster vision, immediate same-turn placement and invalidation after move/reveal. Extra square/mask bookkeeping may erase savings, so measure existing-input heavy turns and generated WASM, not source-line counts. History attributes ~0.5% mean and +2.4% p99 to this 108 feature; that motivates the exact fast path but does not attribute the current deficit to it.

3. **Isolate wall-free unseen threats as a behavior/capability factor.** This change correctly permits ranged/ground threats across walls, but also changes which work/tributes/routes are considered exposed. On enclosed or separated maps, distinguish an actually reachable ranged/cast threat from general pursuit and from a remembered enemy with a broad possible set. A one-factor old/new-field diagnostic on otherwise exact 4db is an attribution arm, not a proposal to restore a known underbound. At a first divergence record enemy possible set, credibility/weight, queried tile, resulting damage/stun, and the rejected job. A future improvement should separate attack geometry from travel geometry while retaining a valid threat bound. Avoid concluding that all walls protect from spells. This can explain altered useful choices even when neither bot exhausts fuel; without a same-input witness it remains a hypothesis.

4. **Use existing frozen a67 as a source control before a broad rollback.** Its published v33 panel is already 40–40, but there is no corresponding full generated direct control in the inspected history. Comparing a67 and 4db against the same released source on the same gen cells separates the c71 scheduling package from the earlier 108/exact-work/comms package. Existing 16 paired 100M replays establish 4db/a67 action identity on that bench, while their eight default-budget emulator cells both win 4/8 and diverge on seven trajectories. Neither supplies the missing generated control. Reusing existing source avoids silently testing a newly compounded policy. Any score change still needs branch/phase attribution; direct win rates against v33 are not transitive estimates against Fish or another release.

5. **Keep capture timing and unit-only kite geometry as narrow later factors.** A hold can now call the altar field while testing whether waiting leaves enough bank time (`bot.cpp:1480–1496`), and newly reobserved friendly holds are dated later (`world.cpp:1853–1860`). Repeated known-state work might be cached with explicit within-turn dependencies, but first establish cold field construction/repeated queries in the relevant phase. For Marcella's kite, witness selected group, viewer stations, fallback and actual cast readiness; preparing member masks can precede a failed move, while a corrected line-of-sight station can also prevent useless arrival. Keep these separate from the Band live-route/cache-obstacle inconsistency already under investigation. Their old bench changes were <=0.06% with identical replays, so they rank below the wider scheduling/ward seams absent fresh activation evidence.

## Prior work that should not be repeated as if new

- CHANGES108's seven judgments were already tested separately and together against 107; combined historical 50.6%/1104 and the old 100M recovery do not settle this combined Willow/default-budget matchup.
- c71 already has 16/16 complete paired high-fuel identity and fewer >100k turns (624→492), plus the corrected eight-cell native diagnostic. Repeating that identity screen alone would add little.
- Generic fuel-gate removals, farm reordering/buckets, enlarged Reach memos, full fight-input memos, broad sight warming, direct rectangle replacement, singleton unseen-field replacement and per-branch search-cap polling have prior failures or no gain. A new design must explain the changed dependency or workload.
- Exact chase deduplication, stamp group listing and cached support endpoints already have measured replay-equivalence/savings evidence. Preserve them in semantic ablations. Removing them only adds codegen factors.
- Survival-first, emergency camp finishing, Band mask trace and the running Focus+safe-yield compound belong to separate active work. This note creates no new runtime authorization and does not change their sources or priority.

Treat combinations as experiments in interaction, with a source-matched common opponent and fixed cells, rather than requiring every useful ingredient to win alone or assuming one positive panel transfers to another opponent. The desired outcome is preserved timely useful choices and better economic conversion, not a lower aggregate fuel number by itself.
