## Implemented scope

Only Marcella can nominate one nearby mixed-map Monolith group with at most16 members and exactly one static damaging member. The descriptor requires current group visibility, native lifecycle state and exact live member records;97 rejects,98 alone invokes a visibility-checked dead-state proof. Unknown/respawning state cannot authorize harmful work. A current/one-step basic station reaches the dangerous member; cleanup has separate deterministic local stations and member-order routes. Only freshly certified or prospectively killed job tiles open, and other static damaging camp ranges reject local work and exits.

Forecasts use native current HP, current Strength and Focus, actual Monster/Jungle/Durability ownership, action flags and slot cooldowns. Each stationary activation receives one basic and at most one legal reset or unit Damage cast. Reset follows a spent basic; Damage respects walking exclusion. Cooldowns recur after eight rounds and age through the paid travel. No amortized `basic_output`, other champion, future item, future shield/heal, retaliation, stun saving or sleeping-entry saving contributes. Ignoring Marcella's deterministic reset stun deliberately overcharges incoming camp damage. Sorties cap at128 activations. Current HP funds the first sortie; only a paid escape, actual recall channel, spawn healing turn and return permit using max HP for the second. Reverse corridor entry hits are deducted. A second unfinished sortie declines.

One retained job stores group/member, phase, last exact HP/round, strict earliest-reset expiry, station/exit corridor and trip use. Expiry is `last_live_round + constants::capture_time + group.respawn`, without `capture_rounds()`'s additional round. Remote HP is only an unexpired upper-bound forecast; complete fresh native local state and real arrival HP are required before exposed return work. An abandoned selected unfinished cycle becomes a closed tombstone; this first arm forbids boss re-entry or replacement with another phased group until fresh capture/respawn evidence ends that cycle, including an exact later HP rise after expiry. Harmless cleanup can still be considered. This deliberately sacrifices later opportunities to prevent repeated normal shopping, death or claim changes from creating unpriced visits.
