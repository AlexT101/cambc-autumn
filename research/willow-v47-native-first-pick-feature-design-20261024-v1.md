## Exact proposed feature

The candidate is an isolated f475 child. On native three-pick maps only, select C/V/H when the tie-robust count below is at least2; otherwise select M/V/H. C means the single Cassi-and-Issac draft selection, yielding two linked bodies. Vesper and Heart of Ruin keep slots2/3. Existing four/five-pick and test-draft paths are unchanged. There is no teammate-Willow binding, map-name/opponent-prefix rule, gem-amount fit, openness threshold, income overlay or combat change.

Use complete native `StaticMap::groups` from both halves, with their full connected membership and native grouped gold/gems. No camp ownership label restricts consideration. An ordinary group has at least one member and no monolith. Harmless means **every** member's base Strength is exactly0. A positive-work group additionally has positive grouped gold or positive grouped gems; gold-only work counts, and zero-gem gold does not become zero opportunity. A mixed cluster/monster group is one damaging group, never a separate harmless cluster job.

Build exactly one additional uncapped multi-root spawn distance field with walls `blocked_for_us | camp_tiles`, roots `ally_spawn`, using existing `make_field`. This blocks walls/holes/altars/enemy spawn/all initial camp tiles, permits bushes and our spawn, and uses the engine's eight-neighbour walking/corner rules. Existing `from_ally_spawn` cannot substitute: it deliberately traverses camp tiles. Do not overwrite that cache, old plan distances or ordinary pathing masks. This feature models public initial walking access, without bodies, dash, teleport, wards, enemy control or clearing a route through another group.

For member m at p, enumerate the at most49 in-bounds tiles q with Chebyshev distance at most3. Its legal stations are `map.viewers(p,3) & map.standable` with a finite value in the new field. The viewer board uses the existing exact engine shadowcast and own-bush-cluster rules. A station's distance is the minimum native walking steps from any own spawn tile; member distance d(m) is the least such value, or unreachable. Distinct members may use distinct stations. This is basic ranged access, independent of cooldowns, cast range, willingness and a specific body's spawn assignment. It does not claim those stations can be occupied simultaneously.

The group rank distance D(g) is the minimum finite d(m). A group with no accessible member is omitted. A partially accessible group still takes an ordinary-work rank position, but cannot count as a harmless job unless **all** its members have finite d(m). This retains the offline distinction between initial contact and complete-group accessibility without claiming that the rank distance is total clear time. Capture geometry, HP/clear time, sleeping-neighbour risk, route exposure, retreat/banking and enemy arrival remain limitations. Native LOS and tie handling deliberately differ from the offline Bresenham/coordinate-ID approximation; report disagreements before outcomes.

## Symmetry-stable nearest-three boundary

Let K=min(3, number of finite-ranked ordinary groups), and D be the Kth-smallest group distance. Let S be the groups strictly nearer than D, T every group tied at D, and r=K-|S|. A good job is harmless, has all members accessible and positive grouped gold or gems. Define Hs as good jobs in S and Bt as non-good groups in T. The minimum possible good jobs over **every** size-K selection tied at the boundary is:

`guaranteed_jobs = Hs + max(0, r - Bt)`.

Choose C only if this lower bound is at least2. All strictly nearer groups and all boundary ties enter this calculation; no raw-coordinate/group-ID lexical order selects physical objectives. This uses only distances and whole-group predicates, so reflected/rotated physical sides produce equal features on a symmetric map. Example: one good group strictly nearer, one slot left, and one good/one bad tied group gives guaranteed1 and selects M. Counting all tied good groups would falsely call that two early jobs.

No groups or no reachable groups gives K0 and M. One reachable group cannot justify C. With two reachable groups, both must be good. Unreachable members, empty-reward groups or malformed/absent layout information cannot create a job. A valid empty-reward ordinary group may occupy a nearby rank position as non-good; an empty-members group is omitted. No static feature can guarantee linked-pair survival or two independent productive decisions; C's four bodies, split bags and joint death remain the mechanism being tested.

## Startup seam and cache reuse

Frozen f475 `draft.cpp:283` calls `draft_order()` before `analyse()`. The selector must finish before first-pick caching, not wait for plan data produced afterwards. Proposed minimal source seam: the non-test three-pick branch's cached lambda obtains `analysis().map`, builds it once if absent, computes the feature, then stores the exact three-choice order. `analyse` later reuses that same StaticMap and its bush caches. Existing four/five-pick lambdas and `params::draft_preference` remain unchanged.

There is one initialization trap to fix explicitly in that prototype: current analyse allocates its steps/spawn_steps inside `if (!a.map)`. Building a.map earlier would skip those allocations. Separate map creation from one-time plan-buffer initialization, with a small explicit initialization flag; retain the existing group-count/plan-capacity/spawn validation. A map exceeding DraftPlan packing capacity may still have a well-defined feature; inability to pack the old plan must not index empty vectors. No plan ABI/version or champion-side static initialization change is required.

The additional camp-blocked field is draft-local and distinct from old fields. It can be discarded after computing the cached decision. Prefer one supported SDK `export_linear_buffer` into at most10,000 u16 values, then direct row-major distance reads for local station loops. This avoids up to49T native field-get calls; do not assume undocumented field buffer layout. Existing viewers bits can be read through the same engine-chunk stride convention already used by analyse's attack-tile writer. No per-group field or global champion cache is introduced. The exact source-extraction seam would expose the scalar tie reducer and group-feature routine; Luna owns tools and fixtures.

The ordinary analyse pass may shadowcast the same range3 members again when writing draft attack masks. Reuse the shared expensive bush-cluster discovery; do not introduce a retained full bitboard per member merely to eliminate this bounded repeat. Its existing range1 writer and old fields stay intact.

## Cheap lower bounds and honest cost bounds

For every ordinary group, first inspect its at most49 stations per member using only standability and the exported spawn field, ignoring LOS. The minimum finite value over all its members is L(g), an optimistic lower bound on D(g). Keep the smallest three exact group distances found so far; before there are three, upper cutoff U is infinity. Compute exact viewers only when L(g) is finite and L(g)<=U. After processing that group, lower U to the third-smallest exact finite distance if available. No sorting is needed, and the group's native enumeration order affects work only, not the final feature: a skipped group has D(g)>=L(g)>U and cannot enter any eventual nearest-three/tied boundary. Equality is always processed. To keep code simple, an admitted group computes every member's exact station distance even if some members are far away.

This is public geometry pruning, not a low-fuel fallback. If qualification shows the lower-bound pass is overhead on small maps, a separate source review may choose the simpler exact-all-groups pass; do not change behavior or thresholds in response to game outcomes. No source variant is implemented by this note.

Let A=width*height<=10,000, T be total ordinary-group members<=A, Q the admitted groups' members<=T, B the number of bush tiles discovered by shared viewer work<=A, and C(m) the bush clusters intersecting member m's radius3 square, at most49. Work bounds are:

- One new uncapped spawn field, one at-most20,000-byte linear export, and constant scratch boards. The field is O(A) storage/work by the native engine; no route is computed per group.
- At most49T cheap lower-bound inspections and49Q exact station inspections, plus O(T+groups) scalar top-three/tie accounting. Retain per-group distance/good flags or repeat a bounded scalar tie pass; no sorting or heap allocation per member is necessary.
- For a non-bush target, viewers performs one native shadowcast plus one per intersecting bush cluster; for a bush target it uses its own cluster's cast. Total viewer casts are bounded by the sum of 1+C(m) over admitted members, at most50Q. This loose legal-layout bound is not a claim that a maximal map fits the draft budget.
- Cold bush discovery is **additional existing native work**, not hidden in the one new spawn-field count. `StaticMap::grow_cluster` floods in32-layer increments, can perform cardinal edge correction and indexes the cluster. Discovery is shared across members and later analyse; each bush tile is indexed once, but a large cold cluster can cost about100k per existing source comment. There are no new per-group fields, yet it is false to promise one native compute/call total.
- Fixed memory remains O(A+groups), including the optional field, one linear distance buffer and scalar group metrics; exact SDK/bot allocations and native-call cost require measurement. The published ~173 fuel/r4 shadowcast and ~2.5k full field are50x50 reference prices, not an upper bound for this draft.

No new fuel gate, partial-count decision, sampled group subset,49-per-member hypothetical-vision alternative, or silent substitution of terrain-only distances is proposed. The initial draft has1,000,000 fuel; a normal draft turn that fails to pick randomizes all remaining picks. Completion under that budget is therefore a qualification requirement, not an assumption. Reuse existing analyse scheduling/reserve after the cached selection is complete; it may finish less plan warmup on draft0 while carrying the same analysis forward.

Expected semantic edge cases return the complete M/V/H recipe. A native/geometry exception must also be caught inside the cached three-pick selection and freeze M/V/H, so the old ensure_picked fallback cannot accidentally pick baseline Prism after a feature exception. This covers ordinary recoverable errors, not fuel exhaustion: exhaustion cannot be caught into a meaningful fallback and must cause qualification failure. Do not add a threshold that chooses M because fuel is low. Pending selection/partially written metrics must never be cached as C. Neither an unavailable feature nor a skipped plan pack authorizes retrying a different policy on later picks.
