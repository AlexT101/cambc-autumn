## One isolated current-sighting arbitration experiment

Keep the wire layout, normal publication rotation, claims/bags/capture/support fields and receiver threat model. Permit **at most one replacement of the normal sighting per round**, for a current directly observed contact. First target the exact765 case: an occupied current-round record repeats the previous received enemy/position/health bucket, while another freshly visible enemy is locally relevant. Preserve the existing record on ties or absent evidence.

- **Relevance from sender-known data:** compare a candidate's current ready strike reach plus existing `params::pursuit_steps` against the sender or current visible allies; use the same cheap geometric reach convention, not a new Safety simulation or map search. An enemy threatening the sender's local work is useful to a nearby visible crew member even when that ally is not yet personally in strike range. At765 Marcella has both the contact and nearby Willow; the incoming HoR fix is far from this local pair. This is a publication priority, not a claim that the enemy can immediately hit Willow. No champion identity bonus is needed.
- **Protect valuable information:** do not displace a changed/new-location incoming fix merely because it is distant. Protect a known winning carrier or active tribute/teleport interception report using existing World beliefs; if the sender lacks enough information to rank it, keep it. For the same enemy, never replace current data with an older observation; a newly confirmed position/health change may use the single replacement allowance, with same payload retaining the incumbent. Different-enemy ties retain normal order. This intentionally limits the first arm to repeated remote information, not a general team-wide threat optimizer.
- **Freshness at send:** `main.cpp:146–152` already supplies `moved` and `refresh`. The occupied-slot branch currently skips `world.observe`. If a moved sender preliminarily qualifies to replace, use that existing refresh permission to observe once, then recompute the nomination. If refresh is unavailable, retain the incumbent. Require alive, visible, `Source::seen`, current `pos_round` and `stats_round` after the refresh; preserve stale-turn checks. Do not stamp an older visible flag with the current round or use hidden teammate positions as current. No extra observation is needed merely to keep a message.
- **Bound publication and prevent counter alias:** the current3-bit write counter (`comms.cpp:19–25,53–60`) cannot simply accept arbitrary same-round rewrites. Reuse it as a2-bit normal-publication generation plus1 replacement-used bit: normal publication advances generation and clears the marker; the one replacement sets the marker without advancing generation; later writers preserve it. No payload-width change is required. A normal ABBA gap spans at most two normal publications, so neither that generation nor the marker aliases a healthy reader. This reduces the repeated-payload wrap period from eight normal publications to four; long missed-turn gaps remain ambiguous and must retain conservative dating rather than invent freshness. Verify unchanged-payload, wrap and skipped-turn cases explicitly. If this protocol change is disproportionate, keep the policy unimplemented rather than allow unbounded writes.

The primary check is narrow: does the new765 Vesper contact actually reach Willow, with correct timestamp and a smaller feasible-position set, and how do her existing guard/goal decisions change? Post-refresh visibility and intervening writers must be observed in that check, not assumed. A changed guard need not force a hold or fight; preserve the heal where still legal. Only later can actions, survival and objective conversion be compared. Staying at18,57 is not established safe, and preserving Willow does not guarantee group12 control or a match win.

Replacing a duplicate can still harm coordination: another distant teammate may need its freshness to bound a mobile low-HP enemy; a new local contact may be redundant to receivers not visible to this sender; repeated replacements can starve the normal report; extra observe work has fuel cost. The duplicate-only, high-value-preserving, one-replacement scope makes these measurable tradeoffs rather than declaring nearby information universally superior.
