## Tier 1: one-visit dangerous work followed by cleanup

The smallest reviewable candidate admits **one locally known group** into farm's existing comparison. Keep all ordinary work, support, shop, bank, emergency and original siege paths. Nominate a qualified local incumbent, otherwise one nearest live non-sealing mixed Monolith from existing spatial data with a current/one-step work position. Do not recruit across the map, enumerate all bosses, alter the draft or add a fuel threshold. Reject unknown/respawning state or missing required member knowledge.

Use two phases, never one station claimed to hit the entire group:

1. **Dangerous-member phase.** First target an observed living damaging member. Start with self-only basic work; a visible-local-crew extension must preserve the reviewed per-actor delay/readiness/anchor contract. The selected legal station must reach this dangerous member, not every harmless cluster. Omit future spells, shopping, heals, shields and ally arrivals from the first admission estimate. Camp mitigation belongs to the actor receiving the hit. Current selfHP must cover charged entry, all damaging-member work and a legal known exit reserve. If several damaging members exist, charge all potentially reaching strikers and cover each dangerous member's station transition; declining these groups in the first implementation is a defensible narrower arm.
2. **Harmless cleanup/capture phase.** After freshly confirming all damaging members dead, move between legal working stations to clear remaining resources, then hold capture and bank under the normal claim/holder rules. Bound cleanup travel using the static member list and existing prepared routes; a cheap conservative route through each remaining member's known reachable station is sufficient. If no finite legal route is available, decline admission. Do not require the boss station to reach all clusters. The existing nearest-holder/Prism preferences apply, and a second actor cannot claim the entire reward merely for helping clear.

For self-only basic admission, use actual basic damage to each member and `ceil(member_HP/basic_damage)` slots, with current action delay and member transitions charged. `basic_output()` includes reset expectations and cannot be reused as guaranteed basic damage unchanged. Range/LOS and a genuinely damaging action must be certified at each station. This conservative tier may reject the observed full boss until sufficient stats/partial damage exist; that is expected, not grounds to invent a tank or health budget. The positive Mite witness is specifically attrition, so a Tier1 nonactivation would not disprove the larger mechanism.

Existing farm `rate` uses gem-equivalent worth over `rounds*(1+rounds/horizon)`. Price the same components and units as ordinary farm: holder-adjusted gem value, existing camp-gold weight, travel, clear, cleanup, capture, bank delay, risk and deadline. Never add boss gems both as immediate work value and as a second bank payout. Do not add Marcella passive gold in this arm. Preserve current bag-retention and Prism handoff accounting. Comparison belongs inside final farm selection; a selected-group executor must not rerank with old `siege_candidate`, reuse all-team readiness, or preempt a better ordinary choice.

Execution revalidates before incumbent shortcuts, after movement/observation and before another harmful action. The old siege body can be reused only with the certified selected group/member/endpoint and explicit engagement; no all-team gathering timeout. Loss of vision, blocked station, changed threat, exhausted reserve, lost real anchor or a better urgent bank/escape action invalidates the certificate. Persistent `memory.claim`/`memory.siege` is intent, not proof of feasibility. Use existing group/member descriptor infrastructure rather than a broad persistent world ledger; if canonical f7 lacks the reviewed exact descriptor, its bounded native import cost must be explicit, not advertised as query-free.

## Tier 2: explicit one-heal-trip attrition

This is a separate arm, because a partial boss hit has zero immediate payout and commits time to returning. Tier1 must not silently allow damage beyond its full-clear health budget.

Eligible states: one currently visible live dangerous member with persistent observed damage; self is a capable real worker; a known legal escape exists; no visible enemy/claim makes contest or return unsafe under current ordinary risk rules; safe recall and known spawn-to-work and work-to-altar routes are available; the job is neither already captured nor imminently resetting. A first version can restrict to self-only and at most one paid heal trip. Visibility is not proof that no hidden enemy exists: keep the same uncertainty penalty and abandon on new interference.

Compute a conservative first sortie from currentHP: before each prospective attack reserve **all** known camp hits on an actual walk-out route, plus any known entry/current-phase cost; never assume a geometric shortest route is unblocked. Only attack when one more damaging action and the reserve fit. At the observed boundary,1076HP covers four additional126 hits and leaves572; the model must distinguish hits before the actor's turn from future exit phases. Do not use maxHP as currentHP. Do not assume Ryth/Tavi stuns or a dash are free if their shared cast/move constraints exclude the planned action.

For admission, estimate first-sortie damage using conservative current stats, remaining bossHP, paid escape+recall+heal time, return travel, and a second sortie at current maximumHP **without future item upgrades**. Require the second sortie to finish the dangerous member and preserve its exit reserve; then charge harmless cleanup, capture and eventual bank. A future shop can occur under normal shopping but cannot be required for the admission proof. Health restoration must use actual recall/base healing rules, not an unpriced instant full heal. The total round estimate includes approach, both sorties, all movement, healing, capture and bank; the historical roughly218-round approach-to-bank is a scale check, not a runtime constant.

Price the entire job against ordinary farming before starting and again after healing. Remember only one intended group with existing claim/memory where possible; fresh observation on return is mandatory. Prior damage is not a reward and sunk work is not an obligation: discard the job if new state makes another route better. A second unavailable trip, camp uncertainty, contested return or missing finite route declines admission. No credit for remote allies, hidden enemy resources or hypothetical Willow heals.

This tier is the closest match to the positive replay. It is larger than endpoint correction and needs an independent activation/exit witness. If implementing only Tier1, label it accurately and do not claim to have implemented the observed solo-attrition counter.

## Bounded qualification and success criteria

Source review first: original Monolith-only/sealing/capture-only behavior unchanged; normal farm wins when more valuable; one nominated group; no identity/map-name branch; all incumbent fallbacks revalidate; no ally maximumHP pool; holder shares and score/gold units counted once. Then one authorized focused activation witness should show actual stat/knowledge inputs, chosen dangerous member, finite cleanup route, full-cycle rate versus ordinary work and camp-only exit reserve. Rejection is informative only when the rejected condition is recorded; unused code is not a successful counter.

For Tier2 additionally demonstrate a paid heal trip, persistent observed boss damage, fresh return admission, successful dangerous-member-to-cleanup handoff and eventual conversion. Verify withdrawal at actor order/turn boundaries, including another ready basic versus leaving, and unknown/blocked exits. The Ryth window is a useful negative arithmetic example: an extra stationary hit can spend the HP required to survive an otherwise open retreat.

Only after activation and safety checks should the parent schedule a small isolated f7 comparison on mixed-boss and ordinary maps at the user's CPU budget. Compare score/capture conversion, deaths, lost productive farming and opportunity rate, not merely boss damage or team gold. This report performs none of those runtime tests and promises no win-rate gain. No additional own-view exports remain authorized in the initial four-window allocation.
