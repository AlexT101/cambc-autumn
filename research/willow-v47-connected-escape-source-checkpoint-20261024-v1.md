## Admission and labels

`camp_trapped` preserves siege, zero camp exposure and the existing one-step already-compelled retreat shortcut; want_home's low-health and outmatched short circuits remain ahead of it. The unsupported maxReach+1 health exemption and geometric ring distance are removed. Eight straight compass sequences test each intermediate walking tile up to maxReach+1. A found path supplies a real upper bound; only health above its existing exit-cost expression can use a non-exact witness to keep working. If no geometrically closer safe goal exists, the verified ray is shortest and settles either side. A failed ray/health comparison otherwise requests the native exact reverse search.

Pathing owns one lazily allocated result. It imports actual current occupancy (walking terrain, current observed ally/enemy/camp tiles, teleport reservations and bumps), applies the effective guard and frees self. Importing directly avoids trusting blocked() across an intervening attack. Goals are standable, unoccupied, unguarded tiles outside the same Safety::camp_area whose awake members produce default camp_damage. Both use the same square reach and positive member strengths; no raw sleeping-range board replaces Safety.

The result validates origin, full walking walls and full goals at every call. begin_turn/invalidate revoke it; observations rebuild Pathing/Safety through the existing path. Exact reverse search uses compute_to_with_expansion_count at the public unreachable cap, stopping at the actual origin; no make_field wrapper discards a valid reached label. Failed search is certified unreachable only when its expansion exhausts before that cap. Unknown labels remain nullopt; an exact ray exposes only its verified prefix. A separate field slot cannot be overwritten by ordinary home/farm route queries. No permanent unreachable group or stale occupancy cache is introduced.

## Retreat and dash

flee_camps selects only known finite descending neighbors, ranking the existing immediate-damage-plus-camp-duration heuristic, then home distance. It does not choose a lower-damage nonprogress pocket over an available accepted descent. Enemy damage cannot increase relative to here; with a visible enemy the total heuristic also cannot worsen. A failed native move refreshes observations before fallback. Unknown/unreachable, enemy-constrained or refused descent reports a debug fallback and retains ordinary emergency escape.

The new dash path is only for current camp exposure with no visible enemy and an actually ready dash. Visible-enemy dash_away remains unchanged. It compares legal <=25 dash landings plus available remaining-walk endpoints against the ordinary connected walk. Strictly better finite connected distance and no greater immediate Safety damage are required; unknown points do not count as unreachable or free. Up to49 unique endpoint distances/Safety values are memoized within this one ranking; no duplicate endpoint triggers a second Safety query. Guard and actual walking walls apply to walk endpoints. The self-ability helper retains existing dash legality, wake, suicide/retaliation and cast checks and attempts only the selected landing.

After accepted dash, observe rebuilds occupancy/Safety. A newly visible enemy uses existing retreat_step. A now camp-safe landing stays safe rather than walking home back into exposure. Otherwise the intended adjacent continuation needs fresh finite descent and damage no worse than the fresh ordinary connected walk; changed/blocked/unlabelled continuation replans flee or emergency escape. Successful camp dash owns this activation's movement so the caller cannot append an independent home step that reverses it. Ordinary attack/reset tails remain subject to existing budgets.

## Cost and limits

Zero exposure, siege and already-compelled admission do not construct the result. Exposed admission pays at most8*(maxReach+1) ray tile checks, native occupancy imports/board operations and input comparisons. Difficult geometry adds one native reverse search per retained input set; calls with identical inputs reuse completed labels. A movement/new observation or changed goals/occupancy may require a fresh search. No new fuel gate or per-group farm search exists. This is not a fuel improvement claim: full connected searches, cold allocations and repeated fresh-board validation can be costly and require measurement.

Dash ranking visits at most225 landing/terminal pairs inside one49-tile region; it memoizes at most49 endpoint Safety queries, in addition to the ordinary <=8-neighbor walk comparison. Home tie distances are currently read at accepted pair visits and can repeat; that is bounded overhead, not a safety assumption. Post-dash revalidation/replanning has its own fresh-input cost. No general combat planner is added.

Unresolved model limits are deliberate: Safety uses actor-known current camp aggro and credible enemies; this is not a guarantee about future wakes, moving allies or hidden opponents. The retained dps*(1.5*D+1) reserve is still based on current-tile DPS, which can underprice stronger exposure along a detour. A walking witness proves connectivity under current blockers, not survival. Exact-ray-only labels can reject a useful dash whose endpoint was not labelled; it retains walking/emergency behavior rather than running a new candidate-specific field. Fracture own-view is motivation only until a source-known branch trace proves this caller.
