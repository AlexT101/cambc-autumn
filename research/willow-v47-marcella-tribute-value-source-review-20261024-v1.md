## Practical validation and limits

Useful small decision checks: eligible empty-bag Marcella gets the exact delayed term; a real carried bag with an unusable altar gets none; a claimed group, visible other holder, multi-holder or winning capture gets none; partner-rescued health gets none; unavailable spawn/altar facts or a cycle beyond the race gets none; Treasure scales B once; increasing positive delay reduces only the added term. Verify pruning can retain a candidate whose final bonus wins without granting spendable gold before actual tribute. These are arithmetic/control fixtures, not a requirement to manufacture a positive historical witness.

Drafted centre/spawn distances remain the existing static estimates, not guarantees of future occupancy, ownership, uncontested tribute or return spawn. Bonus is unavailable for many genuine shared opportunities by design; that is the approved narrow experiment, not a universal Marcella valuation. Nominal B can retain ultimately rejected candidates longer, and the additional bounded holder/race scans plus existing warm-field lookup can affect fuel cutoffs. Non-Marcella B is zero but code-layout/cost effects still require a control. Gem Fields Marcella2 r44–45 remains the bounded source-known activation nomination; measure actual capture recipient, tribute, later purchase and subsequent work after any divergence.
