## Concrete source mismatch

New `safety.cpp:656–684` adds `stun_pending`: raw `sdk_champion_buff_data` passed to throwing `ok`, with no visibility-error handling. `make_slot` invokes it for a believed alive, visible, nonself allied champion that is neither teleport-pending nor stun-immune. This correctly distinguishes a pending stun duration>0 from an expired duration0, but assumes cached `c.visible` still certifies live SDK access.

That invariant is false after self movement. Existing `World::update_champions(false)` (`world.cpp:1113–1120`) deliberately skips allies whose `stats_round == round`, under its comment that allies do not change during our turn. Their position/stats indeed stay fixed, but their visibility from our new position can change. `World::observe` refreshes the vision board but does not refresh those previously read allies. Thus even `see_again` (`bot.cpp:2698–2706`) reconstructing Safety cannot repair this particular cached visibility bit. `World::revealed`1368 only triggers on newly seen enemies; an ally leaving sight alone need not trigger observation at all.

The old combat model consumed cached ally stats without this new live buff read. `fighting_allies` (`safety.cpp:628–651`) also caches its seen-ally mask/boxes for a round, so its inclusion is not a fresh visibility certificate. `Safety::new_fight`2200, `chase_trade`2217 and `stand`2295 can reach `fill → fighter → make_slot` for these allies. A move followed by a newly required fight evaluation is therefore sufficient in source to attempt a forbidden live read. The per-Safety `made_` bit can hide the fault when that ally fighter was already built; a reconstructed Safety or a newly included ally can expose it. No enemy movement or engine race is required.

Other newly introduced live reads were inspected: Search's own-ward list/vision calls use returned own active ward coordinates, and turn-order's added shield read uses live self. Neither has this same ally-belief visibility mismatch. They are not eliminated as actual trace sites merely by this source audit. Existing `has_stun`, `shield_value` and unrelated raw APIs remain alternative code97 sites until the failing import is witnessed.
