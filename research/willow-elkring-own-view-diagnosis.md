The source makes this mechanism explicit: `SupportExposure::safe()` is `!enemy && !camp`; `support_exposure_raw` sets `camp=true` for an in-range living damaging camp even when asleep, while its camp damage, imminent damage and exit burden stay zero for that sleeper. The escape comparator tests `safe()` before home progress. Current camp-free `(0,25)` therefore categorically defeats `(0,24)` whenever current has no enemy hit, regardless of the latter's zero current camp damage. When the enemy horizon makes both unsafe, home progress can win; when that horizon changes again, camp-free reversal can win. This is a source-supported explanation of the observed shape, not a claim that the unexported branch/risk arrays have been witnessed.

The opponent also executed real outplay: after Willow's r306 stay, Vesper walked `(8,21)→(7,22)` later that round, then dashed to `(5,24)` and hit Willow for 135 before her r307 turn. In r308 it walked, reset, and hit for another 135. Priority/order changes mean this two-turn opportunity must not be reduced to a static range-3 check. At r309–310 Vesper chose to break the altar lock; that temporary distraction was observable after an action, but its future continuation cannot be credited to Willow. An earlier move toward `(1,25)` is a useful trace comparison, not an established escape route.

## Small experiment: affordable sleeping-camp transit in stalled owned escape

Keep the normal nine-station construction, `SupportExposure::safe()`, all ordinary support/capture/banking safety, and the original comparator. Add a bounded fallback only after that comparator's original choice fails to make strict home-field progress. **Include current-tile stays and non-progressing reversals.** A current-tile-only trigger would admit the first step and then reproduce the same camp-free reversal next turn. Never replace an already home-progressing original escape.

Require owned escape with `leave` true and a recent credible visible pursuer; original best must be available for comparison. An alternative must already be legal, strictly reduce the existing home field, have no modeled enemy hit/stun, zero camp imminent damage, and obtain its only safety disqualification from sleeping camps. Do not replace a safer enemy/imminent-damage outcome with a worse one. Every relevant living damaging camp must have a successful fresh asleep observation and complete matching member identity; unknown, stale, newly awake or soon-respawning contributors reject. A static camp tile is never a usable station just because its occupant sleeps.

Budget waking conservatively, without assuming an enemy/ally will leave the group asleep or tank it. Use summed incoming damage from every living damaging member that could reach the entry/bailout path. Require HP strictly greater than entry exposure plus a **certified bounded local bailout** to a camp-free tile; this is a camp-only bailout reserve, not a promise to tank the entire home route or evade future enemy movement. A cheap certificate can inspect fixed straight directions for at most the current maximum camp range plus one, against already-held static terrain/blocked information, rejecting paths through blockers or additional unknown camps. Test the proposed direction and its reverse first; at most eight fixed rays are bounded, with early stopping when sufficient reserve is certified. Keep the original enemy/stun/immediate-damage veto on the proposed station; do not add speculative enemy search along hypothetical future turns. It needs no new global route, board, native read or general lookahead search. If a cheap trustworthy certificate is unavailable, reject that candidate. Consume member-index spans before asking another tile: its underlying pool can reallocate.

For A's single Elk, entry `(0,24)` has a one-step bailout to `(0,25)`; counting entry plus that exit at full 60 damage costs 120, below HP430. At a deeper `(0,20)`, the valid vertical exit takes five steps, so the same conservative six-phase allowance is 360. The off-map geometric western exit is invalid. The full nine-tile homeward band would cost 540 if awake throughout, so the arm must **not** claim that full-route tankability is proved. Reevaluate every turn; if the group wakes, discard this asleep-only exception and let the ordinary least-bad escape remain authoritative. Do not forbid necessary escape merely because every option is bad.

Do not attack, stun or cast at camps during the transit decision. Preserve ordinary useful ally healing; it does not wake camps. A failed native move must refresh and use the current station records exactly as the existing retry loop does. At most one successful movement occurs. No direction commitment, ally tank credit, future heal credit, map-specific exception or permanent camp-safety relaxation is required.

One implementation caveat matters for the fresh gate: `health_round[i]==round`, visible tile and `member_asleep(i)` alone do not prove a successful current awake query. `world.cpp` updates health freshness before reading optional awake state, and a failed awake query can leave an older asleep belief. Retain a small success/asleep certificate from the already executed member-state read if no existing field proves that success; do not add another host query.

## Minimum validation and failure modes

This is sufficiently concrete for one narrow arm, with mechanism validation focused on A309–313. Log the executed branch, leave/forced state, original choice and home progress, selected transit candidate, sleeper freshness/identity, wake reserve and bailout certificate, and actual movement. Existing nine station scalars suffice; do not build diagnostic policy caches. If the original branch is not executed or `(0,24)` is rejected by an independent enemy/legality reason, report that and reassess rather than forcing the arm.

The mechanism check must show both entry and continuation when freshly safe, not a cosmetic entry followed by return to the old tile. Check rejection for unknown/awake members, insufficient HP, blocked/off-map bailout, enemy/stun exposure, and an original already-progressing move; verify a failed move cannot reuse stale stations. Watch for newly increased camp deaths, hidden-blocker retries, lost support service and slower gem conversion. After the focused mechanism test, judge the arm against the pinned baseline on the proper broad suite; these two losses alone cannot establish a gain.

A safe recall is not supported at the nominated core turns. B668 has four recall ticks left while a ready-dash pursuer is distance 6; it can close before arrival. A309–310 has four ticks left and a pursuer at distance 4 that can walk and later basic even with both abilities cooling down. Its actual lock-breaking is not guaranteed future protection. At B667, continuing 37 tribute ticks against the already visible 190-Strength pursuer is substantially less credible still. Earlier no-enemy tribute initiation is not disproved by later omniscient pursuit. The useful repair target is mobile escape through affordable dormant-camp space, not forcing these channels to continue.
