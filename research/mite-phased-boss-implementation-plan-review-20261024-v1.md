# Phased solo boss: implementation-plan review

2026-10-24. Source-only review of the concrete plan supplied by `phased_boss_implementation`, canonical Astraeus f7, and `mite-phased-boss-source-design-20261024-v1.md`. No implementation, tests, builds or replay decoding by this reviewer. Willow-first manipulation batches are excluded entirely.

**Recommendation: implement a Marcella-only, one-heal-trip experimental arm with a fresh local escape certificate and the revised safe transit routes.** Do not preserve the generic35% threshold inside that certified job merely to match the old heuristic: it prevents testing the observed4100HP mechanism. Keep the threshold and every existing priority for ordinary work. This review supersedes the earlier proposed unconditional35% stop for the boss forecast; it does not loosen visibility, route, action-budget, enemy-risk or health-survival requirements.

## Admission and executing the same plan

The proposed one-group nomination, exactly one static damaging member, at most16 members, exact current visible member descriptor and bounded local station/cleanup/exit rays are a coherent first scope. Marcella avoids health-dependent damage, dash routing and ranged walk/basic conflicts while retaining both unit Damage and reset. No remote ally output/HP, future items, healing, shielding, retaliation or enemy absence may be invented. Unknown routes/members and another damaging camp overlapping the local work corridor reject this first arm.

Canonical `want_home` (`bot.cpp:2849–2870`) runs before farm; it combines low HP, pack-zone danger, camp exit risk and enemy-fight safety. A generic `phased_safe_now` that bypasses low HP and then lets ordinary dispatch continue unrestricted is too broad. Use a round/position/group/member/observation-epoch certificate for an already selected job. It may waive only the generic low-HP check and its own certified camp-exit heuristic while the job actually owns a WORK or EXIT action. Keep enemy danger, game-ending interruptions, bank/urgent priorities and invalid-certificate retreat. Do not set `memory.siege` to gain the old exemption: that would mix in old all-team admission and gathering semantics.

Revalidate after any preceding movement, shopping, support/search action or new vision. Read current action flags/cooldowns at the executor, not only at turn start. If the certificate is invalidated, the selected job loses comparison, or ordinary work is about to run instead, restore canonical `want_home`/retreat **before unrelated harmful work**. An early low-HP waiver must not leak into farming a different group. The selected executor must not call the old `siege_candidate` again. An ephemeral ownership flag should suppress unpriced `strike_after`/tail casts; normal free actions and recall need explicit handling. These are concrete integration requirements, not a demand for inaccessible public memory.

`continue_recall` (`4140–4188`) cancels routine recalls unless another policy keeps them. Add a narrow continuation for the certified paid heal trip only after its safe exit is reached, retaining game-ending, threat and bank checks. At spawn, allow the normal mend turn and shopping; do not forecast future purchases. Reprice after healing before return. A trip counter must survive normal `memory.claim` clearing/temporary interruption so repeatedly abandoning and renaming the same unfinished job cannot create a third heal trip. Retain one group/member/cycle identity until a fresh capture/respawn establishes a new cycle; a broad world ledger is unnecessary.

## Timing and a real-stat capacity check

The actor-known Big World export records Marcella maxHP4100, Strength290, Focus120, Monster/Jungle/Durability. Thus basic319, unit Damage264, and Monolith hit126 are consistent with existing item multipliers. Forecast one shared cast per activation, reset only after a basic was spent, and exclusive Damage only without walking. Cast in roundR is next legal atR+8. Carry exact cooldowns through escape/recall/return; do not reset them just because a new sortie begins.

A conditional capacity example with both slots initially ready and full HP shows why an artificial5200HP requirement is unnecessary. Reset has greater incremental damage than Damage here, so a legal stationary schedule uses reset at0/8/16/24 and Damage at1/9/17/25. First26 actions deal `26*319 + 4*319 + 4*264 = 10626`; a second24 actions deal `24*319 + 3*319 + 3*264 = 9405`, sufficient for20000 bossHP. Charging one entry hit,25 intervening work hits and five exit hits on the first sortie costs3906, leaving194HP; second costs `(1+23+5)*126 = 3654`, leaving446HP. These deliberately omit all stun savings. They demonstrate possible boss-only capacity at real recorded stats, **not admission at the replay's particular currentHP/cooldowns, a certified local route, or profitability of the complete job**. A positive extra margin can be required and priced; do not silently reinstate35% under a different name.

Camp phase precedes champion actions. The current phase's damage is already reflected in currentHP. Walking/attacking now changes the next phase; killing the boss removes its next hit. Charge successive exit endpoints until outside range, with no double-count of the already-paid phase. Both entry and cleanup transitions need the same accounting. A one-step melee entry may basic/reset, while an exclusive Damage cast cannot accompany that walk.

Marcella's enhanced basic after a successful reset deterministically stuns the camp (`cambc-rules.yaml`, abilities/stun and Marcella kit). It can later be credited as exactly one skipped next camp attack, using a forecast phase bit only when that same planned basic hits the boss. No stacking, saved unused enhancement, phantom second cast or stun on another target. The minimal first arm can omit this credit: the example above already supports the intended two-sortie mechanism. If included, actual action success must match the schedule and the next fresh descriptor must revalidate; it needs no general stun/Reach planner.

## Travel, return and conversion

The implementer's revised route plan is acceptable as a **job-local** implementation: targeted cached fields over public static camp-aware terrain, one to the safe exit/staging tile and one to currently usable altar tribute tiles, with bounded concrete descent paths (proposed cap256). Blocking raw damaging camp ranges for transit is one conservative option, not a requirement to build a universal global path-safety framework. The root's final scope uses existing farm travel risk/distance conventions for long travel, finite camp-aware routes and fresh validation on local arrival. A route avoiding only camp tiles must not be called zero-damage: ranged camps can still hit it. Terrain-only `from_ally_spawn`/ordinary altar distances alone also do not prove camp-aware connectivity. Public static board construction and two native field builds have real cold cost and must be measured honestly.

If transit avoids all raw camp ranges it has zero modeled camp damage; otherwise price its exposure using the existing travel-risk convention rather than assuming fullHP on arrival. Charge the reverse **local** corridor from safe exit to work station exactly and deduct it from second-sortie arrivalHP. At admission, account for the spawn tile recall can actually choose: use a conservative finite bound over possible spawn arrivals, or explicitly reprice from the actual spawn before any return commitment. Future occupants or new threats may invalidate the route; abort/reprice instead of claiming guaranteed future safety. On local arrival, fresh actualHP must support the remaining one-visit finish and exact exit reserve, regardless of the earlier travel estimate. After boss death, open only freshly confirmed job tiles for cleanup/capture/local exit. Preserve other camp exclusions and current altar-lock checks.

Remote last-seen bossHP can be an upper bound only **strictly before `last_live_round + settings.capture_time + group.respawn`**, and newer capture/respawn evidence invalidates it earlier. Another actor may kill and start holding later in the same round as our live observation. Do **not** substitute the farm helper `capture_rounds()` here: its extra+1 would extend the remote-HP assumption an unsafe round. This is a conservative earliest-reset bound, distinct from the whole-job capture-duration estimate. Fresh local observation is mandatory before renewed damage. Price approach, both work phases, exit, actual recall, one spawn healing turn, return, cleanup, capture, altar travel and tribute once, against ordinary farm using the same holder/gold/risk/deadline units. A feasible bank route is not proof normal future banking will realize it; track actual conversion rather than counting partial boss damage as reward.

## Ready for implementation, then meaningful checks

The revised plan is coherent enough to implement without a private server binary. First demonstrate at realistic stats: a selected rate winner, actual planned casts, positive escape margin at each phase, one paid heal trip, fresh return, dangerous-member→cleanup handoff and conversion. Negative checks should include a blocked/unknown exit, stale member data, new enemy risk, current cast already spent, another camp's range, third-trip rejection and loss of job ownership below35%. Original Monolith-only/sealing/cleared paths and ordinary farm must retain their original dispatch. No competitive-strength claim follows from a single activation; no runtime is requested or performed by this review.
