# Fight support: bounded first-exchange participation

2026-10-24. Source review of frozen f475 and M+H; no implementation, runtime or new extraction. The failed Boss target trace is not evidence. This is an isolated modeling experiment, not a claim that it caused a particular loss.

## Established source omission and existing partial protection

References below use `provenance/willow-astraeus-clock-recovery-20261024-v1/workspace/bots/service-base-f7/src/`. `safety.cpp:628–651 fighting_allies` admits visible living allies within Chebyshev4 of the queried tile, independently of enemies, terrain or work commitments. `make_slot:654–674` supplies zero arrival delay except stun/teleport. `attack:269–272` then credits basic plus the preferred ready cast without target distance. `stand:2294–2323` explicitly delays approaching enemies but not ordinary approaching allies. This can credit damage that no legal immediate allied action can deliver.

There is a second effect: `Side::weakest/hurt/spill:108–168` can focus damage onto any included living ally, regardless of arrives_at. Nearby allies therefore provide both output and health-pool protection. Merely setting their arrives_at=1 fixes neither remote damage absorption nor early side-health bounds (`side_health/overwhelmed/simulate:441–559`). Do not describe an arrival-only patch as solving both.

`bot.cpp:3240–3262 prey` already nominates a one-turn delayed ally when `strike(next).hits(target distance)` fails, except ready heal/shield support. `Safety::chase_trade:2216–2245` preserves that delay and deliberately avoids roster-only memo reuse. This existing repair concerns a specific chase target and does not propagate to stand, fight or step_guard. Its scalar Strike ignores terrain and is an upper bound, not a legal route witness.

M+H's safety.cpp differs from f475 only by `channel_enemy_may_reach` for farm-hop safety; the routines above are unchanged. No need to attribute their omission to the combination. Ordinary timer4 recalls are cancelable optimization stubs. A standing comms claim (`comms.h:43–47`) is not a fresh promise to attack or a reliable declaration of retreat. World does not retain an ally's chosen path/leave decision, and ally teleport destinations are not currently imported (`world.cpp:1180–1197` handles enemy destinations). Do not manufacture a traveling-away predicate from these fields.

## Smallest coherent candidate: deferred remote allies in stand only

Use an isolated child of f475 first, keeping all economic, claim, chase, ordinary fight and step_guard policies unchanged. Scope the correction to `Safety::stand`'s actual local fight. Avoid a blanket radius reduction and avoid a new path field or per-tile fight search. The initial version uses a deliberately optimistic distance proof, not a claim to solve all wall detours.

Nominate a non-self ally as **irrelevant to the first exchange** only inside this cheap, explicit envelope:

1. Its live current-round belief is visible/alive and it has **not acted yet this round**. It is not stunned, teleporting or a linked-pair body. Exempt both Cassi/Issac bodies in this first version rather than breaking linked death accounting. Exempt kits with heal or shield service; this preserves rescue roles without estimating their target selection. Cancelable recall/tribute alone is not an exclusion or a reason to defer.
2. All enemies included in this stand forecast have current visible positions; otherwise preserve baseline. No included teleport/respawn uncertainty. The ally's next action is still in the current round, so an enemy cannot obtain two activations before it. Treat every relevant enemy as able to use one full activation regardless of its actual slot order: that is permissive to rescue, not a forecast of intent.
3. For **every** included enemy, prove both no outgoing contact and no incoming contact over that exchange. Use full-kit one-activation Strike reach as an optimistic upper bound (all slots available, ignoring walls and cooldown restrictions), plus the other's maximum walking/dash displacement:1 normally,3 for a dasher. Require distance greater than `ally full-kit reach + enemy displacement` AND greater than `enemy full-kit reach + ally displacement`. Take the actual damage/stun reach, including ground radius, splash and walking/reset legality. A pending teleport is outside this envelope. Any failed proof preserves baseline membership. This bounded <=6×6 scalar test makes no new native/Safety query and intentionally accepts plausible ready ranged followup or immediate melee rescue.

This is an actual prototype with a narrow positive domain, not a requirement to prove every ally route. It catches widely separated support admitted solely because it is close to the actor. Walls may make additional allies unavailable, but failing this generous distance proof is **unknown**, not permission to perform an expensive terrain search or remove them. Do not weaken the bounds to force Boss activation.

For a nonempty deferred mask, remove those allies from both damage **and target/health pools during the first simulated exchange**, then join them at the start of the next exchange if a local fight remains. Run the ordinary existing attack/cast/stun/shield/camp arithmetic for the first two side activations. Keep cooldowns and shield expiry indexed to the original turn origin; joining at turn1 must not reset readiness or shield lifetime. Preserve the deferred allies' health snapshot and do not pretend they absorbed damage or spent a spell while absent. This deliberately optimistic second-exchange joining does not claim actual arrival through terrain.

The clean bounded seam is a small pending roster passed only to this contextual trade, inserted at the first-exchange boundary. Before insertion, `count/alive/weakest/side_health`, linked handling and defeat checks operate on actual local participants; local defeat before help arrives ends the local trade. After insertion, invalidate weakest/quiet/plain and early-bound caches and rebuild applicable links. Disable or cap quiet skipping and early whole-horizon shortcuts across the pending join, so they cannot jump past it or silently count remote health. Do not simply delay attacks on bodies still in Side. Self is always local. Preserve the existing they_first order, self_falls semantics and caller's decision rule; do not introduce a new unconditional retreat veto.

The zero-mask path must retain the current stand/memo behavior. For nonzero masks, bypass the ordinary roster-only `fight_of` memo, as chase_trade already does; p, deferred membership, enemy walking delays and current observation affect the contextual result. Do not store query-specific arrival state in global `made_fighters`, change `fighting_allies`' round cache or invalidate every fight globally. `step_guard`'s roster-subset dominance proof assumes the old homogeneous trade and must not receive these contextual results accidentally. No new fuel gates or runtime log loop.

## Production fixture first, then isolated native comparison

Use unchanged production inclusion/fill/trade bodies bound to the source hash with mocked World snapshots, as in existing source-extraction fixtures. The oracle is legal range/action geometry, not a copied predicate. A minimal positive example is an open, fully visible three-body state: self at20,20, an unacted allied Marcella at16,20, enemy Ryth at23,20. Radius4 admits Marcella. Even allowing Ryth one walking step toward her, she remains beyond her full damage-cast range4; even allowing Marcella a step, she remains beyond Ryth's full ground reach5. Give her no support kit or pending channel restrictions. Baseline nevertheless credits her basic/cast and lets her lower HP absorb focused damage. Assert the first exchange excludes both contributions in the candidate; after joining, assert original cooldown/shield-time indexing rather than a fresh-turn reset. A transparent-hole barrier variant demonstrates that current inclusion has no connectivity check, but is not required to activate an unimplemented terrain classifier.

Negative controls must cover: ready ranged damage/ground radius reaching after enemy approach; immediate melee rescue as the enemy closes; a nearby Willow with a ready heal and an injured ally; ready shield support; zero deferred mask; an ally already acted; a pending stun/teleport; a linked pair; one unseen forecast enemy; and local defeat before reinforcement. Repositioning the same ally close enough must restore baseline participation. Verify first-exchange damage recipients as well as totals, then the next exchange's slots/turn indices. A long-horizon result alone would miss the remote-health bug. These are behavioral expectations, not a promise the candidate wins every case.

The existing Boss trace can still answer whether this mechanism matters there, but successful exact public/private reproduction is not a prerequisite to testing the controlled source counterexample. Conversely its failed trace must not set fixture inputs or justify widening the activation envelope. After source review and positive fixture coverage, compare the isolated arm with f475 on matched stress cells and common opponents; integrate with M+H only as a separately pinned union if useful. No global ally-path planner, economic redistribution, fixed camp commitment or roster change is needed for this experiment.
