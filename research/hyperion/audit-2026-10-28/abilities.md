# Audit: abilities (`bots/astraeus/src/combat/abilities.{cc,h}`)

Line numbers are those of `abilities.cc` after both commits.

## Changed

### Cleanup (commit 1, `b7b21ad5`): bench replays byte-identical (8/8), fuel -0.06% total
Bench: `00ambush 00lanes 01hooks 00interlock`, `--test-draft`. The drafts included Stellar, Heart of Ruin, Cassi & Issac, Marcella, Prism, Ren, Ryth, Tavi, Willow, Vendetta and Vesper Vale, so every branch here ran (unit, ground, landing, camp).
- **`area_targets`** (new): `enemy_cast`'s ground and landing branches had about 60 near-identical lines collecting visible and unseen targets. They now share one helper, parametrised by the area radius (`a.radius` for a landing, `ground_radius` for ground) and an optional `lethal` board. The landing branch now checks a visible Ren's suicidal retaliation before `visible_record`/`damage_to`, as the ground branch did. That skips two host calls, and in the rare case where the belief says visible but the record is gone, the landing now keeps off the Ren like the ground cast (more conservative).
- **`SlotHit`**: the `suicidal`/`back` lambdas are now a small struct. `my_health()` is read once per `enemy_cast` instead of once per enemy per slot.
- **`enemy_cast`**: the early "nothing in reach" gate now counts only castable slots, so a turn with both slots on cooldown skips the reaching scan (strike/visible_record per enemy). The unused `landings` parameter (always true) is gone. The escape-mode index filter runs before any host call. `stun_pending(e.handle)` (a buff host call per enemy per slot) became the cached `e.stun_pending`, which World reads when it observes the enemy. `not_free` is now built with `not_into` instead of fill+xor. The escape test `a.target != unit || a.lands()` became `a.target != unit` (a landing is self-targeted).
- **`camp_cast`**: the ground and landing branches merged: same targets, the same skip of other groups' members at the area radius, and the same unseen-Ren skip. `skip_rens` duplicated `unseen_suicidal` exactly and now calls it. The landing floor expression was `floor` in disguise, since `finishes` is false for landings. The lazily memoised per-group threat list in the landing `keep` is now `landing_threats`, built once from `groups_near(here, max_camp_reach + dash_range)`. That gives the same verdict: any member reaching a landing is a camp tile within that radius of `here`. The local `camp_stunned_now` duplicated util's `camp_stunned` and is removed: it differed only by throwing on an error that can't happen after a successful `sdk_camp`. The camp-stun exposure scan and buff read now run only when a ready slot stuns and the member has Strength.
- **Wrappers**: `cast_at_camp` and `cast_at_camp_on_approach` share `execute_camp_cast`. In `stun_worth`, the game-winning check now sits inside the tribute branch. A few comments were tightened or added (the area helper, approach gates, `damage_to`'s `native`).
- The new anonymous-namespace types are named `SlotHit`/`MonsterReach` because the unity build shares anonymous namespaces with `plan.cc` (`Hit`) and `safety.cc` (`Threat`).

### Fixes (commit 2, `f0a4e576`): bench replays unchanged (8/8 identical), fuel +0.00%
- **Camp spell value ignored Monster/Collectors** (`camp_cast`, ~l.479-490, retarget ~l.530). The SDK's `damage_amount()` excludes target-dependent modifiers, so a spell's camp damage is `raw * camp_dealt_factor()`. The finishing test already used it, but each member's worth (`min(raw, health)`), the ground and landing sums, and the overkill retarget did not. With a Monster emblem, which shop.cc buys, casts were undervalued by up to 15%. In `attackers_only` mode, a cast that would kill an attacker whose health lay in `(raw, raw*1.15]` was refused, because its value `raw` fell under the floor (that attacker's health). Now one `dealt` factor applies throughout. The bench games had no emblem at a threshold, so replays are unchanged. It is unverified by A/B and only matters with those emblems.

## Considered, not changed
- **Free-stun premise with two enemy turns** (l.313-316). An enemy with `turns_before_ours == 2` still gets a turn before ours after one stun, so "keeping the walk gains nothing" isn't strictly true. But the stun costs it a whole turn (walk and hit) while our walk gains one tile, so the stun is plausibly still at least as good. That makes it a judgement call, left alone (see Suggestions).
- **Unconditional stale check in `cast_at_enemies`** (l.571: `if (lethal_landings_only || attempted) world.throw_if_stale()`). The condition looks accidental, but making it unconditional changes behaviour only on stale turns. Left alone.
- **Moving `hit_worth` to a shared header with plan.cc's twin**: that's cross-file, so it's a suggestion.
- `cast_cost` / `min_landing_value` are tuned constants. I didn't touch them.

## Suggestions
1. **Escape stun certification is stricter than the rules** (l.354, `controls = e.acted && ...`). With `!e.acted && turns_before_ours(world, e) == 1`, the enemy's only turn before ours is later this round, and a stun removes exactly that turn (rules: abilities.stun), so it's controlled just as well. Dropping `e.acted` would let Prism's escape certify more stuns. I double-checked against `turns_before_ours`: for a visible enemy, `now = !acted`, plus next-round-before-us. Severity: low-medium (Prism escapes). Effort: trivial plus an A/B.
2. **Heart of Ruin's enemy-hitting landings ignore two costs that `camp_cast` prices** (l.377). (a) A landing off a cleared group we're capturing abandons the capture: `camp_cast` skips it, `enemy_cast` doesn't. (b) Landing inside an awake monster's reach: `landing_wakes` only looks at sleeping groups, while `landing_threats` (now a reusable helper) lists awake monsters that would newly reach a landing. `min_landing_value` = 250 partly covers both. Severity: medium for HoR, which is drafted often. Effort: small (reuse `landing_threats` in the keep, plus the capture check), then A/B.
3. **Unseen-target damage estimate** (l.277: `min(raw * e.taken, health)`). It leaves out our Skirmish/Collectors `champion_dealt_factor()` and the enemy's shield (`e.shield`, Varg/HoR) when last seen. The native-failure fallback `damage_to` (l.68: `min(raw, health)`) has neither mitigation nor dealt factor either. Exact versions are cheap: `raw * dealt * e.taken`, less the shield. Severity: low. Effort: small.
4. **Free-stun only counts visible reachers** (l.305-316). A tracked unseen enemy that could reach us next turn is ignored, so a stun on the visible one is taken as making the walk worthless while an unseen threat remains. `Safety` already has unseen threat areas. Consider counting any unseen threat whose area covers `here` as `unstoppable`. Severity: low-medium. Effort: small, needs an A/B.
5. **`hit_worth` exists twice** (here l.79 and `plan.cc:117`, with different signatures and the same maths, but plan.cc's lacks the recall term). Share one in a header so the cast menu and plan search can't drift apart. Severity: maintenance. Effort: small, cross-file.
6. **`stun_worth` on unseen enemies reads `e.channel`** (tribute/recall/teleport terms, l.90-101) for area targets. `recall_kept`/`teleport_pending` require `visible`, but the tribute term doesn't, so a tribute seen long ago could keep adding `tribute_value + gems` to an unseen target. Check how world.cc maintains `channel` out of sight (it says "hidden completion may clear channel"). If it can stay stale, gate the tribute term on `e.visible` or on channel age. Severity: low. Effort: trivial once checked.
7. **Camp stun value** (l.461-467) is the monster's raw Strength. It could be scaled by our `camp_hit_factor()` (Jungle/Durability) when we are the nearest target, and it counts a hit that would land on an ally the same as one on us. Minor. Effort: trivial.
8. **Spawn healing under a stun** (`hit_worth`, l.82: non-kill damage to an enemy on its spawn is worth 0 because it heals at its turn end). If a stunned champion's turn end still runs (`rules: turn_end` vs `stun` "removes the target's next turn"), that's right; if a stun skips the turn end too, damage plus a stun on a spawn-camper keeps value. Worth a probe if spawn fights matter. Severity: low.
9. **Fuel**: no fuel gates in these files. `max_unseen_tiles` is a bound on aiming work, not a gate. Remaining cost centres are `damage_to` (one host call per visible enemy per slot) and `wake_risk`/`dash_landings_from` (several bitboard calls). The `camp_dealt_factor()` read (items host call) now runs once per non-empty `camp_cast`. It could be cached per turn by whoever owns util/camps.
10. **`cast_at_enemies` stale check** (l.571): either always re-check after `enemy_cast` (it's cheap) or never. The current `lethal_landings_only || attempted` condition reads as an accident.
