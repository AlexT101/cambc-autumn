## What lost or is held, and why

- **Gate removals** (held): no-gates-1 49.5%, out-of-fuel turns 23319 vs 6776; no-gates-1b 50.6%, 4.2x; no-gates-1c
  48.5%, 5.1x. All byte-identical at 100M (-0.7%). 1c's culprit is pick_up's farm-loop gate (restored alone: 1788 ->
  96 on the two worst maps), the ward gate a distant second. guard-fold (step_guard on the one fight model, gates as
  limits): real-budget out-of-fuel turns 861 -> 2931. The turn-order gate removed: +13%.
- **Farm loop:** farm-lean (bound across turns) +0.5%, the skipped visits were cheap prunes; farm-ungate, a limit of
  12 fresh estimates binds 0.46% but 2193 vs 416 out-of-fuel turns on 6 heavy maps, since the gate trims the loop on
  most turns there; farm-bestfirst +3.8%, touching a group (~210-235) is the cost.
- **Exact cuts that cost more than they save:** fight-bound (+0.05%; the sims it settles were the cheap ones),
  strike-reach-eq (+0.19%; a Reach build's ~1k fixed work is the cost), reach-build (+0.27 to +0.48%), world-lean
  (+2.5%; a host test is ~15 fuel), best-first search frontier (~500 a node, 48.7%).
- **No cut found:** guard-exact (repeated keys 2%, no collisions), prey-lean (a bound key first is not exact and
  moves its builds later in the turn; the fight sim is not monotone, so memo settling is unsafe), sim-setup (already
  cached; 44% of sim fuel is shield kits' act()), reach-at (-0.04%, relies on unprobed shadow_cast behaviour),
  strike-read (-0.02%).
- **Codegen:** the ladder builds src/ with its own flags, so none can ship; locally -flto was the best (-1.6%). The
  cost is wasm's stack-machine instruction count, not missed inlining.

## Lessons

- Out-of-fuel turns come from ~50k of fixed fight work before the strike search (world, want_home, step_guard) plus
  the search itself; replays of a lost turn run out again in the same step.
- The remaining gates are the turn's last brakes. They can't go until the farm loop on many-group maps and the fight
  work are cheaper: every removal so far was level at 100M and 4-5x worse in out-of-fuel turns.
- Touching data costs more than arithmetic: a group, a Reach build or a candidate costs its loads, versions and
  fixed set-up, so cuts that still touch everything save nothing.
- Exact cuts are mostly exhausted; the rest of the fuel needs restructuring (spatial buckets, one danger model,
  cheaper sims).
