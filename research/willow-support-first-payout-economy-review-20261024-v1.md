## What one share actually buys

Engine-verified `cambc-rules.yaml:85–107,155–158,184–187` supplies the arithmetic: capture splits base rewards, Vesper adds 33% gold, Treasure adds another 20%, shards cost 30, five identical shards form a 150-gold emblem. Gold is fractional although the getter floors it.

Two holders of g19 would receive base30 each: Willow30 and pre-Treasure Vesper39.9, versus Vesper79.8 alone. Willow gains one shard; Vesper forgoes39.9 growth gold and the team loses9.9 bonus gold. With Vesper Treasure the corresponding loss is45.9 to Vesper and15.9 total team gold. Gems become two each; tribute/deadline effects must still be counted. These are conditional payout arithmetic, not a replay counterfactual.

- **Focus shard:** Willow120→125 Focus, heal cap360→375, at most15 more HP per same selected cast. Cooldown remains eight rounds and range four. Five Focus shards yield cap435; a completed Power Focus emblem yields450 because it gives six shards' stats.
- **Health shard:** 700→750 maxHP; no direct heal increase. At spawn it also restores the purchased50 HP. At the existing35% recall line, usable health455→487.5; under the fixed-upkeep model the healing-trip cost term falls by1/15. Actual survival, camp aggro and earlier low-health triggers are not fixed-upkeep constants.
- **Strength alternative:** 55→58 basic damage, also benefiting reset basics. Forgoing it costs real camp output; raw extra healing cannot be substituted for farm DPS.

`self_abilities.cpp:419–497` requires positive gain and either gain≥60% of heal cap or recipient health fraction<50%. One Focus shard raises that gate216→225. The observed r135 heal is a useful counterexample: Vesper ends134 at800/1000, takes the next camp hit, then receives220 and ends135 full. At the same snapshot a375-cap ordinary heal would reject that220 deficit. The proposed payout occurs later, so this is a threshold example, not a claim that funding at146 changes r135. The 12 saturated casts establish demand for healing, not the additional deficits or prevented recalls needed to value extra Focus.

A30-gold share does not instantly buy anything in the field. Shopping requires spawn/death. `wants_shop` (`bot.cpp:3385–3408`) normally refuses a non-Prism's new dedicated shop trip below150 gold without an already begun emblem. A first shard therefore needs an otherwise useful return, or its extra trip must be paid for. `best_emblem:1244–1311` selects Treasure first in the model's chosen stat; the current model does not automatically choose Focus from receiving money.

## Source omission and smallest useful follow-up

`main_stat:118`, `raw_output:848`, `upkeep_cost:1209`, and `prices:1344` value Willow's own output and own healing-trip savings. They do not credit the allied recovery service bought by her Focus. Farm gold value at1960–1961 inherits that omission. Separately, `holders:1459`, `holding_capture:1495`, and `capture_taken:3494` avoid redundant holders; `local_support:4778` explicitly excludes stepping onto a cleared camp tile. These are efficient default rules, but none compares the marginal funding value of a nearby support against the incumbent's foregone growth. The support availability/claim signals identify readiness or work, not economic value. Allies cannot read Willow's private gold (`cambc-rules.yaml:115`).

Nominate **Willow8 r139–146 in this exact source-known replay**, only if this question is selected for further work: did she know g19 was cleared and Vesper committed, and could she safely occupy the other member tile without losing valuable support/work or payout progress? A bounded branch/own-view inspection should distinguish capture_taken, formation, another farm goal, and retreat; ordinary recall timer4 alone proves none of them. No broad scan is needed.

A first isolated experiment, if that local opportunity is admissible, should price an optional Willow-owned shared payout against her ordinary alternative, using her own gold, actual affordable prefix, next realistic shop opportunity, and signed observed recipient-recovery savings. Charge the incumbent's lost share/bonus and extra holder time; preserve urgent capture, healing, safety and winning-tribute decisions. Do not turn heal readiness or no items into automatic entitlement. Existing service-shopper work offers the recovery-round unit, but its panel showed no Focus purchase and does not establish profitable funding; see `willow-cb61-service-shopper-design-20261024-v2.md` and `willow-service-panel-activation-review-20261024-v1.md`.

Recommendation: investigate this one shared-clear opportunity before implementing redistribution. The missing measurements are the admissible local join/next shop and the *marginal* recipient recovery time saved versus Vesper's delayed upgrade. Existing 6,186 healed HP and own-upkeep counters cannot supply those. This supports a narrow support-funding hypothesis, not a blanket Vesper income cap, Treasure exception, or forced Focus purchase.
